Learn how to apply a label to a segment of your users.

# Overview

Labels are a way to tag people or segments of people for the following purposes:

* Easy sorting and organization
* At-a-glance identification
* Delivering customized content for communications, sites, and pages

[Segments](https://help.element451.com/people/segments/what-are-segments) are groups of people with similar characteristics (location, for example) and/or who have done similar things via an Element451 module, e.g., started an application.

---

# Apply a Label to a Segment

You can apply a label to every person in a segment with just a few steps:

1. Navigate to your segments from **Contacts > Segments**.
2. Find the segment you want to apply the label to and click the **three vertical dots**.
3. Click **Add Labels**.
4. Search and locate the label(s) you wish to add to the segment. Select as many as you want to apply to the segment.
5. Click **Submit**

# Remove a Label from a Segment

1. Navigate to your segments from **Contacts > Segments**.
2. Find the segment you want to apply the label to and click the **three vertical dots**.
3. Click **Remove Labels**.
4. Search and locate the label(s) you wish to remove from the segment.
5. Click **Submit**

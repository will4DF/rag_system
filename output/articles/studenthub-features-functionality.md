# Overview

As our article [Getting Started with StudentHub](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9827408-getting-started-with-student-portals) outlines**,** StudentHub is your students’ AI-powered, mobile-first companion designed for their engagement and success. Seamlessly integrated with Element451, it centralizes everything students need—from appointments and tasks to events and support networks—into one unified experience.

This article will walk you through the key features of StudentHub, detailing what students can explore and access.

---

## Explore

The **Explore** section is the student’s personalized home page, offering a central hub for all their essential information. Upon logging in, students are greeted with a customized message.

This page is designed to provide quick, easy access to key resources and upcoming activities, making it effortless for students to stay on top of their commitments.

Here is what can be found on the Explore page (for a deeper dive into each module that StudentHub integrates with, check out the [last section](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9854238-studenthub-features#h_473f6fda6a) of this article):

* **Bolt Discovery**: Students can find information about your institution using Bolt Discovery, a powerful generative search tool. Prompt starters are also supported here to guide students in their queries.

* **Upcoming Appointments**: This section displays future appointments booked through the Element451 Appointments module. Students can tap on each appointment to view details or to edit or cancel the appointment.

* **Registered Events**: Students can see their upcoming registered events through the Element451 Events module. They can tap an event registration to view event details, make edits, or cancel their registration if needed. Event self-check-in is also integrated into StudentHub.

* **Incomplete Tasks**: In this section, students can view tasks assigned by an internal user. These tasks have not yet been completed, and students can tap on each task to view more details or mark it complete (if permissions allow).

The **Notifications** section of StudentHub keeps students updated on important announcements, reminders, and calls to action. Integrated with the Element451 Campaigns module, it allows you to incorporate push notifications seamlessly into your campaigns—alongside SMS and email messaging.

When students open **Notifications**, they’ll see two tabs:

* **Unread:** Displays any new notifications that haven’t been viewed. Once tapped, the notification is marked read and moves to the **All** tab.
* **All:** Houses all notifications, both read and unread, for easy reference.

# Integrated Element451 Modules + Data

StudentHub is a direct extension of Element451’s powerful features, bringing essential resources like Bolt AI tools directly to your students, all in one convenient place. Instead of scattered URLs or multiple emails, StudentHub centralizes everything right in the palm of your students' hands.

Below, we explore the modules that sync with Element451, detailing the functionality within StudentHub Resources:

## Bolt AI

Bolt AI, Element451’s suite of AI-powered student tools, is embedded directly within StudentHub.

* The Explore page features **[Bolt Discovery](https://help.element451.com/en/collections/9444230-bolt-discovery)** for instant, generative search—giving students direct access to accurate answers from your institution’s knowledge base without the hassle of traditional search.
* Additionally, students can connect with **[Bolt Agents](https://help.element451.com/en/collections/6162096-bolt-assistants-for-students)** through the **Messenger** widget, offering around-the-clock support right in the hub.

## Applications

The **Applications** integration brings a student’s application history directly into StudentHub. Under **Resources → Applications**, students can view all their applications along with key details such as **major**, **term**, and **status**.

For admitted students, related **[Decision Package](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9236362-decisions-packages) items**—like acceptance letters and financial awards—appear automatically once activated by staff. These items display as collapsible sections under the corresponding application, giving students easy access to their important documents and updates in one place.

## Appointments

Powered by the Element451 [Appointments module](https://help.element451.com/en/collections/4066448-appointments), StudentHub displays your team's availability, empowering students to schedule, view, and cancel appointments—all in one place.

### Available

* Displays a list of internal users with availabilities (job title and name), just like your [Appointments Site](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8141376-getting-started-with-appointments-for-admins#h_74ca2b79a2), and provides access to their booking page. They can schedule an appointment directly within StudentHub without leaving the platform. We cover appointment scheduling + details [further down](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9854238-studenthub-features#h_0545eadeb0).
* Students can browse the complete list of availabilities using search and filter options to find exactly who/what they need.

### My Appointments

* Displays all **upcoming** and **past** appointments that the student has scheduled.
* Students can tap on any appointment to view its details ([explained below](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9854238-studenthub-features#h_0545eadeb0)).

### Appointment Scheduled Details

Immediately after scheduling an appointment or opening the scheduled appointment later, you will open the Appointment Scheduled Details page. Here, the student can:

* **Cancel Appointment:** Option to cancel the appointment.
* **Add to Calendar**: Opens the device's default calendar app and prompts the student to add the appointment to their calendar.

## Campaigns

With StudentHub, push notifications become a part of your campaign strategy, reaching students instantly through a dedicated **Notifications** menu item. For a deeper dive into setting up and using push notifications, see our **[StudentHub: Push Notifications](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9888911-studenthub-push-notifications)** article.

## Conversations

The **Chat** menu item in StudentHub connects seamlessly with Element451’s [Messenger](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6249763-messenger-live-chat) channel. Students can easily engage with live support or a Bolt Agent, managed through the Conversations module.

## Events

Seamlessly integrates with the Element451 [Events module](https://help.element451.com/en/collections/124586-events) to bring your event calendar into StudentHub. Students can easily browse, register, manage their registrations, and even self-check-in (if enabled).

### Upcoming

* The "upcoming" tab lists forthcoming events with open registration, just like your [Events Site](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8981854-event-settings-your-event-site).
* Students can browse the complete event list using search and filter options to find exactly what they need.
* Plus, they can register for any event directly within StudentHub without leaving the platform. We cover [event registration + details](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9854238-studenthub-features#h_626ec1027a) further down.

### My Events

* Displays all **upcoming** and **past** events that the student has registered for.
* Students can simply tap on any event to view its details ([explained below](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9854238-studenthub-features#h_626ec1027a)).

## Event Details

### Event Self-Check-In

## Courses

Under the **Resources** tab in StudentHub, students will find an **Academics** card that gives them quick access to their **future**, **current**, and **past courses** based on enrollment history. It also includes a searchable **Course Catalog**, allowing students to explore available offerings.

Each course listing—whether tied to a student’s enrollment or found in the catalog—displays key academic details such as:

* Course code, title/topic, credits, grading basis, enrollment status, course type, department, and description
* Associated sections with details like instructor, meeting dates, section status, campus, school, term, delivery format, and section type

This is view-only information designed for easy reference, helping students stay informed without needing to log in elsewhere. Course data is pulled from Element451 via [course data](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10420398-getting-started-with-courses) and [LMS integrations](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/11589065-native-courses-integrations-for-learning-management-systems-lms), making StudentHub an even more centralized, high-value tool for your students.

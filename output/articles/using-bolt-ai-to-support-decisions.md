# Overview

Bolt AI brings intelligent automation to your admissions process—giving you fast, consistent insights that help you make better decisions, faster. With specialized agents designed for evaluation, fraud detection, and transcript analysis, Bolt AI supports your team during critical moments of the review process.

To read more about Bolt AI, [click here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/11061870-getting-started-with-bolt-ai).

# How Bolt AI Helps with Decisions

Bolt AI agents work in the background to:

* Provide quick, consistent evaluations of submitted applications.
* Analyze transcripts and extract meaningful academic insights.
* Flag unusual or potentially fraudulent submissions.
* Reduce manual review time so you can focus on deeper evaluation.
* Promote fairness and consistency across readers and reviewers.

Currently, three agents are available to assist with decision-making:

## Bolt App Reader Agent

Provides a first-pass review of submitted applications, scoring them based on your admissions criteria. The goal? To speed up evaluation and help ensure consistency across your team.

[Explore More: Bolt App Reader Agent →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10697700-beta-bolt-app-reader-agent)

## Bolt Fraud Detector Agent

Uses AI to flag potentially fraudulent applications by analyzing signals such as submission timing, email patterns, and IP behavior. It’s a safeguard to help your team maintain integrity and trust in the admissions process.

[Explore More: Bolt Fraud Detector Agent →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9927313-preview-bolt-app-fraud-detector-agent)

## Bolt Transcript Evaluation Agent

Automatically reads and processes uploaded high school and college transcripts. The agent extracts structured academic data, summarizes key insights, and surfaces results directly in the student’s profile—while linking back to the original document for transparency.

[Explore More: Bolt Transcript Evaluation Agent →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/12639660-bolt-transcript-evaluation-agent)

Learn how to configure Bolt Discovery Settings, including adding and managing thread starters and lead capture forms.

# Overview

The Bolt Discovery Settings page in Element451 is where you configure the various options that make Bolt Discovery a powerful tool for guiding students to the information they need. This page allows you to manage preloaded knowledge sources, customize thread starters, and configure the lead capture forms used by Bolt Discovery.

In this article, we’ll walk you through each configuration available on the Bolt Discovery Settings page, helping you make the most of this feature.

## Accessing Bolt Discovery Settings

Navigate to **Engagement** > **Bolt** **Discovery** > **Settings**.

## How To: Add and Manage Thread Starters

In Bolt Discovery Settings, you can add and manage the thread starters that display on the **embedded** and **URL** methods.

🚨 **Important:** Starters on Element451 **Pages** are added and managed **directly within the Content Block editor**. This process is outlined above in the [Integrating Bolt Discovery](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9397459-deploying-bolt-discovery#h_c9b5a8df6c) article.

To manage starters for Bolt Discovery when embedded and shared via URL, follow these steps:

### Adding Starters

1. Navigate to **Engagement** > **Bolt** **Discovery** > **Settings**.
2. In the **Starters** section, click the **+ Add Starter** button.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1065933468/7aa72e0f3f9e1cdbb58c8f5e/Bolt+Discover+_+Settings+_+Starters+%281%29.png?expires=1784333700&signature=3f2374c15819004728850b09ed0ca5a3406d5094f6e5c087b6f6682315985a53&req=dSAhE8B9noVZUfMW1HO4zRki33S%2Bf7QQwj2W%2FXYeMA2scvo0UZKV5B5PF%2FWt%0ARQ8F%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1065933468/7aa72e0f3f9e1cdbb58c8f5e/Bolt+Discover+_+Settings+_+Starters+%281%29.png?expires=1784333700&signature=3f2374c15819004728850b09ed0ca5a3406d5094f6e5c087b6f6682315985a53&req=dSAhE8B9noVZUfMW1HO4zRki33S%2Bf7QQwj2W%2FXYeMA2scvo0UZKV5B5PF%2FWt%0ARQ8F%0A)
3. Configure the starter settings:

   * **Title:** What appears under the search bar and prompts the student.

     + Example: Programs Offered.
   * **Message:** The question sent to Bolt Discovery after the starter is clicked.

     + Example: What programs does Fire University offer?
   * **Conditions** (optional): Tailor the starters to specific user contexts using Page URL, Path URL, or UTM Parameters conditions.

     + Example: Page URL Contains "poultryscience"

       [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1053208077/01d8f0dde1e29bf529567247/Bolt+Discovery+-+Settings+-+Starter+Config.png?expires=1784333700&signature=82a4fb41e91031bd9ad2051ff03ce82e54bb90e2edaaf68c75f82b5da8dc945c&req=dSAiFct%2BlYFYXvMW1HO4zQ9vCG5%2BbelpH86FAqJUS%2Bp343%2BqY3a5pHWO0cNU%0AjqvJ%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1053208077/01d8f0dde1e29bf529567247/Bolt+Discovery+-+Settings+-+Starter+Config.png?expires=1784333700&signature=82a4fb41e91031bd9ad2051ff03ce82e54bb90e2edaaf68c75f82b5da8dc945c&req=dSAiFct%2BlYFYXvMW1HO4zQ9vCG5%2BbelpH86FAqJUS%2Bp343%2BqY3a5pHWO0cNU%0AjqvJ%0A)

       ​
4. Click **Save**.

2. In the **Starters** section, locate the Starter you wish to relocate.
3. # Spaces

**Spaces** allow you to control the **knowledge Bolt Discovery can access** under specific conditions. This enables the creation of specialized “spaces” that restrict the agent to specific knowledge sources.

For example:

* A **Financial Aid** space that only pulls answers from financial aid–related knowledge.
* An **Admissions** space that only pulls answers from admissions requirements and application details.

When a user’s query matches the **conditions** you set for a space, Bolt Discovery will only use the knowledge sources defined in that space to generate answers.

## Adding a Space

1. Click **+ Add Space**.
2. Complete the configuration fields:

   * **Title** – Internal name for your space.
   * **Slug** – A short, unique identifier that limits embedded Discovery to this space. Examples: current-students, international-students.
   * **Enabled** – Toggle to turn the space on or off.
   * **Conditions** – Define when this space should be evaluated. Supported **Browser Conditions** include:

     + **Page URL**
     + **Path URL**
     + **UTM Parameters**
3. Define the knowledge this space can access by choosing one or more of the following:

   * **Add sources individually** with the **+ Add Sources** button.
   * **Select categories** from your Knowledge Hub.
   * **Define URL patterns** to include specific sources.
   * **Define Title patterns** to filter sources by title.
4. Click **Save** to create your space.

Your new space will now appear in the **Spaces list**. When a user’s query matches its conditions, Bolt Discovery will limit itself to only the sources defined in that space.

## Custom Form

🚨 **Important:** For partners on a legacy package, the ability to select a custom form is only available to those partners on the Ignite and Engage plans.

* You can bypass the default form by selecting which form you want Discovery to use. You will have the ability to select one of your existing **prospect**-**type** forms.
* You can also determine which forms are deployed where using URL, path URL, or UTM parameter conditions.

Note: If no custom form is specified in settings, the default form will apply.

## How To: Add and Manage Lead Capture Forms

1. Navigate to **Engagement** > **Bolt** **Discovery** > **Settings**.
2. In the **Lead Capture** section, click the **+ Lead Capture Form** button.
3. Configure the form settings:

   * **Title**: Give the form a name; this will be displayed to the student/user.
   * **Form** **to** **Show**: Select which form you wish to use.
   * **Conditions**: Optional, but you can add conditions to fine-tune the audience that sees this form.

## What Happens After Submission?

Once the form is submitted, it functions like any other prospect form submission:

1. The system checks if the contact already exists in the database:

   * If the contact exists, the record is updated with any new information.
   * If the contact does not exist, a new contact record is created.
2. The system assigns a prospect milestone to the contact (new or existing).

   * This milestone includes a hyperlink to the conversation thread, providing context for future interactions.

For more details, refer to the [Understanding Form Submissions](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9184596-understanding-form-submissions) article.

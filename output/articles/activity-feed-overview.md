The Element451 activity feed tells a story of the student engagement through the admissions funnel.

# Overview

You can gain insights into student engagement with your institution through Element451's Activity Feed. This feature chronicles students' engagement, presenting a chronological narrative encapsulating their journey.

📙 ***Note****: You must have the Activity Feed card displayed on your profile template.*

*[Learn more about Bolt Profile Templates](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1475735-the-person-profile).*

## Activity Tracked

The activity feed includes the following activities by an external user:

* Creation of the user record
* Communications sent
* Communications received
* Communications opened/clicked (with details of date and time)
* When they logged into their account
* Links clicked
* Registration for Events
* Cancellation of Events
* Attendance of Events
* Page views
* Tasks/changes completed by staff members

# Accessing + Viewing Tracked Activity

1. Navigate to **Contacts** > **People** or use the search bar in the top right corner to search the student's name.
2. Click on their name to open the profile.
3. Look for the **Activity** card on the user profile. You can also search for it using the search bar.
4. To expand and view all activity, open the Bolt Sheet by clicking the **arrow** next to Activity or clicking the **More** button at the bottom.

# Email Content Viewer

📙 **Note**: By default, only Element451 administrators have access to view Email content. Contact your administrator to request one of the following *Records* permissions:

* *See Sent Email/SMS Content*
* *See Sent Email/SMS Content - Sensitive Info Redacted*

For most users, we recommend assigning the *See Sent Email/SMS Content - Sensitive Info Redacted permission.*

Within the Activity Feed, you can view emails exactly as they were rendered when sent, inclusive of tokens and dynamic content. This ensures that you see the delivered email as the Contact saw it. Emails tracked by the Activity Feed include:

* One-Time Campaigns
* Ongoing Campaigns
* Appointments: Autoresponders
* Applications: Autoresponders
* Events: Notifications
* Events: Custom Messages
* Password Reset: Autoresponders

Email content is viewable on the following email activities: ***delivered***, ***bounced***, ***opened***, and ***clicked*** ***link** **in** **email**.* However, the 'View Content' button is only available for messages sent via Workflow campaigns and one-time communications. It is not available for messages sent through Conversations or Bolt Jobs.

📙 **Note**: Access to email history in the content viewer is limited to emails sent on or after February 5, 2024.

# Adding Custom Activities

As mentioned above, Element451 automatically tracks a lot of activity, but there may be situations where you might want to monitor additional activities. You have the ability to add these custom activities to a student's profile.

[Learn More: Adding Custom Activities](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/3097376-activity-feed-adding-custom-activities)

# Tracking Activity Feed Changes by Internal Users

The activity feed includes tracked changes made by internal users. As a result, if needed, leadership/admins can quickly investigate the changes in a student's record.

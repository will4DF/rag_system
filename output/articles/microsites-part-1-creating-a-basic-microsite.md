# Creating a New Microsite

1. Navigate to **Engagement** > **Microsites** > **All** **Microsites**
2. Click the blue **+** button in the top right corner  
   ​

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879362332/3bdb59643dbc879ef43ce9fe/Accessing+Microsites.gif?expires=1784333700&signature=4869f36eb242a5ae0d0813337a9a658c829f31fc0185c7fab5ced48c6cf564c8&req=fCcuFc98noJdFb4f3HP0gGkBF55gbDfwVuhxLM8OKcoBprk3ACtZi5idZ1M2%0AWe8%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879362332/3bdb59643dbc879ef43ce9fe/Accessing+Microsites.gif?expires=1784333700&signature=4869f36eb242a5ae0d0813337a9a658c829f31fc0185c7fab5ced48c6cf564c8&req=fCcuFc98noJdFb4f3HP0gGkBF55gbDfwVuhxLM8OKcoBprk3ACtZi5idZ1M2%0AWe8%3D%0A)
3. Name the site and choose a general look. You can change your choices later.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879364489/da8192600270ae58cfdb07f0/Microsites+-+New+Site+Configurations.png?expires=1784333700&signature=c380c31d44021fea113846b2f0f4813e415a196a6ac3d1c422d90c68ee36f4b9&req=fCcuFc96mYlWFb4f3HP0gKALmdaHkb7eYyMY%2Fwfwad6XRBPpAzhLmDULc3sR%0AOA8%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879364489/da8192600270ae58cfdb07f0/Microsites+-+New+Site+Configurations.png?expires=1784333700&signature=c380c31d44021fea113846b2f0f4813e415a196a6ac3d1c422d90c68ee36f4b9&req=fCcuFc96mYlWFb4f3HP0gKALmdaHkb7eYyMY%2Fwfwad6XRBPpAzhLmDULc3sR%0AOA8%3D%0A)

   * **General:** Name the site (ex. Admitted Student Microsite)
   * **Theme:** Choose either a light or dark theme
   * **Design:**

     + Cards Style: Select one: soft rounded, rounded, or squared cards
     + Buttons Style: Select one: soft rounded, rounded, or squared buttons
4. Click **Next** to save your changes and create your new Microsite
5. Now that the site has been created, Part 1 of the process is complete, and it's time to configure your design, layout, and settings.

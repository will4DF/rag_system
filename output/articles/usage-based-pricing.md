Most usage in Element451 is unlimited; a few features are usage-based and billed one of two ways depending on your plan.

# Overview

Most of Element451 is unlimited as part of your contract — sending email, building segments, and adding contacts and users, to name a few. A handful of features are **usage-based**, such as messaging, phone calling, and AI-powered evaluations.

How those usage-based features are billed depends on your plan. Every plan uses one of two billing modes — **Usage Caps** or **Usage Credits**. This article explains the difference and links to the details for yours.

---

# The Two Billing Modes

* **[Usage Caps](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/15934464-understanding-usage-caps)** — each usage-based feature has an amount included in your contract, measured in natural units like messages, minutes, and evaluations. Caps are soft limits; if you need more, your account manager adds capacity.
* **[Usage Credits](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10421758-usage-based-billing-credits)** — usage-based features draw from a single credit balance, and you can purchase more credits at any time.

**Which one do you have?** Go to **Settings > General Settings**. If you see a **credit balance** and an **Add Credits** option, you're on Usage Credits. If your usage shows as caps in natural units, you're on Usage Caps.

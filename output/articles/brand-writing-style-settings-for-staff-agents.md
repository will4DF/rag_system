Define your brand's voice, tone, structure, etc, for content generated by Staff Agents.

# Overview

Element451's Brand Writing Style settings help content generated by Staff Agents follow your institution's voice and communication objectives. Customize tone, voice, vocabulary, formatting, and structure to align generated content with your brand.

Your Brand Writing Style is applied when Staff Agents **write content** or **generate** **Campaigns**. This article covers how to harness these settings to their full potential.

📌 **Note**: Writing style settings only affect Staff Agents when they create content for you. They do not apply when Bolt Agents communicate with students in Conversations (including Jobs). For Bolt Agents, you can configure their individual "Response Style" [setting](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/11426404-onboarding-managing-bolt-agents-for-students), which controls how they reply in conversations.

---

## Accessing Brand Writing Style Settings

To begin customizing your brand’s writing style, navigate to the Brand Writing Style settings within your Element451 General Settings. This section is designed to be intuitive, allowing you to start tailoring your content quickly.

From your profile picture/avatar in the top right corner, navigate to **Settings** > **General** > **Branding.**

# Settings

Here's a breakdown of what each setting entails and how they impact your content:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Overall Writing Style** | This setting lets you define the general approach your content should follow, ensuring consistency across all communications. |
| **Tone** | Choose from tones such as friendly, authoritative, playful, serious, professional, enthusiastic, or helpful. Your choice should reflect how you want your brand perceived when communicating with your audience. |
| **Voice** | Similar to tone, but focusing more on the personality projected through your writing. Options include friendly, authoritative, playful, serious, professional, enthusiastic, and helpful. |
| **Point of View** | Select from first person (I, we), second person (you, your), or third person (he, she, they) to align with how you address your audience. |
| **Writing** **Intent** | Define your primary goal with each piece of content: inform, persuade, entertain, or motivate. This will help you craft messages that serve a specific purpose. |
| **Language & Vocabulary** | Adjust the complexity of your language from formal to informal, and choose the level of complexity and jargon that best suits your audience. |
| **Formatting & Structure** | Customize elements like sentence structure, paragraph length, and the use of bullet points to enhance readability and engagement. |

# Editing Your Brand Writing Style Settings

## Manual Adjustment

You can directly type in your preferences for each setting. This manual approach gives you complete control over your brand’s communication style nuances.

1. From your profile picture/avatar in the top right corner, navigate to **Settings** > **General** > **Branding.**
2. Scroll down to the **Brand Writing Style** section.
3. Click the **pencil** **icon** in the top right corner of the section.  
   ​

4. Click on any of the style settings to type your preferences.
5. Click Save in the top right corner.

## Infer from Example

You can use the **Infer from Example** button for a more automated approach. Paste a text sample that embodies your brand’s style, and the system will analyze it and suggest settings that match the example provided. This is particularly useful for maintaining consistency with your existing content.

1. From your profile picture/avatar in the top right corner, navigate to **Settings** > **General** > **Branding.**
2. Scroll down to the **Brand Writing Style** section.
3. Click the **pencil** **icon** in the top right corner of the section.

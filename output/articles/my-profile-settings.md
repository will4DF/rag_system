Learn how to configure your personal account settings, including your preferred start page, change your password, and mute the chat sound.

# Overview

Internal Element451 users can configure their personal information in Account Settings. On this page, you can:

* Update your personal information (first name, last name, email, title)
* Change your [Primary Team](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8346250-teams#h_db9a8cc19e)
* Set or change your Preferred Start Page
* Add a Cell Phone number for use with optional Phone MFA
* Update your Office Building, Room and Phone Number
* Change Password
* Mute Chat Sound

---

# Accessing My Profile Settings

To view or update your personal information:

1. Click on your **avatar/picture** in the top right corner of the orange navigation bar.
2. Select **Manage Account.**

# Phone Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Phone MFA allows you to receive login verification codes via text message (SMS) as a backup to email. This helps ensure you can still access your account if email delivery is delayed or unavailable.

**Note:** Phone MFA must be [enabled by your Element451 administrator](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8569773-security-authentication-settings#h_75d1d8e2f5) before you can use this feature.

To set up Phone MFA:

1. Click your **profile avatar** in the top-right corner and select **Manage Account**
2. Select **Edit Profile**
3. Enter your **cell phone number** in the *Cell Phone* field
4. Click **Save**
5. Once saved, wait for the system to recognize your number as SMS-enabled. If you don’t see the SMS indicator after ~30 seconds, refresh the page
6. Click **Verify** next to your phone number
7. Enter the verification code sent to your phone

Once verified, you’ll be able to choose text message as an alternative method to receive your login verification code.

**Remember,** while your Office phone is listed in StudentHub,your cell phone number is used only for MFA and is not visible to students.

# Mute Chat Sound (Conversation Notifications)

Our notification system ensures you are always alerted to new inbound conversation messages. Notification sounds are triggered when an inbound message arrives in the following scenarios:

* You are a **participant** in that conversation.
* You are an **assignee** in that conversation.
* The conversation is marked as a **favorite**.

## To disable the sound notification:

1. Click on your **avatar/picture** in the top right corner of the orange navigation bar.
2. Select **Manage Account**.

## To enable the sound notification:

1. To unmute, repeat the same steps outlined above. The only difference is that the button will read "**Unmute** Chat Sound."

   * Click on your **avatar/profile picture** in the top right corner of the orange navigation bar.
   * Select **Manage Account**.
   * Click on the **person/gear silhouette icon** in the upper right corner.
2. Click **Unmute Chat Sound.**

The Sophomore / Junior Search Campaign Pack is a communications campaign targeted at high school sophomore and junior students. Message segmentation will vary depending on if the recipient is a junior, sophomore, in-state, or out-of-state.

This Pack can be used by any type of institution looking to reach high school students at the beginning of their college search. You may also be interested in our [Sophomore / Junior Search Campaign by Funnel Stage](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6922626-sophomore-junior-search-campaign-by-funnel-stage).

## Pack Install Options

* **Intended Terms:** Select the intended enrollment term(s) for both sophomores and juniors.
* **Landing Page URL:** Specify the destination link for all CTA buttons in the campaign. E.g., a link to sign up for a visit day.
* **Audience Segment:** Specifies who will receive these messages.
* **Configure Communications**

  + **Sender email address and name**: configures the reply-to email address and name of the sender in messages that are sent.
  + **SMS phone number and sender name**: similarly configures the phone number and name referenced in text messages that go out. *(e.g., "This is Chris from Element University...")*
  + **Contact info**: Populates the footer fields in the associated emails.
* **Communications frequency:** sets the delay between messages (weekly by default).

* [user:first\_name, fallback="there"]

  + Displays the user's first name with a fallback option in the event it isn't in the system (unlikely).
* [client:name]

  + Displays your institution's name as specified in your Pack tokens.

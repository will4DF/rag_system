# Overview

StudentHub is your students' **AI-powered**, **mobile-first companion**, with engagement and success at its core. Seamlessly integrated with Element451, it centralizes all they need—appointments, tasks, events, and their support network—into one unified experience.

With Bolt Agents and Bolt Discovery right in their pocket, students get instant, personalized information and assistance anytime and anywhere. StudentHub empowers students to navigate their academic journey confidently, putting the full power of your institution's support directly into their hands.

# Key Features + How it Works

StudentHub leverages your existing Element451 data and configurations to create a tailored, unified experience for each student. Dive into what makes StudentHub a must-have resource in the pockets of your students 👇

## AI-Powered, 24/7 Support

* **[Bolt Agents](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/7173429-bolt-assistants-for-students-overview)**: Provide around-the-clock, personalized support to help students with academic guidance, tasks, and general inquiries. These AI-driven agents are available anytime, ensuring students can get help when needed.
* **[Bolt Discovery](https://help.element451.com/en/collections/9444230-bolt-discovery)**: An intuitive AI search system that helps students navigate the platform efficiently. It guides them to relevant information, making it easy to find resources, answer questions, and complete tasks.

## Mobile-First Web Application

* Accessible through any web browser; no download required.
* Optimized for smartphones and tablets, with desktop compatibility.
* Fully branded to reflect your institution's identity.

## Element451 Integration

StudentHub simplifies the student journey by providing essential services in one place. It seamlessly integrates with existing Element451 modules, ensuring a smooth flow of data and functionality across your digital ecosystem. Events, Appointments, Tasks, and other data from your Element451 modules **automatically** populate the relevant sections in StudentHub, creating a dynamic and up-to-date experience.

* **Events**: Browse, register, and manage [event registrations](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1520520-getting-started-with-events) and use [self-check-in](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9650448-event-self-check-in)
* **Applications**: View applications, their term, status, and major. For admitted students, [Decision Packages](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9236362-decisions-packages) (letters, financial aid, and other) items will appear here when activated for that contact's Decision.
* **Appointments**: Schedule, view, and manage [appointments](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10458679-getting-started-with-appointments)
* **Tasks**: Track to-do items using [Contact Tasks](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/3065709-getting-started-with-tasks#h_2eb1edd6af) assigned by you
* **My Network**: Access [connections](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9884014-network-connect-contacts-with-internal-users) and their contact information
* **Messenger**: Engage with [live chat](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6249763-messenger-live-chat) and [Bolt Agents](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/7173429-getting-started-with-bolt-agents)
* **Bolt Discovery**: Use [AI-powered search](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9331910-bolt-discovery-overview) to find concise, accurate answers
* **Courses**: View [course-related data](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10420398-getting-started-with-courses) such as schedule, sections, grades, and more!

## Proactive Student Support

* Data-driven interventions triggered by predefined conditions (e.g., grades, deadlines).
* Enables timely, targeted assistance to improve retention and success rates.

## Push Notifications

* Deliver real-time updates, reminders, and important information directly to students' devices.
* Keep students informed and engaged with campus and personal academic updates.

## Customizable Feature Set

* Tailor StudentHub to match your institution's specific needs and priorities with the flexibility to add or remove features for an ideal student experience.
* StudentHub uses your institution's branding configurations from general settings, ensuring a consistent look and feel.

# Best Practices

* Regularly review and update the content in your Element451 modules to ensure students have access to the most current information.
* Communicate the StudentHub URL and login process clearly to your students.
* Consider creating a brief guide or tutorial for students on how to navigate and use the StudentHub effectively.
* Periodically review which features you have enabled in the StudentHub to ensure they align with your current student engagement strategies.

# FAQ

### How can I create a segment for contacts who have accessed StudentHub?

You can easily segment contacts or students who have accessed StudentHub using filters. Here’s how:

1. Use the **Account (Logged In)** filter.
2. Use the **Site** property within the filter and select your **StudentHub** site.
3. You can refine your segment by applying the **At Least** or **At Most** operator. For example, to segment contacts who have logged into StudentHub **at least once**, set the operator to “at least” and input “1.”

Bolt Priority Index ranks contacts’ enrollment potential based on interactions and institution metrics.

# Overview

The **Bolt Priority Index** uses advanced machine learning to gauge a contact’s likelihood of progressing to the next major step in the enrollment funnel. This contact trait helps you identify and prioritize records with the highest probability of taking action, allowing your team to focus on the most promising prospects.

The Bolt Priority Index is displayed alongside other contact traits on the **Traits Profile Card** or by clicking **More Data** from the left-hand panel of a contact’s profile. Additionally, you can use the Priority Index as a filter when creating segments, enabling targeted communication and strategic outreach. Learn more about this in the [segments section](#h_c8730ad62e) below.

This includes historical records and newly created contacts. Scores are generated after sufficient interaction data has been collected.

# Calculation of Bolt Priority Score

Element451 calculates prioritization using numeric scores and labels based on the following behaviors and classifications:

* Interactions with Email
* Interactions with SMS
* Interactions with Conversations
* Interactions with Events
* Interactions with Applications
* Interactions with Appointments
* Days Between Key Behaviors
* Days since Last Key Behavior
* Institution Type
* Institution Category
* Institution Size

# Bolt Priority Index Descriptions

The Bolt Priority Index categorizes contacts into the following levels:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Priority Index** | **Description** |
| **Prime** | Very active and likely to take the next step. |
| **Warm** | Active and moving in the right direction. |
| **Steady** | Somewhat active but not close to moving forward. |
| **Stalled** | Somewhat inactive and unlikely to take a step soon. |
| **Stagnant** | Inactive and very unlikely to move forward any time soon. |

# Understanding the Difference: Bolt Priority Index vs. Student Engagement Score

The **Bolt Priority Index** is a forward-looking tool that predicts future student behavior, distinguishing it from the **Student Engagement Score**. While the Engagement Score reflects a student’s current activity within the Element451 platform, the Priority Index focuses on what lies ahead.

Using artificial intelligence and machine learning, the Priority Index forecasts how likely a student is to move forward in the enrollment funnel. This predictive power enables you to:

* **Make smarter decisions** about allocating resources
* **Target your efforts** on students most likely to enroll
* **Act with greater confidence** in expected outcomes

Although the Priority Index can help identify at-risk students, its primary strength lies in uncovering opportunities. By pinpointing students with a high probability of progressing, you can optimize your recruitment strategies and improve enrollment rates.

In essence, the Bolt Priority Index transforms data into foresight, empowering you to make an impact where it matters most.

# Locating a Student's Priority Index

As [mentioned earlier](#h_01ec530858), you can use Segments to quickly generate a list of students who meet specific conditions related to the Bolt Priority Index. However, if you need to find an individual student’s Priority Index for a one-off situation, you can do so by navigating to their profile.

To locate the Priority Index within a student’s profile, follow these steps:

1. Go to the **Traits** section, which provides detailed information about the student.
2. Access Traits through one of the following methods:

   * **Traits Profile Card**: This must be enabled in the [profile template](https://intercom.help/element451/en/articles/6449965-bolt-profile-templates) assigned to your user view.
   * **More Data**: Click “More Data” in the left-hand panel of the student’s profile. This will open the student's Profile Data sidesheet. From here, locate and select Traits in the lefthand menu. Expand the traits to view all and locate the Priority Index in the list of traits.

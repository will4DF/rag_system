On this page, we go over how to export the profile data of students in a segment directly to your email.

# Overview

If you have a list of users in a particular segment that you need to export, this guide will walk you through the process step-by-step.

---

# Exporting a Segment

1. Navigate to **Contacts** > **Segments**
2. Locate the segment you wish to export and click the **three vertical dots icon**

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877618150/e16439e71c9d4cc2fd6df299/Segments+-+Export.png?expires=1784333700&signature=c79c10c4cbe0283167ea560f4f972d88d876308d98ee2c9d1041614cc03e3075&req=fCcgEMh2nIRfFb4f3HP0gADAvwsSFY7R4yVwtEfRDoOJMSqa1%2Fjc2KAl%2B4Ec%0ARlU%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877618150/e16439e71c9d4cc2fd6df299/Segments+-+Export.png?expires=1784333700&signature=c79c10c4cbe0283167ea560f4f972d88d876308d98ee2c9d1041614cc03e3075&req=fCcgEMh2nIRfFb4f3HP0gADAvwsSFY7R4yVwtEfRDoOJMSqa1%2Fjc2KAl%2B4Ec%0ARlU%3D%0A)
3. From the menu, select **Export**

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877619190/12645a42226c8d130d87ae86/Segments+-+Menu+-+Export.png?expires=1784333700&signature=704ac0dfc8e0fbe8fc949a8a58a4cb50519aa352c410f0f1566e1dd27cb6c83a&req=fCcgEMh3nIhfFb4f3HP0gDEqT07s0eBGJ07TYUuHWcxjWinHPHz10AUTTjzX%0A5WQ%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877619190/12645a42226c8d130d87ae86/Segments+-+Menu+-+Export.png?expires=1784333700&signature=704ac0dfc8e0fbe8fc949a8a58a4cb50519aa352c410f0f1566e1dd27cb6c83a&req=fCcgEMh3nIhfFb4f3HP0gDEqT07s0eBGJ07TYUuHWcxjWinHPHz10AUTTjzX%0A5WQ%3D%0A)
4. Select a template for your Export. Note: *A template is required in order to export your users.*

   * To manage your export templates, navigate to **Data +** **Automation** > **Import/Export** > **Templates**, and for a guide on creating templates, check out [this article](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9006851-creating-export-mapping-templates).
5. Click **Create Report**
6. Your export file will be generated and emailed to you, or you can track its progress by navigating to **Data +** **Automation** > **Import/Export** > **Export**. Keep in mind that larger segments with more template fields will take longer to generate.

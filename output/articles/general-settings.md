# Overview

**General Settings** is the central location for managing key information about your institution and setting global preferences in Element451. It includes tools for branding, communication compliance, billing, and administrative setup.

Within General Settings, you can:

* Customize your institution's branding (colors, logos, and fonts)
* Register phone numbers for SMS and voice calls (A2P 10DLC + CNAM)
* Track credit usage and manage billing details
* Configure foundational details like time zone, contact info, and school facts
* Set up SFTP accounts, privacy policy links, and internal login messages
* Authenticate sender domains and manage approved "send from" addresses

## Accessing General Settings

You can find the general section of your global settings by navigating to your profile picture in the top right corner of the orange navigation menu and then clicking **Settings > General**.

# Phone Registration (A2P 10DLC)

Before you can send text messages or make voice calls through Element451, your phone numbers must be registered to comply with carrier regulations and ensure message deliverability. This includes both SMS (via A2P 10DLC registration) and voice call features like CNAM and SHAKEN/STIR. Element451 provides a self-service registration process directly within General Settings, under **Phone Registration**.

[Explore More: Phone Registration →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10628858-registering-your-phone-number-for-text-messages-voice-calling)

# Billing + Usage

Element451 uses a usage-based billing model for some premium features. Your institution receives an annual allotment of credits (based on your contract), which can be used for services like texting, in-app calling, Bolt AI tools, and more. Additional credits can be purchased at any time. Both **Billing** and **Usage** are found in General Settings:

* **Billing**: View your current plan and credit balance, purchase additional credits, and manage billing details.
* **Usage**: Monitor how credits are consumed across features, identify trends, and forecast future needs.

[Explore More: Usage + Billing →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10421758-usage-based-billing-credits)

# Campaign Tokens

Tokens managed under General Settings are no longer supported and remain available only for legacy features. Additionally, these tokens are not used in Packs. Instead, [Packs Content Tokens](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6825249-guide-to-packs-tokens) are managed separately within their respective settings.

## Internal User Login Acknowledgment

The **Internal User Login Acknowledgment** feature allows you to display a custom message that internal users must acknowledge **before** **logging** **into** **the** **system**. This is ideal for sharing compliance notices, internal policies, or terms of use that require confirmation prior to access.

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/2364926788/b462bfd822e4f78e19095b11e096/Internal-Acknowledgment-Modal.png?expires=1784333700&signature=5517e2d324beb48ccc0559231e32b7539bff5ca83fde235a227cdabccec0b920&req=diMhEsB8m4ZXUfMW1HO4zfbM8CHXOSo6J50cGLtlYxu7dBvb9SKqe2EOBxWH%0AhNIXRJwtB2bxuE8mywc%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/2364926788/b462bfd822e4f78e19095b11e096/Internal-Acknowledgment-Modal.png?expires=1784333700&signature=5517e2d324beb48ccc0559231e32b7539bff5ca83fde235a227cdabccec0b920&req=diMhEsB8m4ZXUfMW1HO4zfbM8CHXOSo6J50cGLtlYxu7dBvb9SKqe2EOBxWH%0AhNIXRJwtB2bxuE8mywc%3D%0A)

The acknowledgment modal appears **on the Element451 login page for your instance**. Once acknowledged, the message will not appear again unless:

* The **Disclaimer Message** is updated or changed.
* The user clears their browser cache.

**Settings**

* **Show Internal Login Acknowledgment:** A slide toggle to **enable** or **disable** the acknowledgment modal.
* **Disclaimer Message:** The text displayed in the pop-up modal communicates the terms or policies requiring acknowledgment.

  + **Example:** *By logging in, you agree to comply with all Element University policies, including FERPA guidelines for student data privacy, security protocols, and acceptable use standards. Unauthorized access or misuse of student information may result in disciplinary action.*
* **Updated At:** A timestamp that indicates when the acknowledgment message was last updated. This helps internal users verify the recency of the message.

​  
By authenticating your domains, it tells email servers that emails from Element451 are truly from you and improves email deliverability and security.

## DMARC

* As of Feb 1, 2024, Google and Yahoo [domains also require DMARC records.](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8810117-overview-of-email-authentication)
* "v=DMARC1; p=none" is the simplest policy that meets the Google/Yahoo standards. However, your organization may have stricter requirements. Please take a look at this guide to [implementing DMARC](https://www.twilio.com/docs/sendgrid/ui/sending-email/how-to-implement-dmarc) for more details.

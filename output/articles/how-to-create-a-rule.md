Learn about the different steps necessary to create a Rule.

# Overview

Creating a Rule is a multi-step process that involves the initial creation, then adding steps and actions, and finally, fine-tuning it by adding specific conditions and triggers to make it work the way you need.

In this article, we will break down the creation of a Rule into eight steps.

1. Create a Rule
2. Add the First Step + Action
3. Add Additional Actions
4. Set Condition(s)
5. Add Additional Steps
6. Finish Rule
7. Activate Rule
8. Add Trigger or Set Schedule

# Step 1: Create a New Rule

1. Navigate to **Data + Automations** > **Workflows** > **Rules**.
2. Click the **+ New Rule** button ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/942041658/38711e72eaac8e3d6c431d4e/Screenshot+2024-01-23+at+8.19.19%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784430000&signature=44c84b6a8b971b527903d46b259fd79ac84bd1ed26e854ca6b5d9c16b56f8936&req=fSQlFs1%2Fm4RXFb4X1HO4gc3scCtcpJnqOf863B1YclAFHheNVLm299x%2FjQj6%0A) in the top right corner of the header.
3. Give your Rule a **Name**.
4. Provide a brief **description** of what the Rule will do.
5. Select a **Type**:

   * **Triggered**: Enrolls Contacts based on a characteristic or Action taken. For a list of actions and descriptions, review our article, [Actions](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1500292-actions).
   * **Scheduled:** Enrolls Contacts based on a schedule and specific conditions. The actions for a Scheduled Rule are limited to *Create Login Token*, *Create Profile*, *Validate Phone Numbers*, or *Run Rule*.
6. Click **Save** in the top right corner.
7. You will be taken to the edit page for your new Rule, where you can configure and define what it will do.

# Step 2: Add the First Step + Action

After completing step one, you are taken to **edit** your new Rule. This screen will appear when you open your Rule to edit, run, or view activity.

## How to Add a Step + Action

1. Click the **+ button** ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/937563380/c6f2bfab71e573baeaf9c5cc/Screenshot+2024-01-18+at+4.28.01%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784430000&signature=0dbc957e5877673b9e32691406111e7263fd422135cafd8b0e14e4dceb8af74b&req=fSMgE899nolfFb4X1HO4gazj%2FaWs2fjj1xKqg8tFSiKpfkdLWr5B3xUrYD%2Bf%0A) to open the **Action** side sheet, allowing you to select an action.
2. Click **Select Action** to pick an action from the list. For a complete list of actions and descriptions, view our [help article on Actions](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1500292-actions).
3. After selecting the Action, you'll be prompted to select a corresponding value. For example, if you choose **Send Communication** as your action type, you must select which campaign you wish to send.
4. After clicking save, your first step will automatically be created using the Action you chose.

[Learn More: Actions](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1500292-actions)

# Step 5: Add Additional Steps

Do you need to add additional steps/actions to your Workflow?

**🟢 Yes:** Follow the steps outlined in Steps 2-4 for each step you need to add. Then, when finished, proceed with Step 6.

**🔴 No:** Proceed to Step 6.

# Step 6: Finish Rule

If you add conditions to your Rule, you likely already did this. However, it is best practice to add one final Action to your Rule, **Finish** **Workflow**. This indicates that it is the final step of the Rule.

1. **Add an action** to your final step (as demonstrated in [Step 3](#h_90d410f7be)).
2. Click **Select Action** to select **Finish Workflow** as the Action.
3. Click **Save**.

# Step 7: Activate Rule

As the final step before determining how Contacts will enroll in your Rule, you'll need to activate your Rule.

1. Navigate to the drop-down menu, Inactive, located to the right of your Rule name.
2. Click the drop-down and select **Active**.

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/942059253/ef6d8bf4efd43d1eb45a52a5/Screenshot+2024-01-23+at+9.05.05%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=513a2b6b187409598c99ed222c02ae60fbc84aa0aca08a5de6bd7327f23bdb30&req=fSQlFsx3n4RcFb4f3HP0gMBeognGJeSTCTb9WWTbfM%2BkfaUu%2FguZRlPow6Ta%0Azb9x7SU7jmIuOysciw%3D%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/942059253/ef6d8bf4efd43d1eb45a52a5/Screenshot+2024-01-23+at+9.05.05%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=513a2b6b187409598c99ed222c02ae60fbc84aa0aca08a5de6bd7327f23bdb30&req=fSQlFsx3n4RcFb4f3HP0gMBeognGJeSTCTb9WWTbfM%2BkfaUu%2FguZRlPow6Ta%0Azb9x7SU7jmIuOysciw%3D%3D%0A)

   ​

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/944066497/8f81970dc15f8a542e177242/Screenshot+2024-01-25+at+2.09.43%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=e66fc55e5248f8551af97f8a9bde70b834f0ef4061859134a8cb8bc18e39ba61&req=fSQjFs94mYhYFb4f3HP0gE%2F7Dy445vHB6Gh6siC4PJjagEHzAwsOtBE3GxK7%0Ad5c%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/944066497/8f81970dc15f8a542e177242/Screenshot+2024-01-25+at+2.09.43%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=e66fc55e5248f8551af97f8a9bde70b834f0ef4061859134a8cb8bc18e39ba61&req=fSQjFs94mYhYFb4f3HP0gE%2F7Dy445vHB6Gh6siC4PJjagEHzAwsOtBE3GxK7%0Ad5c%3D%0A)

5. Click **Save** in the top right corner.

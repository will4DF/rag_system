# Overview

Saved Replies allow you to quickly respond to frequently asked questions or other inquiries with consistent, pre-drafted messages. This feature saves you time by eliminating the need to retype the same responses and ensures consistent communication across all your interactions.

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303471856/37f6287525d62682c9164752083f/Saved+Replies+-+General.png?expires=1784333700&signature=98e0776cd8a2371ea541c4b50053f06a8fd417e26b81792d2caa301e497e7390&req=dSMnFc15nIlaX%2FMW1HO4zQD5%2FPN8PqQtVwckX8njjqySmEIPk5uH52OAlV3p%0AdCLLTGRP1B0pE%2BUu0HU%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303471856/37f6287525d62682c9164752083f/Saved+Replies+-+General.png?expires=1784333700&signature=98e0776cd8a2371ea541c4b50053f06a8fd417e26b81792d2caa301e497e7390&req=dSMnFc15nIlaX%2FMW1HO4zQD5%2FPN8PqQtVwckX8njjqySmEIPk5uH52OAlV3p%0AdCLLTGRP1B0pE%2BUu0HU%3D%0A)

You can use Saved Replies with **Email, SMS, and Messenger (live chat)** conversations.

# Creating Saved Replies

There are several ways to create a Saved Reply in Element451:

### Save a Message Directly from a Conversation

While replying to a message:

1. Type your response in the conversation window.
2. Click the **Save a Reply** button (floppy disk icon).
3. Provide the following details:

   * **Name**: Give your Saved Reply a clear, descriptive name.
   * **Channels:** Select the channels (Email, SMS, Messenger) where the reply can be used.
   * **Content:** Add the message content. For **Email**, include a subject line in addition to the message.
4. Click **Save**.

### Create Saved Replies from the Saved Replies Menu

If you’re already in a conversation:

1. Click the **clipboard icon** (Saved Replies) to open the Saved Replies menu.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303457824/ccdaa239e06af04cde3e59b0b16c/Saved+Replies+Icon.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f6540bfedef4e444c02c77aa2116d8121a25a97264864e8c9e468002421fd88a&req=dSMnFc17moldXfMW1HO4zejlw%2BBOjqKU781MX0FA9P0pXHUIrkJq4UKEOabj%0ADQrh%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303457824/ccdaa239e06af04cde3e59b0b16c/Saved+Replies+Icon.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f6540bfedef4e444c02c77aa2116d8121a25a97264864e8c9e468002421fd88a&req=dSMnFc17moldXfMW1HO4zejlw%2BBOjqKU781MX0FA9P0pXHUIrkJq4UKEOabj%0ADQrh%0A)
2. From the menu, click **Create** to open the creation sidesheet.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303461566/c737e6e188c62002bbbb57bbf6b1/Create+Saved+Reply+-+Menu.png?expires=1784333700&signature=e781a631428f281edf8539039638a7bc7cab18a6fb3c924be021de6fcadd1752&req=dSMnFc14nIRZX%2FMW1HO4zfhEYGehNVZzrVTct8m68seCraM1ASjXS50qt06K%0APRd5%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303461566/c737e6e188c62002bbbb57bbf6b1/Create+Saved+Reply+-+Menu.png?expires=1784333700&signature=e781a631428f281edf8539039638a7bc7cab18a6fb3c924be021de6fcadd1752&req=dSMnFc14nIRZX%2FMW1HO4zfhEYGehNVZzrVTct8m68seCraM1ASjXS50qt06K%0APRd5%0A)
3. Provide the following details:

   * **Name**: Give your Saved Reply a clear, descriptive name.
   * **Channels:** Select the channels (Email, SMS, Messenger) where the reply can be used.
   * **Content:** Add the message content. For **Email**, include a subject line in addition to the message.
4. Click **Save**.

**Pro Tip:** You can also **Manage** your Saved Replies from this menu. Click the **Manage** button to open a sidesheet to edit or delete existing replies.

### Create Saved Replies in Conversation Settings

1. Go to **Engagement > Conversations > Conversation Settings**.
2. Under the **General** tab, find the **Saved Replies** section.
3. Click **+ Create Reply**.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303465539/d79b96698c19d37a7d92cf884afa/Saved+Replys+-+Add+from+Gen+Settings.png?expires=1784333700&signature=92daecd6e62536a4aebaf2f80f53c731d87af5572e71fdc4c367810f556a9c7d&req=dSMnFc14mIRcUPMW1HO4zRqQt3rQYJ8RO81CIfhX66tzKNgGDhMCkgMSmyiC%0AGqBS%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303465539/d79b96698c19d37a7d92cf884afa/Saved+Replys+-+Add+from+Gen+Settings.png?expires=1784333700&signature=92daecd6e62536a4aebaf2f80f53c731d87af5572e71fdc4c367810f556a9c7d&req=dSMnFc14mIRcUPMW1HO4zRqQt3rQYJ8RO81CIfhX66tzKNgGDhMCkgMSmyiC%0AGqBS%0A)
4. Fill in the following:

   * **Name**: Add a descriptive name.
   * **Channels**: Choose the channels for your Saved Reply.
   * **Message Content**: Add the message for each channel. For **Email**, include a subject line.
5. Save your reply.

# Managing Saved Replies

To edit or delete Saved Replies:

1. Navigate to the **Saved Replies** section under **Conversation Settings**:

   * **Engagement > Conversations > Conversation Settings > General > Saved Replies**
2. Locate the reply you want to manage.
3. Click the **three vertical dots** icon next to the reply.
4. Select **Edit** to update the reply or **Delete** to remove it.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303464112/d924639b16d5e8636df618180299/Saved+Replies+-+General+Settings+-+Edit+or+Delete.png?expires=1784333700&signature=024440a1d062d02f173d56b5ac53ececa248fda07281ea2f0c126b7bc3b8aeec&req=dSMnFc14mYBeW%2FMW1HO4zSUN98PhmeX%2FgJEdeW1fg0Yd6wIs74whLofBTHRr%0ADooH%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303464112/d924639b16d5e8636df618180299/Saved+Replies+-+General+Settings+-+Edit+or+Delete.png?expires=1784333700&signature=024440a1d062d02f173d56b5ac53ececa248fda07281ea2f0c126b7bc3b8aeec&req=dSMnFc14mYBeW%2FMW1HO4zSUN98PhmeX%2FgJEdeW1fg0Yd6wIs74whLofBTHRr%0ADooH%0A)

# Inserting a Saved Reply into a Conversation

To insert a Saved Reply into a conversation:

1. With a conversation open, click the **clipboard icon** (Saved Replies) in the message toolbar.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303457362/035743b40ed84bcf2d466d799827/Saved+Replies+Icon.png?expires=1784333700&signature=3aac676d34d6e5c9e3fd26f448c1b7d644571a54852c661d112727e224a32afd&req=dSMnFc17moJZW%2FMW1HO4zRLD5A6%2FoK0fc2Rys6WyumTHiMPE2nFUv8F5M7hS%0AGmAa%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1303457362/035743b40ed84bcf2d466d799827/Saved+Replies+Icon.png?expires=1784333700&signature=3aac676d34d6e5c9e3fd26f448c1b7d644571a54852c661d112727e224a32afd&req=dSMnFc17moJZW%2FMW1HO4zRLD5A6%2FoK0fc2Rys6WyumTHiMPE2nFUv8F5M7hS%0AGmAa%0A)
2. The Saved Replies menu will open.
3. Scroll through the list or use the **search bar** to find a specific reply.
4. Click on the reply to insert it into your message.
5. Make any necessary adjustments before sending the message.

**Pro Tip:** You can create or manage Saved Replies directly from this menu without leaving the conversation. Simply click **Create** or **Manage** to open the sidesheet.

# Enhancements for Saved Replies

Saved Replies support the following enhancements to make your responses more engaging and efficient:

* **Image Attachments**: Include visuals directly in your Saved Replies.
* **Emojis**: Add emojis to personalize and add a friendly tone.
* **Loom Videos** (additional purchase required): Embed video links from Loom.
* **Covideo** (additional purchase required): Share video messages using Covideo.

## Using Tokens in Saved Replies

Tokens allow you to dynamically personalize your Saved Replies with details about the recipient, sender, or institution.

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1323087494/6e4e0e685c21be05cf0bc2138f31/Inserting%2BTokens.png?expires=1784333700&signature=18d2cc151ca06eb7c424e92959ca3f5de8db962d0f923bf7a49532451ef4a51f&req=dSMlFcl2moVWXfMW1HO4zUS%2FonVKwDJfPTkm3vEaN1JKdBvdm9OZQeGJhuOX%0AX7REFs1v6WhirBdz4XE%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1323087494/6e4e0e685c21be05cf0bc2138f31/Inserting%2BTokens.png?expires=1784333700&signature=18d2cc151ca06eb7c424e92959ca3f5de8db962d0f923bf7a49532451ef4a51f&req=dSMlFcl2moVWXfMW1HO4zUS%2FonVKwDJfPTkm3vEaN1JKdBvdm9OZQeGJhuOX%0AX7REFs1v6WhirBdz4XE%3D%0A)

### Important Notes Regarding Tokens in Saved Replies

* The **only tokens supported** at this time are:

  + **External Participant Tokens**:

    - User First Name: [user:first-name]
    - User Last Name: [user:last-name]
    - User Preferred Name: [user:preferred-name]
  + **Sender Tokens**:

    - Sender First Name: [sender:first-name]
    - Sender Last Name: [sender:last-name]
  + **School (Institution) Tokens:**

    - Institution Name: [client:name]
* Fallback parameters are **not** supported. However, when you insert a saved reply into a conversation message, the tokens are replaced with their corresponding values. If a token isn’t replaced, it means the value is missing, allowing you to manually update it before sending.

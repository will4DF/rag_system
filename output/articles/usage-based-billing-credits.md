# Overview

📌 This article covers the **Usage Credits** billing mode. For an overview of how Element451 bills usage — and how to tell which mode your plan uses — see [Usage-Based Pricing](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/15934836-usage-based-pricing).

Under the Usage Credits model, usage-based features such as phone calling and SMS/MMS messaging are billed based on usage and draw from your included annual credit balance.

Each year, on the anniversary of your contract activation date, your institution receives a new allotment of usage credits. This credit package is outlined in your Element451 agreement and can be used across any eligible feature that requires credits. We’ve designed these allotments to support typical usage based on your institution’s size, so most partners never need to purchase more.

In this article, we'll walk you through the **Billing** and **Usage** sections of General Settings, explain how credits work, and help you manage your usage effectively

## Accessing Billing

Access to billing is granted through the permission ***Administer*** ***Billing***, which is **automatically** included in the **Element451 Administrator** system permission group. Internal users within this permission group will have the authority to purchase additional credits and enable premium features.

To access the **Billing** page, navigate to **Settings > General Settings > Billing**. The **Billing** page is divided into four sections ([plan](#h_6f72227eb6), [usage credits](#h_7bae518e41), [enable usage-based features](#h_03d45074f5), and [credit history](#h_d88ce02ad9)). Below, we describe each in detail:

## Plan

The **Plan** card displays:

* Your Element451 [plan](https://www.element451.com/pricing)
* Your Element451 [support tier](https://element451.com/pricing#:~:text=IMPLEMENTATION%20%26%20SUPPORT-,Standard%20Support,-Industry%2DLeading%20Support)
* The date you became a customer

  + This is the date when your annual credit allowance will be added to your balance.
* Billing email address

  + This is the email address associated with the credit card on file and where your invoices/receipts will be sent upon purchasing additional credits.

### Purchasing Additional Credits

To add more credits:

1. Click **Add Credits**.
2. Enter the number of credits you wish to purchase.
3. Click **Confirm** in the top-right corner.
4. Complete your purchase on the payment screen.

**Important:**

* Additional credits cost **$0.01 each**.
* Purchased credits are **non-refundable**, **have no cash value**, and **do not expire**.

### Billing Information

* View and manage payment methods.
* Update billing details.
* Review invoice history.

*The information in Billing, specifically your billing details and payment methods, only applies to your **additional usage credits** and is not used for your Element451 annual contractual agreement fee.*

### Usage Credit Settings (Auto Recharge + Admin Alerts)

Manage the following features:

**Auto Recharge**: Automatically adds a set amount of credits when your balance falls below a designated threshold.

**Admin Alerts**: Displays a banner in Element451 to notify admins when the credit balance drops below a specified threshold.

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1401519192/7ef097d9e01bd73cc55871596e31/Credit+Threshold+Banner.png?expires=1784333700&signature=066dfb1858cc27999a3f88eb5c982d502546d6c5fac0f382a1ea2dcd42e6870d&req=dSQnF8x%2FlIBWW%2FMW1HO4ze3yAR%2B99AG%2ByjqG2kNfOEkHMeZ1mQ%2BKJnMxU3Rq%0ATPfiby7%2BvhMaGcK3acQ%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1401519192/7ef097d9e01bd73cc55871596e31/Credit+Threshold+Banner.png?expires=1784333700&signature=066dfb1858cc27999a3f88eb5c982d502546d6c5fac0f382a1ea2dcd42e6870d&req=dSQnF8x%2FlIBWW%2FMW1HO4ze3yAR%2B99AG%2ByjqG2kNfOEkHMeZ1mQ%2BKJnMxU3Rq%0ATPfiby7%2BvhMaGcK3acQ%3D%0A)

To configure:

1. Enable **Auto Recharge** and set the threshold for replenishment and the number of credits to add.
2. Enable **Admin Alerts** and set the threshold for triggering the in-app notification.

## Enable Usage-Based Features

The **Enable Usage-Based Features** card allows you to manage access to premium features that require usage credits.

Currently, only **Phone Calling (in-app calling)** can be self-enabled here. Other credit-using features, such as SMS/MMS messaging, are automatically enabled and draw from your credit balance as they’re used. There’s no need to turn these on manually.

Note: Some customers may see features like AI Block Rendering or AI Workflow Evaluations listed under their billing section. These will soon be removed from usage-based pricing and no longer appear in this area.

## Credit History

The **Credit History** card provides a monthly period-based view of your starting balance, credits used during the month, credits added during the month, and ending balance.

### Added Credits

When credits are added to your balance, they will be listed here under the 'added this period' section. Your annual credit allowance per your contract will be added on your account anniversary and be listed as an "Annual Contract Grant." Any additional credits you purchase will be labeled as "Purchased." Element451 may occasionally add "One-Time Grant" credits for promotions at their discretion.

## Accessing Usage

To access the **Usage** page, navigate to **Settings > General Settings > Usage**. You must have the *`Administer Billing` permission* to access the Usage tab.

## Credit Usage Breakdown

* Displays the **quantity** of units used per feature and the **total credits** consumed.
* Offers insights into which features use the most credits to help optimize usage.
* Defaults to the **current month** but can be adjusted using the date picker.

## Credit Consumption by Day

* Provides a visual overview of your credit usage over time.
* Defaults to the **current month** but can be adjusted using the date picker.
* Helps track trends and ensure you stay within your credit allocation.

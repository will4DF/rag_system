Use the Unsplash integration for easy access to free, high-quality images, enhancing campaigns with engaging visuals and cropping tools.

# Overview

Visual content is key to creating compelling campaigns that resonate with the right students. At Element451, we understand the importance of engaging imagery in storytelling. That's why we've integrated [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/), providing you with easy access to a vast library of high-quality images. You can enhance your campaigns with the perfect photos, at no extra cost!

---

# Finding + Using Images from Unsplash

To add an image from Unsplash to your campaign:

1. While editing a campaign, insert an **Image Block**.
2. In the **Image Settings**, click **+ Add a file**.  
   ​

3. Navigate to the **Upload** tab, then select **Upload from Unsplash**.  
   ​

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/324236987/2b09b4f589821a49156570d9/Screen+Shot+2021-04-13+at+4.53.26+PM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=9dbb7ef7c198a5954a04442b93fe6b63ecfafec4df1460d82aa8956f87e001b6&req=dyIjFMp4lIlYFb4f3HP0gIpZyBchmw7wxtGdJESDFqpxFPAOl3%2BSeFHoC3tF%0Ax0E%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/324236987/2b09b4f589821a49156570d9/Screen+Shot+2021-04-13+at+4.53.26+PM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=9dbb7ef7c198a5954a04442b93fe6b63ecfafec4df1460d82aa8956f87e001b6&req=dyIjFMp4lIlYFb4f3HP0gIpZyBchmw7wxtGdJESDFqpxFPAOl3%2BSeFHoC3tF%0Ax0E%3D%0A)

5. Once you’ve found your ideal image, click **Select** to insert it into your campaign.

# Cropping Images

Optimize how your images fit within your campaigns by cropping them to highlight the most important elements:

1. When editing a campaign, click the **gear** **icon** on the image to open the editing panel.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/330688743/3663aee69711263d3fb7cc36/Screen+Shot+2021-04-29+at+11.37.12+AM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=7e33464401134353479c22c2a8af701cfcbd42e522628a6c538f6795935a0a5c&req=dyMnEMF2moVcFb4f3HP0gBLMDNE28xOb1K6Qa2XptCYNy%2B4RYhlEPEbUp7hL%0AEHo%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/330688743/3663aee69711263d3fb7cc36/Screen+Shot+2021-04-29+at+11.37.12+AM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=7e33464401134353479c22c2a8af701cfcbd42e522628a6c538f6795935a0a5c&req=dyMnEMF2moVcFb4f3HP0gBLMDNE28xOb1K6Qa2XptCYNy%2B4RYhlEPEbUp7hL%0AEHo%3D%0A)
2. Below the image, you’ll find a **Crop** option. Click on **Crop**. A new editing window will appear, allowing you to adjust the size and focus of the image to suit your campaign's needs better.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/670256798/cb08c1c3b63e6806929471e4/crop_gif.gif?expires=1784333700&signature=023aac251482bd2068e342a4c6aa6bd53c9a46b7ffb1da6c12335179e690ffd4&req=cicnFMx4mohXFb4f3HP0gCQoLPrWxqOmQcK8W5quq4g%2FKbGkpRIMVgNW0qnr%0Ajv4%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/670256798/cb08c1c3b63e6806929471e4/crop_gif.gif?expires=1784333700&signature=023aac251482bd2068e342a4c6aa6bd53c9a46b7ffb1da6c12335179e690ffd4&req=cicnFMx4mohXFb4f3HP0gCQoLPrWxqOmQcK8W5quq4g%2FKbGkpRIMVgNW0qnr%0Ajv4%3D%0A)

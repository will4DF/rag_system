Q: Does the Canvas incident impact Element451? A: No.

We’re aware of the recent security incident involving Instructure’s Canvas LMS platform and the associated ransomware/extortion activity reported in May 2026. Public reporting indicates the incident affected Canvas infrastructure and potentially exposed certain Canvas-hosted user data.  
​  
Element451’s Canvas integration is architected as a **read-only, one-way integration from Canvas into Element451**. Canvas does not have access to Element451 systems, databases, or customer data through this integration. There is no bidirectional sync that would allow Canvas-originated access into Element451 infrastructure.

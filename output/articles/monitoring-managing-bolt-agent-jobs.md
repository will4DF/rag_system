Learn how to track job-level performance and metrics, make updates to active jobs, and monitor overall impact.

# Overview

The **All Jobs** page is your hub for managing every Bolt Agent Job in your institution. From here, you can see how many jobs are active, track overall impact, and take quick actions on individual jobs.

In this article, you’ll learn how to:

* Navigate the **All Jobs** page and understand its metrics
* Open, review, and edit details of an existing job
* Edit job settings
* Duplicate or delete a job

---

# Monitoring Your Jobs

Once a job is created, you can monitor it from **Engagement** > **Bolt Agents** > **Jobs**.

## Summary Bar

At the top of the All Jobs page, you’ll see high-level counts (active jobs, completed goals, and estimation of hours saved) for all jobs across your institution.

📈 These counts help you understand the overall impact your agents are making.

Stats listing in the All Jobs view include all activity—even for jobs that were later deleted. This means counts of completed goals and estimated time saved will still reflect those deleted jobs.

## When Do These Counts Refresh?

Job metrics do not refresh continuously on a timer. Counts load when you open the page and recalculate when your view changes. On a job's **People** view, the metrics bar loads when you open the view and updates when you change the selected job, search, or filters. If enrollment activity happens while you're on the page (new enrollments, status changes, approvals, cancellations), a **reload icon** appears on the bar. Click it to fetch the latest counts.

# Jobs by Assigned Bolt Agent

In addition to viewing all jobs from the **All Jobs** page, you can see jobs that are scoped to a specific Bolt Agent. Each agent has their own dedicated page that displays their **settings**, **approvals**, and **jobs**.

## How to View Jobs for a Specific Bolt Agent

1. Navigate to the **Engagement** > **Bolt Agents** > **All Agents**.
2. Find and click on the agent you want to view.
3. On the agent’s page, use the left-hand menu to switch between:

   * **My Approvals** – shows approvals scoped to that agent.
   * **Jobs** – shows jobs scoped to that agent.

# Viewing + Editing Existing Jobs

While most job settings can be edited at any time, **the job’s goal cannot be changed** after it is created. Each time the agent enters the “thinking” state, that is, when they evaluate what to do next for a specific contact, they pull the most current version of the job. This means that any updates you’ve made will be used going forward. This makes Bolt Agent Jobs flexible and adaptive, allowing you to iterate as you go.

**To view or edit a job:**

1. Find the job you want to view/edit.
2. Click on the job name in the table or, alternatively, click the vertical ellipsis **(⋮)** at the end of the row and select "open."

🚨 **Important | Changing Instructions:** If you’ve already launched the job and the agent has taken action for some contacts, changing instructions significantly midstream may cause inconsistencies. The agent remembers what it’s done so far, and a major change could result in confusing behavior. When possible, keep instructions consistent once a job is in progress. If your strategy has changed significantly, consider creating a new job.

# Duplicating or Deleting Jobs

You can quickly create or remove jobs from the All Jobs table.

1. Find the job you want to act on.
2. Click the vertical ellipsis **(⋮)** at the end of the row.
3. Choose one of the following:

   * **Duplicate** → Create a new job using the same configuration.
   * **Delete** → Permanently remove the job from your institution.

## Important Notes on Duplicating Jobs

* Enrollments (contacts) are not copied. You’ll need to manually add people to the duplicated job, either by segment or individually. 👉 See [Reviewing + Managing Enrollments in Bolt Agent Jobs](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/12037492-managing-enrollments-contacts-approvals-in-bolt-agent-jobs) for details.
* The duplicate job inherits the status (Active/Inactive) of the original. If the original was active, the copy will also start as active. Consider switching it to Inactive while you refine instructions and add enrollments.

# Organizing Jobs Using Folders

The **Folders** feature in Bolt Agent Jobs allows you to organize your Jobs for quick access and better management.

* **All Jobs:** The system automatically generates an **"All Jobs"** folder, where every Job is listed. Even if you assign a Job to a custom folder, it will still appear in *All Jobs*.
* **Custom Folders:** You can create folders tailored to your needs. These folders are listed in alphabetical order and cannot be reordered.

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1887694106/97e8470107957fbb4dbacb4e1073/Job+Folders.png?expires=1784333700&signature=c82f304fd6e000a33db77d50cc054dc0464346d75dba980c923f8728aad0d189&req=dSgvEc93mYBfX%2FMW1HO4zXZFqM3g8exZXU5aVY5GrRs0SHYs0E44AnUJk8cm%0AmTcBcdyRjiqK1UvmMfY%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1887694106/97e8470107957fbb4dbacb4e1073/Job+Folders.png?expires=1784333700&signature=c82f304fd6e000a33db77d50cc054dc0464346d75dba980c923f8728aad0d189&req=dSgvEc93mYBfX%2FMW1HO4zXZFqM3g8exZXU5aVY5GrRs0SHYs0E44AnUJk8cm%0AmTcBcdyRjiqK1UvmMfY%3D%0A)

## Creating Folders + Managing Folders

* **Create a Folder**: Click the **+** icon next to **Folders**, name it.
* **Rename/Delete a Folder**: Click the vertical ellipsis **(⋮)** next to the folder and select either rename or delete. *Note: Deleting a folder does **not** delete Jobs.*
* **Add Job to Folder**: From the Job list, click the three vertical dots at the end of a Job's row, select **Move to Folder**, and choose the desired folder.

# Adding or Removing People

After a job has been created, you can manually add new people or cancel existing enrollments at any time.

* Use the **Add People** button in the job header to manually enroll contacts or add a segment.
* To cancel an enrollment, go to the **People** tab of the job and remove the contact.

👉 For step-by-step instructions, see [Reviewing + Managing Enrollments in Bolt Agent Jobs](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/12037492-managing-enrollments-contacts-approvals-in-bolt-agent-jobs).

# Viewing Conversations Related to a Job

In addition to reviewing enrollments individually, you may want to see all conversations tied to a specific job at once. You can do this from the **Conversations Inbox** using **Advanced Filtering**.

1. Open the **Conversations Inbox**.
2. Select **Advanced Filtering**.
3. Under the filter options, use the **Bolt** **Agent** **Jobs** filter by selecting the job from the dropdown that you want to review.

This will display every conversation associated with that job, across all enrollments.

✨ **Pro Tip:** You can also filter by individual Bolt Agents.

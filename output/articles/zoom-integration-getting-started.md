Summary of the prerequisites and Zoom installation process into your Element451 instance.

# Overview

The Element451 + Zoom integration makes it easy to host your events in Zoom and to automatically take attendance for the students who log into the Zoom-hosted event.

**What data will be shared between Element451 + Zoom?**

* **Meetings details**: Allows us to display and update meeting details, including the advanced settings (only for meetings created through Element451).
* **Profile email**: Used to display the account that is currently signed in for Zoom integration.

---

# Connecting Zoom to Element451

1. Navigate to the **Engagement** > **Events** > **All** **Events**.
2. Create a new event and select the **Make it a Zoom** option.

3. After creating your event, navigate to **Event** **Overview** > **Event** **Date(s)**.
4. Click the **Login** **to** **Zoom** button.  
   ​

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/957866888/c3bfc351cc8077e4cee2e60b/Login+to+Zoom.png?expires=1784333700&signature=1d9f527947a5b2ac6b63159decfe8dfe915dfd66e0a88b902bca60962296825c&req=fSUgHs94lYlXFb4f3HP0gBTtae6A1ufc1SglWXyJ6swyllDW99j2V6qzUpHd%0Ang0%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/957866888/c3bfc351cc8077e4cee2e60b/Login+to+Zoom.png?expires=1784333700&signature=1d9f527947a5b2ac6b63159decfe8dfe915dfd66e0a88b902bca60962296825c&req=fSUgHs94lYlXFb4f3HP0gBTtae6A1ufc1SglWXyJ6swyllDW99j2V6qzUpHd%0Ang0%3D%0A)
5. The Zoom website will appear, where you will log in and allow Element451 permission to access your information. You'll need to check the box and click **Allow**.  
   ​

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1109177955/ed0ddb80edcede73e8a8ad0e/Note.png?expires=1784333700&signature=caa9997310d658d852a262946c40ce97fbff3e867f474345480d2a22540b463f&req=dSEnH8h5mohaXPMW1HO4zfAXobivcSzaNPQ1oXeh7e9dpDN%2Fj%2FnHaQVa1F2X%0AqFfa%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1109177955/ed0ddb80edcede73e8a8ad0e/Note.png?expires=1784333700&signature=caa9997310d658d852a262946c40ce97fbff3e867f474345480d2a22540b463f&req=dSEnH8h5mohaXPMW1HO4zfAXobivcSzaNPQ1oXeh7e9dpDN%2Fj%2FnHaQVa1F2X%0AqFfa%0A)

   *When attempting to integrate with Zoom, if you receive an error stating that Zoom is unable to install this app because it needs pre-approval by your account admin, [click here to visit our troubleshooting article to learn more](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8927495-zoom-integration-troubleshooting#h_0415cb71ae).*  
   ​

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/957867032/782e8dc26e3447d702af7f7f/Zoom+Permission.png?expires=1784333700&signature=d044cd6815344f219a8db7ae769fec7f271b9e3c3d6a1846b76828f988357c9d&req=fSUgHs95nYJdFb4f3HP0gGYryZA%2Bp%2BTI5%2Fg6cys4d2qr6m6KMnf9A4eyzUqe%0AtXk%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/957867032/782e8dc26e3447d702af7f7f/Zoom+Permission.png?expires=1784333700&signature=d044cd6815344f219a8db7ae769fec7f271b9e3c3d6a1846b76828f988357c9d&req=fSUgHs95nYJdFb4f3HP0gGYryZA%2Bp%2BTI5%2Fg6cys4d2qr6m6KMnf9A4eyzUqe%0AtXk%3D%0A)
6. Once authorized, you will be redirected back to Element451, and now you will see the **Zoom** **button** in the top corner has been updated and now displays your email address. (Note: That button can also be used to log out of Zoom should you want to disconnect the integration).  
   ​

7. You are now ready to start creating Zoom events in Element451! For more information on managing Zoom-hosted events, please see [How to use Zoom Integration in Events](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/5108154-how-to-use-the-zoom-integration-in-events).

When creating and updating Zoom links from Element451, it is important to know that Zoom has a rate limit of 100 API requests per day per Zoom account. Remember, you may use more than one request when adding a Zoom link to an event. Limits are reset daily at midnight UTC.

Learn how to apply a label to an individual user's profile.

# Overview

Labels are a way to tag people or segments of people for the following purposes:

* Easy sorting and organization
* At-a-glance identification
* Delivering customized content for communications, sites, and pages

---

# Apply a Label to a Profile

1. Go to the person's profile by navigating to **Contacts** > **People**.
2. If the person has already been tagged with one or more labels, the label chips will appear under the person's name at the top of their profile.
3. To add a new label:

   * To a profile that already has a label:

     + Click on an existing label chip to bring up a list of available tags.
   * To a profile that has no labels:

     + Click "**add label**" to bring up a list of available options.

Need to add a new label to your list? [Learn more here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6953481-labels-overview).

---

# Remove a Label from a Profile

1. Go to the person's profile by navigating to ***Contacts > People***.
2. Click on any of the label chips to open the label selector.
3. Click the **X** next to the label(s) you wish to remove from the profile.

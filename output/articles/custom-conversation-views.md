# Overview

Custom Views bring flexibility and personalization to your **Conversations Inbox**. These saved, filtered views allow you to organize and access conversations based on your specific needs. Unlike **System Views**, which are preset and cannot be edited, Custom Views let you define and save filters tailored to your unique workflows.

For more information on System Views and their functionality, see the [Conversations Inbox article](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8507376-conversations-inbox#h_2d28443ba5).

By saving frequently used filters, you can streamline navigation and reduce time spent searching for conversations. With options to customize access and visibility, you can ensure that the right views are available to the right people on your team.

## Key Features

* **Save Filters for Reuse**

  + Easily access frequently used filters by saving them as a view.
* **Custom Titles + Descriptions**

  + Titles and descriptions help you identify and select views.
* **Restrict Access**

  + Decide who can access and edit each view by assigning it to individuals or teams or leaving it public.

# Adding + Managing Custom Views

Custom Views allow you to save pre-configured filters for quick access and tailor your inbox to specific workflows. Where you can add or manage Custom Views depends on your permissions:

* **Admins (Administer Conversations Permission):**

  + ✅ Can add and manage Custom Views through **Conversation Settings** and the **Advanced Filter Sidesheet**.
* **Users (Access Conversations Permission):**

  + ✅ Can add and manage **self-created** Custom Views directly via the **Advanced Filter Sidesheet** (funnel icon).
  + ✅ Can manage Custom Views that are explicitly restricted to them and created by another user.
  + ❌ Cannot modify public views (views without restriction).
  + ❌ Cannot adjust **Restrict To** settings.

The following sections provide step-by-step instructions for adding, editing, and managing Custom Views using both methods:

## Method 1: Conversation Settings

### Adding a New Custom View

1. Navigate to **Engagement > Conversations > Settings**.
2. Under the **General** tab, scroll to the **Conversation Views** card (bottom of the page).

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274739532/803355d7187a4260bed1d105134f/Convo+Settings++Convo+Views.png?expires=1784333700&signature=e3c834afe064523abcb23e942bb3f8ac8790f228921600dcbeff84985035fab7&req=dSIgEs59lIRcW%2FMW1HO4zVY3%2FZzjPDA51VnKhYcuYZvdEenwU36fqD5TokpZ%0AxHdE%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274739532/803355d7187a4260bed1d105134f/Convo+Settings++Convo+Views.png?expires=1784333700&signature=e3c834afe064523abcb23e942bb3f8ac8790f228921600dcbeff84985035fab7&req=dSIgEs59lIRcW%2FMW1HO4zVY3%2FZzjPDA51VnKhYcuYZvdEenwU36fqD5TokpZ%0AxHdE%0A)
3. Click **+ Add Conversation View** (the New Conversation View sidesheet will open).
4. Configure the settings and filters (see [below](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8507376-conversations-inbox#h_b7ec40e5c7) for descriptions).
5. Click **Save** in the top right corner. The new view will become active immediately.

### Editing or Deleting Custom Views

1. Navigate to **Engagement > Conversations > Settings**.
2. Under the **General** tab, scroll to the **Conversation Views** card (bottom of the page).

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274740175/c12b7b826008caa8d8ec9732cc71/Convo+Settings++Convo+Views.png?expires=1784333700&signature=ab5ac028372420210d0e341f1427d14fc8f3b2e9d66e99dbd7eccb7ce9d143db&req=dSIgEs56nYBYXPMW1HO4zaA9qbKcvOmv6FP5hdyTdlDe1yUdeMK0OGqg646A%0ArCD%2B%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274740175/c12b7b826008caa8d8ec9732cc71/Convo+Settings++Convo+Views.png?expires=1784333700&signature=ab5ac028372420210d0e341f1427d14fc8f3b2e9d66e99dbd7eccb7ce9d143db&req=dSIgEs56nYBYXPMW1HO4zaA9qbKcvOmv6FP5hdyTdlDe1yUdeMK0OGqg646A%0ArCD%2B%0A)
3. Locate the view you wish to edit or delete.
4. Click the three vertical dots at the end of that view’s row.

   * Select **Edit** to open the sidesheet and make changes. Click **Update** when finished.
   * Select **Delete** to remove the view. You’ll be prompted to confirm the action, as it is permanent.

     [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274742343/0a3fd9bc62d8b8be91b99ce61418/Convo+Settings++Convo+Views.png?expires=1784333700&signature=31ea69954dc68675e5260d057a877e60969a701b564fe214b4e4105cd45eb456&req=dSIgEs56n4JbWvMW1HO4zcM0T9jcwbSyKcWWRjZKDg1eEBjVUqKwXeAKKltc%0Aqjgy%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274742343/0a3fd9bc62d8b8be91b99ce61418/Convo+Settings++Convo+Views.png?expires=1784333700&signature=31ea69954dc68675e5260d057a877e60969a701b564fe214b4e4105cd45eb456&req=dSIgEs56n4JbWvMW1HO4zcM0T9jcwbSyKcWWRjZKDg1eEBjVUqKwXeAKKltc%0Aqjgy%0A)

# Custom View Settings

The settings for Custom Views are consistent across both Conversation Settings and the Advanced Filter side sheet.

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274784414/3c6bd1253546646a324c9c0863eb/Create+New+Convo+View.png?expires=1784333700&signature=fd969dc75903334e7927adc8d8f73c9aa9824c4658926090e74faebc281293d0&req=dSIgEs52mYVeXfMW1HO4zciIoeC3biXOqnIFYh6fBPD9VK7hY%2F4CSUKl6lrM%0AxWVHtc%2BkkX%2F6%2F9qUx4c%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274784414/3c6bd1253546646a324c9c0863eb/Create+New+Convo+View.png?expires=1784333700&signature=fd969dc75903334e7927adc8d8f73c9aa9824c4658926090e74faebc281293d0&req=dSIgEs52mYVeXfMW1HO4zciIoeC3biXOqnIFYh6fBPD9VK7hY%2F4CSUKl6lrM%0AxWVHtc%2BkkX%2F6%2F9qUx4c%3D%0A)

Here is an explanation of each setting:

* **View Name**: Choose a short, descriptive name.
* **Description**: Add a concise description to help identify views from the inbox. The description appears directly under the name in the view dropdown menu.

  [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274759323/866b96160774962a7535961326fa/Custom+Views+-+Dropdown+Menu.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f52239c6a4fb05dbf014e4c645cdbc3a196499bafc6beed436bb50acbc56be0b&req=dSIgEs57lIJdWvMW1HO4zbu5AcWRJejU17I88gSFQ9evga%2Fkw3K9SQaA4MPM%0APl6m%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274759323/866b96160774962a7535961326fa/Custom+Views+-+Dropdown+Menu.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f52239c6a4fb05dbf014e4c645cdbc3a196499bafc6beed436bb50acbc56be0b&req=dSIgEs57lIJdWvMW1HO4zbu5AcWRJejU17I88gSFQ9evga%2Fkw3K9SQaA4MPM%0APl6m%0A)
* **Restrict To**: Assign specific users, teams, or both. Leave blank to make the view public (only admins can edit public views).
* **Filters**: Select the filters you want the view to apply.

For a detailed list of available filters, see the [Filters section](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8507376-conversations-inbox#h_2d28443ba5) of the **Conversations Inbox article**.

# Applying a Custom View

**To Apply a Custom View:**

1. Navigate to your inbox: **Engagement** > **Conversations** > **All** **Conversations**.
2. Click the “**Select** **View**” dropdown at the top of the inbox.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274762199/cf9002a9ce459d2a3911e59f042f/Select+View.png?expires=1784333700&signature=0669a7113f883f76167b38052c3507e529432f70df49ba52a90703d0292a468c&req=dSIgEs54n4BWUPMW1HO4zTbKQVxL4DqP%2FmB0plisdaVHCTi84yjeTDTxxutq%0A76Vg%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274762199/cf9002a9ce459d2a3911e59f042f/Select+View.png?expires=1784333700&signature=0669a7113f883f76167b38052c3507e529432f70df49ba52a90703d0292a468c&req=dSIgEs54n4BWUPMW1HO4zTbKQVxL4DqP%2FmB0plisdaVHCTi84yjeTDTxxutq%0A76Vg%0A)
3. From the list, select the custom view you wish to view. The conversation inbox will dynamically update based on the filters for that view.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274763021/dbd3ed636f6d2c0499cf46a66413/Select+Custom+View.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f1834ad04a5fcfffc63ddcb71ffaee161c682bcd1fef381113a8779f16eac4e1&req=dSIgEs54noFdWPMW1HO4zcYhJhx40kHOq95lvMG8kf6LFtuUhcDypeFcIIla%0Azpjx%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1274763021/dbd3ed636f6d2c0499cf46a66413/Select+Custom+View.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f1834ad04a5fcfffc63ddcb71ffaee161c682bcd1fef381113a8779f16eac4e1&req=dSIgEs54noFdWPMW1HO4zcYhJhx40kHOq95lvMG8kf6LFtuUhcDypeFcIIla%0Azpjx%0A)

**Important Note for Users with “Access Conversations” Permission**

* Users with this permission can add, edit, and delete Custom Views they’ve created.
* They can only edit Custom Views if explicitly included in the **“Restrict To”** setting or if the view is self-created.
* They cannot modify public Custom Views (no restriction set).

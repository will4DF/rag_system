# Content Blocks

#### Billboard with CTA: Why do my images look "cut off" on mobile and tablet?

When your images appear "cut off" on smaller screens, this is actually the system working as intended. Our responsive design uses "cover" functionality to ensure images fill their container completely while maintaining their aspect ratio (width-to-height proportion) to prevent distortion. On smaller screens, this means the sides of wider images may be cropped to maintain focus on the center.

#### Billboard with CTA: What aspect ratios work best for different devices?

While there are no exact pixel specifications due to the variety of devices available, these general aspect ratio guidelines work well:

* **Desktop:** Wider images (approximately 4:3 ratio)
* **Tablet:** More balanced images (approximately 1:1 ratio)
* **Mobile:** Taller images (approximately 3:4 ratio)

Learn how to seamlessly create and integrate Supplemental Forms into your application process.

# Overview

Element451 offers flexibility in collecting additional information from applicants. Whether verifying residency, gathering enrollment agreements, or collecting program-specific details, **Supplemental Forms** are your go-to feature. These forms provide an excellent opportunity to gather program-specific information that may not be captured in your standard application process.

**Important Notes:**

* It is possible to add one Supplemental Form to multiple applications. However, each form will be treated independently. If a form is attached to multiple applications, applicants must complete the same form for each application they submit.
* Supplemental Forms are designed to pre-populate fields with existing data whenever possible to make the application process as efficient as possible. This reduces the time applicants spend filling out forms and improves the accuracy of the information collected.
* For Supplemental Forms to appear to applicants, two steps are required:

  1. Ensure the checklist card is added to the application's dashboard
  2. Add a supplemental form checklist item in Decision Settings

  Once these steps are completed, the form will automatically appear on the applicant's checklist.
* Supplemental Forms appear in the application dashboard/portal's menu **after** the applicant has submitted their application.

* All supplemental forms must be initiated and submitted through the Element451 application to ensure proper tracking and association with the applicant's profile.
* It is not possible to send a supplemental form directly to an individual or share a standalone link for completion outside the application.

# Creating and Managing Supplemental Forms

## Creating a Supplemental Form

1. Navigate to **Applications** > **Applications** > **Supplemental** **Forms**.
2. Click the blue/white **plus** **sign** (+) in the bottom right corner.
3. Replace '**untitled**' at the top of the side sheet with a name for your Supplemental Form. This name is for internal reference only.
4. If you wish for a PDF to be generated for every form submission, use the toggle to activate that feature. By default, it will be disabled. This feature can be beneficial for record-keeping and ensuring all necessary documentation is easily accessible.
5. Click the **Add** **Field** button to select the fields and field groups you'd like to add to the Form. These are the questions and information you need from your applicants.
6. When you're finished, click **Create** in the top right corner.
7. Once the Supplemental Form has been created, you can [add it to an existing Application](#h_af7d960503).

## Editing + Deleting Supplemental Forms

1. Navigate to **Applications** > **Applications** > **Supplemental** **Forms**.

   * To **edit**, click the **pencil** **icon** to the right of the Supplemental Form you wish to edit. Edit as needed. Your changes will be saved automatically.
   * To **delete**, click the **trash can** icon to the right of the Supplemental Form you wish to delete. You will be prompted to confirm the action.

## Cloning a Supplemental Form

If you need a new form that's similar to an existing one, you can duplicate it instead of building it from scratch. This is especially helpful when you maintain many forms that share most of the same fields, such as department-specific intake forms or residency verifications with minor variations.

1. Navigate to **Applications** > **Applications** > **Supplemental** **Forms**.
2. Locate the Supplemental Form you want to copy and select **Duplicate** from its actions (alongside **Edit** and **Delete**).
3. A copy of the form is created, including its fields and settings.
4. Open the copy with the **pencil** **icon** to rename it and adjust any fields. Your changes will be saved automatically.

📙 **Note:** Duplicating a form creates an independent copy. It is not automatically attached to any Application, so you'll need to add it to an Application the same way you would any Supplemental Form.

# Adding Supplemental Forms to Applications

Once your Form has been created, you can add it to an Application. Applications can support multiple Supplemental Forms, depending on your needs.

1. Navigate to **Applications** > **Applications** > **All** **Applications**.
2. Locate and click on the **Application name** to which you want to add the Supplemental Form.
3. From the Application editor, locate and click on **Supplemental** **Forms** in the lefthand menu under *Content*.
4. When you add Supplemental Forms to an Application, they are added in the Form of Steps. To add a form, click the **Add** **Step** button.
5. Configure **General** Settings:

   * **Name**: The name added when creating the Supplemental Form is for internal reference. However, the name you add here will be visible to the applicant on the Application.
   * **Description**: The description appears at the top of the Supplemental Form for applicants to see before completing.
   * **Supplemental** **Form**: Select the Supplemental Form from the drop-down menu you wish to add to the Application. Click Add in the top right corner to attach the supplemental Form to the Application.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/992903330/8967d0a849c20331061bbec9/Supplemental+Forms+-+Title+and+Description.png?expires=1784333700&signature=e963f34c94a46a321629d1f2d7bc6f531f5cf2ab5bcecfbae57c8e1b38223802&req=fSklH8l9noJfFb4f3HP0gEo9H3ZkRGWW8l%2FTDpdD%2F5Oggyt9FeypU6jR0U%2BC%0A%2FbY%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/992903330/8967d0a849c20331061bbec9/Supplemental+Forms+-+Title+and+Description.png?expires=1784333700&signature=e963f34c94a46a321629d1f2d7bc6f531f5cf2ab5bcecfbae57c8e1b38223802&req=fSklH8l9noJfFb4f3HP0gEo9H3ZkRGWW8l%2FTDpdD%2F5Oggyt9FeypU6jR0U%2BC%0A%2FbY%3D%0A)
6. Add **Conditional** **Logic** if you wish to display the Form to certain applicants. For example, international students may have different residency requirement forms than domestic students, or some health science programs require additional forms. Using this feature, you can set the logic of the forms accordingly.
7. **Custom** **Sidebar**: The custom sidebar provides the same functionality here as it does in your Application—it displays help text within the *Tips and Info* button shown in the top right corner of the dashboard header.

   * The sidebar has a main header (**Sidebar Title)**, and then there can be **Paragraphs** with separate headings under it. This section can be used for various things but was designed to provide quick access to helpful information the applicant may be looking for.

     + To edit the **Sidebar** **Title**, click the pencil icon.
     + To add a **Paragraph**, click + Add Paragraph. Then, use the editor to add a **Paragraph** **Title** and **Body** **Text**. If you choose, you can add multiple paragraphs.

# Next Steps

After creating your Supplemental Form and adding it to an Application, it will automatically appear in the applicant's portal menu after they submit their application (assuming they meet any conditions you may have added).

However, if you want the form to also appear as a checklist item (which can help applicants track their progress and understand what's still needed), you'll need to complete these two additional steps:

## Making Your Supplemental Form a Checklist Item (Optional)

1. Create a **Supplemental Form** "Checklist Item" in Decision Settings. You can find the step-by-step process [here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9210688-decisions-checklists#h_01411c343e).
2. Add the Checklist Card to the Application Dashboard if not already added. You can find the step-by-step process [here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9210688-decisions-checklists#h_251e02cdb3).

It's also essential you understand how the Supplemental Form checklist item works. For example, only one supplemental form checklist item should be added per application, regardless of the number of forms required. You can read about that [here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9210688-decisions-checklists#h_0218dafdac).

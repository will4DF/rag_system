# Overview

The Appointments module in Element451 makes it easy to schedule one-on-one meetings between students and staff — whether with a specific person or through a team, where the system automatically assigns an available team member. This covers advising sessions, admissions interviews, financial aid support, and more through a self-service booking system.

This article provides a high-level overview of how Appointments works, plus links to role-specific guides for setup and configuration.

## Key Features

* Branded Appointments Site
* Appointment Categories + Types
* Personalized Booking Links
* Staff Availability Management
* Team-Based Scheduling with Automatic Assignment
* Connect External Calendar via ICS URL or Export
* Automated Confirmation + Reminder Messages
* Mobile Access via StudentHub

## Accessing Appointments

The Appointments module provides a centralized location to view and manage your appointments. To access Appointments, navigate to **Engagement** > **Appointments**. We go into more details on the Appointments screen at the end of this article.

### 💡 Did You Know?

You can create a **Bolt Agent Job** right from the Appointments module to support your outreach or follow-up. Just click the "**Create Bolt Agent Job**" button at the top of the page to get started. Each job is powered by a best-practice template that you can personalize before launching.

# How It Works

Here’s what the Appointments workflow looks like:

1. Admins configure the Appointments Site, categories, types, and optionally configure teams for [team-based scheduling](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/13921320-team-appointments)
2. Internal users (anyone offering appointments) set their availability and booking preferences.
3. Students or guests visit the site, select a person or team, choose an appointment type, and schedule a time.
4. The system sends confirmation and reminder messages to both parties.

# For Admins: Initial Setup

Admins are responsible for setting up the Appointments system. This includes:

* Configuring the **Appointments Site** (branding, domain, thank-you message)
* Creating **Categories** to organize offerings (must create at least one category)
* Creating **Appointment Types** to define meeting formats and registration forms (must create at least one type)
* Configure teams for appointment scheduling (optional) — set up teams in **Team** **Settings** so departments or groups can share availability and let the system assign appointments automatically
* Add your availability for themselves, other internal users, and teams

# What Students See

On the Appointments Site, students and guests can:

* Browse available appointments organized under **People** (individual internal users) and **Teams** (groups of internal users)
* Filter available appointments by category, type, location, and duration
* View real-time availability
* Book times with immediate confirmation
* Reschedule or cancel appointments

When booking with an individual, the student selects a specific staff member and chooses from their available times. When booking with a team, the student selects a time slot and the system automatically assigns an available team member based on your assignment setting.

[StudentHub](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9827408-getting-started-with-studenthub) mobile app users can manage appointments from their phones.

# Appointments Screen Overview

When you access the Appointments module, you'll be taken to the Appointment screen. Here, you can find a centralized location to view and manage your appointments in an easy-to-use table format. From here, you can also adjust your settings, access your booking site link, and schedule new appointments. Below, we break down the screen's anatomy.

To learn more about a specific highlighted area, expand its corresponding numbered section below.

### 1 - Filter Menu

* The filter menu lets you quickly customize the appointments you want to view.
* ​**All appointments**: View all appointments, regardless of the assignee.
* ​**Your appointments:** View only appointments where you are the assignee.
* Drill down your view further and use the date filter to view appointments scheduled for **today**, **tomorrow**, **this** **week**, or **this** **month**.
* To remove the date filter, click *clear date filters*.
* For advanced filtering, click the filter icon on the far right side of the screen.

### 2 - Count + Total

* **Total**: The total count of all appointments, regardless of status.
* **Overdue**: The count of all appointments with a start time in the past and the status is scheduled. The text color for the appointment's date/start time will be red.
* **Today**: The count of appointments that are scheduled for today.
* **Upcoming**: The count of appointments that are scheduled for a future date.

### 3 - View + Manage (Edit, Reschedule, Cancel)

* A list of appointments is displayed, showing pertinent information at a glance.
* To edit or delete an appointment, click the more button (⋮) at the end of the respective row.

  + **Edit**: Reschedule, edit information such as the invitee, assignee, meeting type, and status, and add notes.
  + **Delete**: Cancel the appointment.

✨ **Pro Tip:** Add your pre-meeting and post-meeting notes to the appointment to reference at a later date and for seamless cross-departmental communication.

### 4 - Booking Site, Export, Schedule

* **Visit** **URL**: Opens your booking site, allowing you to copy and share the URL.
* **Schedule** **Appointment**: Manually schedule an appointment.
* **More** **(⋮)**: Configure your availability and settings /admins can access page, type, and category settings.

The Login content is displayed on the page where the student must log in before they can access the Microsite. The login page is designed to be inclusive and generic, catering to all audiences. As the user has yet to log in, limited customization options are available. However, our configurations allow you to make your page branded, visually appealing, and engaging.

## Login: Header

Before you start adding content to your login page, we recommend setting your heading first.

1. Click on **Login** on the left menu

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879597197/d4df0c40acc76e50d6fd7a20/Microsites+-+Content+-+Login.png?expires=1784333700&signature=71740d3bc280494306b6416160fd31959bb288bf41b91decef30a2d7a4884202&req=fCcuE8B5nIhYFb4f3HP0gE1JAC9yR6wiyJIbpPHtSEFfQEUShg0jLTiXKWyN%0AVp8%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879597197/d4df0c40acc76e50d6fd7a20/Microsites+-+Content+-+Login.png?expires=1784333700&signature=71740d3bc280494306b6416160fd31959bb288bf41b91decef30a2d7a4884202&req=fCcuE8B5nIhYFb4f3HP0gE1JAC9yR6wiyJIbpPHtSEFfQEUShg0jLTiXKWyN%0AVp8%3D%0A)
2. You will see a **Body** and **Header** tab at the top of the Login section. Click on the **Header** tab.
3. Under content, you will only see **Default** **Header** **Content**; there is no option to add versions for different audiences. This is because the user still needs to log in, and we can only display the default header to them. This is also the reason why the only token you will be able to use is your institution's name.
4. To configure the heading, click the **pencil icon** next to the **Default Header Content**.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879604185/fbf1aac8cb2991909d003518/Microsites+-+Login+-+Header.png?expires=1784333700&signature=2f361615b8d4e2c97ae7e84b8377c3c1ea22cdb1e594e29c28bd70e5756ce19e&req=fCcuEMl6nIlaFb4f3HP0gMu6iaWr3MdeH8Gr7UnnLXB6oLnUIqNv3STlC9zd%0Aygw%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879604185/fbf1aac8cb2991909d003518/Microsites+-+Login+-+Header.png?expires=1784333700&signature=2f361615b8d4e2c97ae7e84b8377c3c1ea22cdb1e594e29c28bd70e5756ce19e&req=fCcuEMl6nIlaFb4f3HP0gMu6iaWr3MdeH8Gr7UnnLXB6oLnUIqNv3STlC9zd%0Aygw%3D%0A)

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879592378/892fc13b861a047b4f285558/Microsites+-+heading+and+subtitle.png?expires=1784333700&signature=9936744f7b5214726eb587275c7b53ceb761a9b786bab60e81511603ce3840ef&req=fCcuE8B8noZXFb4f3HP0gGPFrAE%2BqZ2ZYPQkZD8NVdtKqAKD8s93eCrrUFZa%0AT9k%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879592378/892fc13b861a047b4f285558/Microsites+-+heading+and+subtitle.png?expires=1784333700&signature=9936744f7b5214726eb587275c7b53ceb761a9b786bab60e81511603ce3840ef&req=fCcuE8B8noZXFb4f3HP0gGPFrAE%2BqZ2ZYPQkZD8NVdtKqAKD8s93eCrrUFZa%0AT9k%3D%0A)
5. Adjust the Heading configurations as needed:

   * Headline text: Appears large over your background image
   * Subtitle text: Appears smaller under your headline text
   * Header background image (must be in .jpg format)

2. Click **Add** **Block**

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/882582644/f7ea67e91d2ace5fac32d79e/Microsites+-+Add+Block+Button.png?expires=1784333700&signature=8c5471c78eaf4be7ad46adfcab51f16a1c25473206ff78e23dba118454e8615f&req=fCglE8F8m4VbFb4f3HP0gGDRmj1IAdr80IB0ZahFDIJQkl%2BJPbQ%2FmO3N18ww%0AHfc%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/882582644/f7ea67e91d2ace5fac32d79e/Microsites+-+Add+Block+Button.png?expires=1784333700&signature=8c5471c78eaf4be7ad46adfcab51f16a1c25473206ff78e23dba118454e8615f&req=fCglE8F8m4VbFb4f3HP0gGDRmj1IAdr80IB0ZahFDIJQkl%2BJPbQ%2FmO3N18ww%0AHfc%3D%0A)

   * When adding a block, most will have a **Content** and **Design** tab. The Content tab will have configurations specific to that content block, and the Design tab will allow you to select an icon and adjust coloring.
5. Preview your content block by clicking the **eye** icon

# Dashboard

### View an example of a Dashboard

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/882527033/bcd18f65429affaaab9e92ee/Microsites+-+Dashboard+Page+2.png?expires=1784333700&signature=37bb55fa0afdabbdbdfaf67725f73eaea1c8346edbc5b1768ee2ec983d3c7acc&req=fCglE8t5nYJcFb4f3HP0gBazMBbqOul45WnShJhoWvN%2F7cjQu12ef%2Byiey4i%0Ab5b268NQ%2B9shTKfpiA%3D%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/882527033/bcd18f65429affaaab9e92ee/Microsites+-+Dashboard+Page+2.png?expires=1784333700&signature=37bb55fa0afdabbdbdfaf67725f73eaea1c8346edbc5b1768ee2ec983d3c7acc&req=fCglE8t5nYJcFb4f3HP0gBazMBbqOul45WnShJhoWvN%2F7cjQu12ef%2Byiey4i%0Ab5b268NQ%2B9shTKfpiA%3D%3D%0A)

The dashboard is students' personalized home page after logging into the Microsite. It displays relevant content in a visually engaging card format.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1013093851/d79d2e19f9c65407758d2de8/Important+-+Orng.png?expires=1784430000&signature=ddbb5aa7d2825255d47b8b7e27e115013fa673c77ddb6fbe918ff7da6c002334&req=dSAmFcl3nolaWPMW3Hu4gd%2F9UVyTT1HKGGQQ0olFug9D%2F2iI2LUtOp%2F5D%2FWx%0A4g%3D%3D%0A) Each content block or card can be configured to display for all students or targeted to specific audiences using labels. This feature allows you to create customized experiences based on interests, demographics, funnel stage, and more. **The logic operates as an ‘and’ statement, meaning if multiple labels are assigned to a content block, a person must have all of the assigned labels to view that block.**

Unlike blocks, **sections** use 'or' logic, so a person only needs one matching label to see a section.

## Dashboard: Header

Before adding content to your login page, we recommend configuring your heading first.

1. Click on **Dashboard** on the left menu.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879628492/e30f0672ab4468fc4b683fb4/Microsites+-+Content+-+Dashboard.png?expires=1784333700&signature=7b4fb7e220e1cb3fe6e787028bcea11a30bc6b6e5e4b1f07d8e7c781d2feb71b&req=fCcuEMt2mYhdFb4f3HP0gIi3i828tmLXrYpIQahQEKYOLKO9%2BPpnIgtrjLG9%0As%2Fg%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879628492/e30f0672ab4468fc4b683fb4/Microsites+-+Content+-+Dashboard.png?expires=1784333700&signature=7b4fb7e220e1cb3fe6e787028bcea11a30bc6b6e5e4b1f07d8e7c781d2feb71b&req=fCcuEMt2mYhdFb4f3HP0gIi3i828tmLXrYpIQahQEKYOLKO9%2BPpnIgtrjLG9%0As%2Fg%3D%0A)
2. You will see a **Body** and **Header** tab at the top of the Dashboard section.
3. Click on the **Header** tab. You will see the **default** **header** **content**.

   * **Default**: The default content will appear for users without matching labels.
   * **Add Version**: You can add header **versions** to customize the header for different audiences. Versions are displayed based on user labels.

     + If multiple labels are applied, they function as ‘or’—users must match at least one label for the header to appear.
     + Avoid reusing the same label across multiple versions.
4. Click the **pencil** **icon** to edit the headline, subtitle, and header background image.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879845210/ff420dc1bd5b82eb7bcdac83/Microsites+-+Dashboard+-+Header.png?expires=1784333700&signature=9e680f7ee1cd29b93a8765314967695deee617d85a3fbc97ce79805b57ebd9f1&req=fCcuHs17n4BfFb4f3HP0gH10ShfS70rTGSwYPKpl7ZnHLE8H0tWfmmp1mlcf%0AT3A%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879845210/ff420dc1bd5b82eb7bcdac83/Microsites+-+Dashboard+-+Header.png?expires=1784333700&signature=9e680f7ee1cd29b93a8765314967695deee617d85a3fbc97ce79805b57ebd9f1&req=fCcuHs17n4BfFb4f3HP0gH10ShfS70rTGSwYPKpl7ZnHLE8H0tWfmmp1mlcf%0AT3A%3D%0A)
5. To customize the header for different audiences/labels, click **Add** **Version**. People who match the label will see this version rather than the default. When adding a version, configure the following:

   * **Enter Labels**: Add labels for the user groups who should see this version of the header
   * **Headline Text**: Add your headline text
   * **Subtitle Text**: Add your subtitle text
   * **Header** **Background** **Image**: Choose a header background image to upload. An image is required.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879846700/518dfbbec1a5e4a1056698e0/Microsite+-+dashboard+-+header+-+add+version.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f24720b758b956350219d38c9c5c9e5fc0871999748914549fe84d0f7059f58e&req=fCcuHs14moFfFb4f3HP0gNmzCtBa0pfBH72JQ2TWwt64Y8gamIEQ0xN6d5Qt%0AXVM%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879846700/518dfbbec1a5e4a1056698e0/Microsite+-+dashboard+-+header+-+add+version.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f24720b758b956350219d38c9c5c9e5fc0871999748914549fe84d0f7059f58e&req=fCcuHs14moFfFb4f3HP0gNmzCtBa0pfBH72JQ2TWwt64Y8gamIEQ0xN6d5Qt%0AXVM%3D%0A)

2. Click **Add** **Block**

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879595405/290e3ca87353b7d766873ddc/Microsites+-+Add+Block.png?expires=1784333700&signature=d25fac4f83f1698836440fed8410de4622a8b7942e6405f61acc215538e6dbec&req=fCcuE8B7mYFaFb4f3HP0gI2ZCP5vjksQvqm6HgGCexDf8PxmaEc9aDFaWWRZ%0ANPw%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879595405/290e3ca87353b7d766873ddc/Microsites+-+Add+Block.png?expires=1784333700&signature=d25fac4f83f1698836440fed8410de4622a8b7942e6405f61acc215538e6dbec&req=fCcuE8B7mYFaFb4f3HP0gI2ZCP5vjksQvqm6HgGCexDf8PxmaEc9aDFaWWRZ%0ANPw%3D%0A)

# Surveys

The survey feature lets you quickly gather feedback and insights directly from admitted students via a Microsite. Surveys are a simple way to collect additional information from admitted students, which can be used to guide recruitment strategy and personalize support throughout the enrollment funnel. Survey components include:

* Multiple-choice, yes/no, and open-ended text questions
* Surveys are only visible to students after logging into their Microsite
* View real-time results and respondents
* Export survey data to CSV for further analysis

## Surveys: Adding Content

1. To add a survey, click **+ Add Survey**.

   * **Survey Name**: Give your survey a name. The title will be used as the header text on the survey card.
   * **Start date/end date**: If you want your survey to run for a specific length of time, give your survey a start and end date. If you want it to remain open, leave those fields blank.
   * **Activate**: Toggle on **Activate** if you want to begin accepting survey responses
2. Click **Create**
3. Once created, you can add questions to your survey. You can add the following question types: multiple choice, yes/no, and text input.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879859060/f974413aa6f803d6b859573d/Microsites+-+Surveys+-+Add+Question.png?expires=1784333700&signature=fbdc9c59001cc75234a1db61126e831b5d7bbc62c590d6eb066b7e8b905ca095&req=fCcuHsx3nYdfFb4f3HP0gHcr8S6VE4leFIc5X5Nc24nbz%2FP6ZLbqHqJcUoq3%0ApIM%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/879859060/f974413aa6f803d6b859573d/Microsites+-+Surveys+-+Add+Question.png?expires=1784333700&signature=fbdc9c59001cc75234a1db61126e831b5d7bbc62c590d6eb066b7e8b905ca095&req=fCcuHsx3nYdfFb4f3HP0gHcr8S6VE4leFIc5X5Nc24nbz%2FP6ZLbqHqJcUoq3%0ApIM%3D%0A)

You can return to the Survey section of the Microsite to:

* Edit survey (pencil icons)
* Review survey results/respondents (tabs)
* Export results (paper with down arrow icon)

# Sections

Under the Content section (login, dashboard, surveys), in the left menu, you will see **Sections**.

As you learned in [Part 1](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8576402-microsites-part-1-getting-started), content sections are designed to hold large amounts of content and provide an interactive experience. Sections create a supplemental page within the Microsite and are linked in the header and footer. In addition to the page, depending on the content type, the section may create a content block (or card).

## Sections: Adding Content Sections

1. Click the **+ Add Section** button

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/882637114/da2744e70e44d42e265f4421/Microsites+-+Add+Content+Section.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f80a3f6a88ed0a16d6e39b0b01dc2172a8995079a9395ecde3f7185301f6b1ce&req=fCglEMp5nIBbFb4f3HP0gFUfH1KMZ5J%2FzpBH4uk3iWSor55IHwrK12jlTO5B%0AV84%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/882637114/da2744e70e44d42e265f4421/Microsites+-+Add+Content+Section.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f80a3f6a88ed0a16d6e39b0b01dc2172a8995079a9395ecde3f7185301f6b1ce&req=fCglEMp5nIBbFb4f3HP0gFUfH1KMZ5J%2FzpBH4uk3iWSor55IHwrK12jlTO5B%0AV84%3D%0A)
2. Select the section you wish to add

   * There are eight different types of content sections you can add to the Dashboard: **Academic, Acceptance, Career, Checklist, Documents, General, and Location Explorer**. Review [Part 3](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8576832-microsites-part-3-content-block-and-section-descriptions) of this series, Content Block and Section Descriptions, for more information, including a description of each section.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/882638922/ff86c5a5b87a7289bc7fa4cd/Microsites+-+Add+Section+Types.png?expires=1784333700&signature=b206674ed22473bd567d91b5634c3705391dabb01dd1373716a6511f10cc1592&req=fCglEMp2lINdFb4f3HP0gOzxM%2FJyMdWDBki38XIAofHERnMKLTH92fqcuYKN%0AQbU%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/882638922/ff86c5a5b87a7289bc7fa4cd/Microsites+-+Add+Section+Types.png?expires=1784333700&signature=b206674ed22473bd567d91b5634c3705391dabb01dd1373716a6511f10cc1592&req=fCglEMp2lINdFb4f3HP0gOzxM%2FJyMdWDBki38XIAofHERnMKLTH92fqcuYKN%0AQbU%3D%0A)

For example, if you use the Checklist type, you'll be required to add a block for each "to-do item."
5. After you've completed the body tab, you can navigate to the header tab. This contains your default header content to edit and the ability to add versions for different audiences.

   * Each section can be configured to display for all students or targeted to specific audiences using labels. This feature allows you to organize content based on interests, demographics, funnel stage, and more.

# Overview

Element451 offers two powerful AI-driven tools to enhance student engagement and support: Bolt Discovery and Bolt Agents. While both leverage advanced AI technology, they serve different purposes within the higher education ecosystem.

This article offers a comparison to help you understand when and how to use each tool effectively.

# What are the purposes of each?

**Bolt Discovery**: Bolt Discovery, Element451's generative search tool, is designed to provide an alternative to traditional website search tools. It leverages your Element451 Bolt Knowledge Hub to offer quick, accurate information to students, parents, alumni, and visitors, transforming the search experience into an interactive, AI-powered journey.

**Bolt Agents**: Bolt Agents are personalized digital team members designed to augment your internal staff by having 1:1 conversations with students, providing tailored support and guidance using the sources in your Element451 Bolt Knowledge Hub.

# What are the primary use cases for each?

**Bolt Discovery**: Provides immediate access to information about programs, admissions processes, campus life, or any other aspect of the institution stored in the Element451 Bolt Knowledge Hub. It delivers relevant information from your centralized repository, effectively replacing and improving traditional search functions without altering the institution's website structure.

**Bolt Agents**: Perfect for more complex, nuanced interactions where students need personalized advice or support. Agents can guide students through specific processes, answer individual questions, and provide tailored recommendations.

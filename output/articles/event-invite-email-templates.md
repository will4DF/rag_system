# Event Invite Email Templates Use and Installation

Installing email templates is easy and can be repeated as many times as you’d like. ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1084683147/f251810d4265fb72b9793f9e/Note-Orng.png?expires=1784430000&signature=09f46c29aa9c78e97068ccab1e349b25b3086c647fbf93421db04021d95aa204&req=dSAvEs92noBbXvMW3Hu4gRybzRPsCq2ZzffkYl5DyujL2A1hlgOAhA6hERmu%0AAw%3D%3D%0A) Upon installation, the templates will be branded to your institution. You can customize the style and content of the email template as you see fit.

You can use these for events on the Element platform or for an event you may be hosting externally, such as on your institutional website. For events on the Element platform, you can use event tokens to import fields from your existing events into these emails. [Learn more about using tokens](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1524113-tokens).

These templates can be used for one-time or ongoing communications campaigns.

1. Navigate to the Packs library at **Data + Automations** > **Packs** > **Library**.
2. Select the event invite email template use case:

   * **Custom illustration/pattern**: Simple, includes a calendar illustration or a background pattern depending on the style you select.
   * **No image needed**: Straightforward templates you can use for events where you may not have an image to include.
   * **Includes campus photo**: These have a space for an image. They are great for events where you have a photo to include.

   For each of these, you can choose a design style (commons, quad, or library) and (light or dark).
3. Once installed, the email template is now available under **Engagement** > **Campaigns** > **Templates**.

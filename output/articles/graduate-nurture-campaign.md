The Graduate Nurture Campaign is a communications campaign targeted to prospective graduate students, encouraging them to apply to your institution.

This Pack can be used by any type of institution with graduate programs.

## Pack Install Options

* **Application:** Specifies the application type and term.
* **Audience Segment:** Specifies who will receive these messages.
* **Configure Communications**

  + **Sender email address and name**: configures the reply-to email address and name of the sender in messages that are sent.
  + **SMS phone number and sender name**: similarly configures the phone number and name referenced in text messages that go out. *(e.g., "This is Chris from Element University...")*
  + **Contact info**: Populates the footer fields in the associated emails.
* **Configure Workflow**

  + **Communications frequency:** sets the delay between messages (weekly by default).

## Email Components

The emails included with this campaign include suggested pre-written content to address topics of interest to graduate students, such as financial aid opportunities, the return on investment of an advanced degree, and the campus community. Each message encourages the recipient to apply to your institution, with further personalization depending on whether the recipient is an international student or not.

## Workflow Components

The installed workflow is available under **Data + Automations** > **Workflows** > **All Workflows**.  
​

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/656642662/b87fa5d7e29dc113f073e515/grad_workflow.gif?expires=1784333700&signature=9640dc89f428d2dd2f53ce74cbfc77cf7aaf830245e05c09ef128e1fe90b8ce0&req=ciUhEM18m4ddFb4f3HP0gBwb7V4GimqRunFZmHJf0wHUvpxUR0hFBxQqxAqq%0AOqncPAcfyErfcG86ow%3D%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/656642662/b87fa5d7e29dc113f073e515/grad_workflow.gif?expires=1784333700&signature=9640dc89f428d2dd2f53ce74cbfc77cf7aaf830245e05c09ef128e1fe90b8ce0&req=ciUhEM18m4ddFb4f3HP0gBwb7V4GimqRunFZmHJf0wHUvpxUR0hFBxQqxAqq%0AOqncPAcfyErfcG86ow%3D%3D%0A)

The workflow sends a series of emails addressing various topics about academics, alumni, and financial aid. Each message encourages recipients to click the CTA button included in the email to start an application. Once a user starts an application, they are removed from the workflow. You can send users who have started an application but have not completed or submitted it reminders using our [Application Completion](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6887547-application-completion-push) and [Application Submit Push](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6887554-application-submit-push) Packs.

Make sure to review every message in the campaign and the message personalization options for each email. [Learn more about dynamic content and personalization](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1513684-dynamic-content-personalization).

Before activating the campaign, check the delay in between steps to confirm the campaign will run at your desired message frequency.

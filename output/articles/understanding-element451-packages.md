# Overview + How Team Packages Work

All customers will onboard using the new team-based package structure launched in May 2025. This model reflects Element451’s **AI Workforce** philosophy, the belief that the future of work lies in humans and AI agents working together to achieve real outcomes.

This model combines a foundational platform, Element Core, with modular, outcome-based Teams. You can add one or multiple Teams, customizing your AI workforce to fit your outcomes and institutional needs.

## Highlights

* Every customer must purchase **Element Core** and at least one (1) team.

  + **Element Core** is the foundational platform that powers the system. It includes essential features and enables the use of AI agents.
  + **Teams** are functional packages focused on specific outcomes: Marketing, Engagement, Admissions, and Student Success.

    - Teams can be purchased individually or in combination.
    - **Element One** is a discounted bundle that includes all teams for full platform access. Element One is a bundle, not a standalone package. Refer to each team for specific features.
* Messaging (SMS, MMS, WhatsApp, voice) remains usage-based and draws from annual contract-provided credits.
* Existing customers will be automatically mapped to the new structure.

  + Mapping is explained in the Legacy Packages section below.
  + Current terms/pricing remain in place until renewal, when the contract will transition to the new pricing model.

# Legacy Packages

Before May 2025, packages included **Discover**, **Connect**, **Ignite**, and **Engage**. These legacy plans bundled features differently from the new team-based structure described above.

## Guidance for Legacy Customers

If you were contracted before May 2025 and were on the Discover, Connect, Ignite, or Engage plan, here's what you need to know:

* Your existing features and access will **not** change.
* You will automatically be migrated to the new team-based structure with the equivalent or, in most cases, more features than you originally had.
* Your current contract terms and pricing (including any support upgrades and add-on features, such as Snowflake) will remain in effect until your renewal.
* At your next contract renewal, you will transition to the new pricing model aligned with your team selections.

If you have questions about your package or would like to explore adding additional teams to your new mapped package, reach out to your Account Manager.

# What Package Do I Have?

You can find your package listed in General Settings. Navigate to **Settings > General Settings > Billing.** Locate the "Plan" card, and your package will be listed there.

# Joining an Existing Email Conversation

*SMS conversation threads are denoted in your inbox with a cell phone icon.*

1. Navigate to **Engagement** > **Conversations** > **All** **Conversations**
2. Locate and select the message you wish to join
3. Click **Join Conversation** at the bottom

# Starting a New Email Conversation

1. Navigate to **Engagement** > **Conversations** > **All** **Conversations**
2. Click the **New Conversation** button in the top right corner
3. Select the channel - **Email**
4. Complete the fields:

   * **Recipient**: Search to find the intended recipient.
   * **Recipient Email**: If the recipient has more than one email address, you can change from the primary email to another email (secondary or school).
   * **Message**: Compose your email message. You can include a CoVideo, Loom video, emoji, files, or images.

     + 💡 Use [Bolt Writing Tools](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8380026-boltai-writing-tools) to compose your message!
   * **Subject**: Compose your email subject line.

     + 💡 Use [Bolt Writing Tools](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8380026-boltai-writing-tools) to generate your subject line!
   * **From** **Email**: If your institution has multiple email addresses connected, select the email account from which you want to send the message.

     + **Reply-To Address:** When a student replies, their response is automatically routed back into the Conversations module using a unique reply-to address (e.g., [67b77531924d3@element451.io](mailto:67b77531924d3@element451.io)). While this address may look different, students will see the **From Name** you’ve set in **Conversations Settings > General > Inboxes** as the sender in their inbox. This ensures the email appears recognizable and professional while maintaining accurate conversation threading.
   * **Participants**: Add one or more internal participants to loop others into the conversation.

     + Participants receive email notifications for all student responses.
     + You are automatically added as the first participant when you create a new conversation.
   * **Assignee**: If you want to assign the conversation to an individual participant, you can also complete it on this screen. **A conversation can only have one assignee.**
5. When everything is as you want, click **Send** to send the email and open a new conversation.

# New Email Conversations from Inbound Email Messages

When using Element451's **[Email Inbox feature](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6321778-connecting-individual-group-email-inboxes-email-forwarding)**, inbound messages can create new email conversations. When an email is received in the Inbox, Element451 evaluates the message to see if the inbound email is associated with a known contact record by checking against the Primary Email, Email Identity, and School Email Identity fields.   
​  
By default, to prevent spam and emails not relevant to a known contact from entering Conversations, Element451 does not create conversations from unknown email addresses. You can, however, enable anonymous conversations by navigating to **Conversations Settings** > **Email**.

## Bolt Writing Tools

Bolt Writing Tools for Conversations is an AI-powered tool to assist you with automated and customized responses. It can generate replies based on message context or user prompts. It simplifies drafting by improving writing, fixing spelling and grammar, shortening text, expanding details, changing tone, and translating. It also summarizes conversations into shareable notes. These tools save you time by automating repetitive communication tasks. For more information Bolt Writing Tools, [click here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8380026-bolt-writing-tools).

# Overview

Element451 offers flexible tools for managing duplicate records. You can merge duplicates directly from a contact’s profile, use the dedicated Deduplication Module, or perform bulk merges. Customize your approach to ensure clean and accurate data efficiently.

## Merging Records in Bulk

You can merge records in bulk using the Deduplication Module.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1084528008/6c76e6fa5c19305b446aea7f/Important+-+Orng.png?expires=1784430000&signature=ec35da16025f1e0bd82ffc47a69253b692389c82b3bd4e1af81609dbc9022a36&req=dSAvEsx8lYFfUfMW3Hu4gYUHJGOXDGJaaHP6CVFnMEEE9dAosF6CCsdesXYA%0ANg%3D%3D%0A) When doing so, **the records on the left (master record) will be used as the primary record** in case of data discrepancies. Ensure you’re confident that the data fields don’t need review before performing a bulk merge.

1. From the list of possible duplicate records, select multiple records to merge using Control+click (Windows), Command+click (Mac), or Shift+click (both).
2. Once you select multiple records, a blue action bar will appear at the bottom of the interface. From the bar, you can choose to merge or ignore all the records you selected.

# Searching + Merging Records from the Contact's Profile (Manual Method)

You can directly search for possible duplicates from an individual record and then execute a merge. This tool allows you to compare an individual record against your existing contact list to identify and match potential duplicates. This is helpful in cases where Element451 may not have identified a duplicate, and you suspect one.

1. Navigate to **Contacts** > **People** to access your list of contact records.
2. Open the contact record you wish to **keep.**
3. Click the **See Possible Duplicates** ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1084510780/a87511491a9148addf1b77d9/see+possible+duplicates+icon.png?expires=1784430000&signature=2eb1831032aa9df07233071373dc0defff77f8067fca0f0830aa751dc0337d1f&req=dSAvEsx%2FnYZXWfMW3Hu4gY9PrxJ1%2FoVgbES4cbLeQKKx9fWv2OLpW0qDk5SF%0Ahw%3D%3D%0A) **Icon** located in the top right corner of the profile header. Don't see the icon? See the [end of this section](#h_690f34d210) for more information.
4. Once you click on the icon, the merge interface will open, similar to the Deduplication Module.​​
5. In the duplicate field, begin typing the name or email of the suspected duplicate contact. Once you have found the record, select it.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/867848544/b1ed69e3b238759abe02c4ef/Deduplication+-+Select+Dup+User.png?expires=1784333700&signature=d807e7af6607e91cc6c9b194947b22431ef46bae60cdc9e00d8c73c6870f2096&req=fCYgHs12mIVbFb4f3HP0gCEADwoOJDAFApRAjYmvb%2FkXQmTHxUQK2LBpHrRS%0AV3Q%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/867848544/b1ed69e3b238759abe02c4ef/Deduplication+-+Select+Dup+User.png?expires=1784333700&signature=d807e7af6607e91cc6c9b194947b22431ef46bae60cdc9e00d8c73c6870f2096&req=fCYgHs12mIVbFb4f3HP0gCEADwoOJDAFApRAjYmvb%2FkXQmTHxUQK2LBpHrRS%0AV3Q%3D%0A)

   * If you determine this record is not a duplicate, you can click the red X icon to clear the record and search for a new one.  
     ​

# Best Practices for Merging Records

* Do not attempt to merge records that are actively being processed by the system. For example:

  + Records undergoing workflows (e.g., triggered by an application submission).
  + Records that have been active in the last 10 minutes.
* Allow at least 10 minutes after significant actions (like app submissions) before attempting to merge records.

# Troubleshooting

**Error: "*Workflow is outdated, probably changed since last time.”***

This occurs when workflow data for one or more of the records being merged changes between the time the merge page is opened and when the merge is attempted. This safeguard ensures that no data is overwritten or left dangling. If you receive the error, refresh the page to load updated workflow data and try merging again. Additionally, be sure to read the above [best practices](#h_4317d087f7) to avoid receiving this error.

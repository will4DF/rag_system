# Survey Invite Email Templates Use and Installation

Installing email templates is easy and can be repeated as many times as you’d like. ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1084681292/7e2dbde607f0cd8d53e49a36/Note-Orng.png?expires=1784430000&signature=d819c8d0c8e40e580895e50df2b640bf9307ddae6b37d84241840fd4c64ec222&req=dSAvEs92nINWW%2FMW3Hu4gWo8IjQ8s4fFOHQDr8ZcOT7mCeuvFA0gHLv6QWsg%0A3A%3D%3D%0A) Upon installation, you will see that the templates are branded to your institution. Customize the style and content of the email templates as you see fit.

These templates can be used for one-time or ongoing communications campaigns.

1. Navigate to the Packs library at **Data + Automations** > **Packs** > **Library.**
2. Select the survey invite email template use case:

   * **Letter**: Brief, direct message with no illustration or photo
   * **Brief** **Invite** **with** **Photo**: Includes a photo from your Pack install options
   * **Illustration and Pattern**: Includes a small illustration

   For each of these, you can choose a design style (commons, quad, or library) and (light or dark).
3. Once installed, the email template can be found by navigating to **Engagement** > **Campaigns** > **Templates**.

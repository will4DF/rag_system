# Overview

The **Bolt Agent Jobs Dashboard** analyzes the performance and impact of all AI-powered jobs at your institution. Use this dashboard to explore metrics such as job activity, goal completion rates, approval patterns, and time savings, both at the institution-wide level and for individual jobs.

## Accessing the Dashboard

Navigate to **Data + Automations** > **Bolt** **Insights** > **Bolt Agent Jobs**.

---

# Dashboard Features

The Bolt Agent Jobs Dashboard displays period-over-period comparisons, performance changes over time, and a wide range of job-related metrics.

## Global Controls

The Bolt Agent Jobs Dashboard can be filtered and controlled in several ways. Find the global controls at the top of each tab. These global controls affect each tab. Filters applied on one tab will carry over to the others.

Global controls feature filters for **date range**, **job goal**, **agent type**, **approved by**, **channel**, **funnel stage**, **urgent status, and whether or not to include data from deleted jobs**.

---

# Tabs

Data on the Bolt Agent Jobs Dashboard is organized by date and various dimensions selected in the controls menu.

## Bolt Job Activity Tab

### Visualizations

#### Jobs Highlights

* People impacted by jobs
* Total active job assignments
* Minutes saved
* Approval rate
* New jobs created
* Messages sent
* Active agents
* Total goals completed

#### Prospect Progress

*Contacts moved into each funnel stage after being contacted/impacted by a job.*

* Apps Started Progress
* Apps Submitted Progress
* Enrollment Progress
* Deposit Progress

#### Jobs Performance

* Top jobs in use
* Top agents with the most jobs completed
* Top goals completed
* Top jobs with the lowest goal completion rates
* Top agents with the lowest approval rates

#### AI Analysis

* An assessment that measures the content of each conversation using the Confusion Score, Engagement Score, and Experience Score

#### Sentiment

* Breakdown of positive, negative, and neutral sentiment

#### Job Messages by Channel

* Distribution across email, SMS, and phone

#### Approvals

* Breakdown by approver showing the number approved, pending, rejected, and the approval rate percentage

#### Job Activity Over Time

* Messages sent over time by the agent
* Goal completions by goal type

## Bolt Job Log Tab

The Bolt Job Log Tab shows a table of all job enrollments, including name, status, created date/time, updated date/time, unanswered question (Y/N), needs follow-up (Y/N), and a link to the specific job

# Metric Glossary

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Jobs** | AI-powered, proactive, and autonomous outreach performed by Bolt Agents, designed to accomplish specific goals |
| **Active Jobs** | Jobs currently running with enrolled contacts |
| **Messages Sent** | Total communications sent via jobs |
| **Goals Completed** | Number of successful job outcomes achieved |
| **Minutes Saved** | Time saved by utilizing Bolt Agents in jobs, calculated from messages originating in Jobs |
| **Approval Rate** | Percentage of messages approved (either self-approved by AI or by users) |
| **Experience Score** | Overall measure of the interaction, with reasoning that highlights successes and any friction points |
| **Success Rate** | Measurement of how well jobs achieve their intended outcomes |
| **Engagement Score** | Measurement of how engaged the contact was during the conversation |
| **Confusion Score** | Measurement of how confused the contact was during the conversation |
| **Sentiment** | Analysis of conversation tone as positive, negative, or neutral |
| **Deleted Jobs** | Jobs deleted in your instance. You can choose to include data from these jobs or to exclude it via the control labeled "Include Deleted Jobs." |

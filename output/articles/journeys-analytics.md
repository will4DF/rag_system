Learn about Journey Analytics: visualize steps with bar charts and gain insights on enrollment, completion, and activity trends.

# Overview

Once you have created a Journey, it has a dedicated page where you can monitor its analytics. By utilizing Journey Analytics, you can gain valuable insights into the performance and progress of your Journeys.

## Accessing Journey Analytics

1. **Data + Automations** > **Journeys** > **All Journeys**.
2. By default, you are taken to the **Analytics** screen; however, you can access it from the left-hand menu.

The Journey Analytics section is broken down into two visuals, **Journey Steps** and **Insights**, which we'll explain below.

# Journey Steps

The Journey Steps section visualizes each step of the Journey with a bar chart. It displays the total number and percentage of participants who have completed each step within the Journey.

# Insights

The Insights section provides a detailed breakdown of the following information:

* **Enrolled All-Time**: Total number of people who have been enrolled.
* **Active Now**: Number of people currently enrolled in the Journey who have not yet completed or exited.
* **Completed All-Time**: Total number of people who have completed the Journey, along with the percentage of the total enrolled.
* **Exited All-Time**: Total number of people who have exited the Journey, along with the percentage of the total enrolled.
* **Enrollments Over Time:** A calendar heat map of your enrollment history.

# Overview

Messenger is a powerful tool for instant communication with students, parents, and prospects. It can be seamlessly integrated into internal Element451 pages and external websites via a simple embed code or shared directly using a link—perfect for emails, social media, and more.

Personalize interactions with icebreakers, conversation starters, and saved replies, and enhance engagement further with **[Bolt Agents](https://intercom.help/element451/en/articles/7173429-bolt-assistants-for-students-overview)**, our AI-powered chatbot for timely, automated responses.

This article covers how to access, integrate, and customize Messenger, along with key features like automation rules, saved replies, and BoltAI Writing Tools to help you maximize its potential.

# Accessing Messenger

### Internal (Element451) Pages

Pages, Microsites, Application Sites, and the Appointment Booking Site each have a setting to enable the Element451 Messenger. We explain how to enable each one below in the [Adding Messenger to Internal Pages](#h_ed141f49a4) section.

### External Website Pages

Embed a code directly on your institution's website or use our WordPress plugin. Learn more about adding the Messenger widget to your external website in our [Adding Messenger to External Pages](#h_d8929b67c8) section below.

### Direct Link

With a custom URL, anyone can directly access your messenger via a dedicated page. This offers you the flexibility to integrate Messenger links seamlessly into emails, campaigns, social media, and more. You can access your URL in Conversation Settings under Messenger. Explore more in [Messenger Settings](#h_df32db7601) below.

# Key Messenger Features

## Internal Users

Consider using the following features to complement Messenger from the administrative side of Element451 to enhance the conversation experience for both you and your participants:

## Features

​**Automation Rules**  
You can tag, assign, automatically reply, close conversations, and more by using Conversation Rules. [Explore More →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1930478-automating-with-conversation-rules)

**Saved Replies**  
The saved replies feature allows you to configure and save common Messenger responses ahead of time. This lets your team quickly access and send pre-written replies to frequently asked questions during a chat conversation. Saved replies also support basic tokens, such as the participant’s name and the sender’s name, for a more personalized experience. [Explore more →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8510895-conversations-settings#h_52e446aa6a)

**Conversation Starters**

Allow you to pre-configure prompts that guide how a student or visitor may want to interact with you. For example, if a student visits your program page, you could display a conversation starter like “What Can I Study?” When clicked, it will send the message, “Hello, I’d like to learn more about your majors.” You can read more about conversation starters below under [Messenger Settings](#h_df32db7601).

**Ice Breakers**

An Icebreaker is the text displayed within the widget bubble before the messenger is opened (think of it as a prompt to get the student to open the messenger). You can get creative with these using page conditions to be more intentional. For example, on your application page, you could use an Icebreaker that says, “Need help with your application?” You can read more about ice breakers below under [Messenger Settings](#h_df32db7601).

**BoltAI Writing Tools**

BoltAI Writing Tools are integrated throughout Element451 to assist you in creating automated and customized responses or editing and improving your text. These tools generate replies based on message context or user prompts, and they simplify drafting by improving writing quality, fixing spelling and grammar, shortening text, expanding details, changing tone, and translating content. BoltAI also summarizes conversations into shareable notes, saving you time by automating repetitive communication tasks. ​[Explore More →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8380026-boltai-writing-tools)

**Voice Messages**

Messenger supports voice-**recorded** messages, allowing communication with students and other contacts using voice recordings. [Explore More →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9626902-voice-messages-in-messenger)

**Advanced Voice Mode for AI Agents**

Students can speak naturally with an AI Agents live directly within the Messenger widget by clicking the phone icon. [Explore More →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10093808-real-time-voice-for-ai-student-assistants)

## External Participants (Contacts/Students)

External participants benefit from features configured by internal users, such as **Automation Rules**, **Conversation Starters**, and **Ice Breakers**. However, they also have access to features within the Messenger widget designed specifically to enhance their experience.

## Features

**Message History Retention**

Previous conversations are retained when Element451 recognizes the user, allowing for seamless continuity and context.

**Full-Screen Viewing**

Participants can expand Messenger to full-screen width for better accessibility and an improved viewing experience.

**Rich Media Support**

In addition to sending text, external participants can enhance their messages using:

* **Emojis**: Add personality and tone with emojis.
* **Images**: Attach and send image files.
* **Files**: Share documents and other information by attaching files.

**Voice Messages**

Messenger supports voice-**recorded** messages. [Explore More →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9626902-voice-messages-in-messenger)

**Voice Calls with AI Agents**

If an AI Agent is managing the conversation, participants can opt to **call the agent** by clicking the phone icon in the Messenger interface. This enables live, natural voice interactions, simulating a human conversation experience. [Explore More →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10093808-real-time-voice-for-ai-student-assistants)

# Anonymous Conversations: How Visitors Start Conversations

When visitors start a conversation through Messenger, Element451 may not immediately know who they are. The **Anonymous Conversations** setting determines whether visitors can chat without identifying themselves first. This section explains how each option works so you can choose what's best for your institution.

## Your Two Options

* **Option A: Anonymous Conversations Enabled** – Visitors can start chatting immediately without providing any information.
* **Option B: Anonymous Conversations Disabled** – Visitors must provide their name and email before they can start a conversation.

Each option is explained in detail below:  
​

## Option A: Anonymous Conversations Enabled

With this setting, visitors can begin conversations **without** sharing their identity. However, the experience differs depending on whether Bolt Agents are actively handling messages.

### When Bolt Agents Are Active

When a visitor opens Messenger, they'll see a prompt: *"Add your email to see past conversations."*

* Providing an email is **optional**—visitors can skip this and chat immediately
* Because visitors may remain anonymous, we recommend enabling the [Inquiry Flow Skill](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8993380-bolt-agent-skills) on your Bolt Agents to collect contact information during the conversation
* If they do provide an email, you'll still need to [manually associate them](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1894279-getting-started-with-conversations#h_8b2248c1aa) with an existing contact in the conversation's "Profile" tab.

### When Bolt Agents Are NOT Active

After a visitor sends their first message, they'll automatically receive this system prompt: *"Don't have time to wait for a response? Leave your email, and we'll be in touch as soon as possible."*

1. This system prompt cannot be edited or disabled.
2. Providing an email is **optional**—visitors can continue without sharing it.
3. If they provide an email, they'll also be prompted for their first and last name (also optional).
4. If they don't provide any information; therefore, you may need to ask for identifying details directly in the conversation.
5. **Pro Tip:** You can easily associate the visitor with an existing contact or create a new profile directly from the conversation.
6. If an email is provided, association with existing contacts is **not** automatic—you must confirm this in the conversation's "Profile" tab.

## Option B: Anonymous Conversations Disabled

With this setting, visitors **must** identify themselves **before** starting a conversation. This essentially turns every visitor into an inquiry before the chat begins.

### How the Authentication Process Works

When a visitor opens Messenger, they're prompted to enter their **email address**. A verification code (OTP) is sent to that email and must be confirmed.

**If the email matches an existing contact:**

1. Once the code is verified, they're linked to their existing record
2. Any previous Messenger conversation history is displayed
3. The visitor can begin chatting with your agents or staff members

**If the email is new (doesn't exist in your system):**

1. Once the code is verified, they're then prompted to provide:

   * **Required fields:** First Name and Last Name
   * **Optional fields (if enabled):** Term, Major, and/or Date of Birth
2. Once all fields are collected:

   * A new contact record is created
   * A Prospect milestone is automatically assigned
   * The visitor can begin chatting with your agents or staff members

**Configuring Optional Fields:** You can choose which additional fields to collect during this process in Messenger Settings: *Term*, *Major*, and/or *Date of Birth*.

**Note:** If you enable optional fields, visitors will be prompted for them even if they're unsure. For Term or Major, if they provide a non-existing value, the field may be left empty, but they must still provide a response to proceed.

# Responding When Participants Are Offline

If you reply to a chat after a participant has left the page and is no longer online, your response can still reach them, under specific conditions:

* **Identified Participants**: If Element451 recognizes the participant (e.g., they are logged in or have interacted before), they will automatically receive your reply via email.
* **Anonymous Participants:** If the participant provides their email when prompted, **and** you have associated the user with a contact record, they will receive your reply via email.

If the participant is unknown and hasn’t provided an email and been associated, they will not receive your reply.

**Recommendation**: Set up **[Conversation Rules](https://intercom.help/element451/en/articles/1930478-conversation-rules)** to manage after-hours communication. For example, you can disable Messenger or configure automated responses to inform participants of your team’s availability and set clear expectations for follow-up.

# Difference Between Messenger + Bolt Agents

Messenger and Bolt Agents work together within the Conversations Module to provide flexible, real-time communication and support.

* **Messenger** is a real-time communication channel that provides a conversational interface for students to get answers or assistance quickly. It can be embedded on your Element451 pages, website, or StudentHub or accessed via a direct link—such as one shared in an email signature. Messenger supports rich interactions with features like voice recordings, file attachments, and emojis, making conversations seamless and engaging.

* **Bolt Agents** are AI-powered assistants that *overlay* the **Messenger** channel (and other conversation channels, like SMS or email). When enabled, they act as the **first line of defense**, providing around-the-clock student support through automated, real-time conversations.

Leveraging your knowledge base, Bolt Agents answer questions, guide students through tasks—like completing an application or registering for an event—and deliver accurate, timely responses. This allows your team to focus on more complex issues while ensuring students always have access to support. If human intervention is needed, Bolt Agents seamlessly hand off conversations to your team for personalized follow-up.

You can use **Messenger** with:

* **Humans only** for personalized, real-time support.
* **Bolt Agents** to automate responses and free up staff for more complex issues.
* **Both**: Start with AI and seamlessly hand the conversation off to a human when needed.

For more details on how to use Bolt Agents and manage handoffs, [explore this article](https://intercom.help/element451/en/articles/7173429-bolt-assistants-for-students-overview).

## Setting Up Human-Only Chat on the Direct Link Page

Your Messenger **Direct Link** (found in **Conversations > Settings > Messenger**) is a dedicated URL that gives anyone direct access to your Messenger, making it easy to share in email signatures, campaigns, and social media. If you use Bolt Agents but want conversations started from the Direct Link page to be handled by humans only, you can accomplish this with an agent team that contains no agents:

1. In **Bolt Agents > Teams**, create a new agent team and leave it empty (no agents).
2. Enable the team on the Messenger channel.
3. Order it at the top of your team list so its conditions are evaluated first.
4. Add a condition so the team only applies on the Direct Link page (Page URL contains your Direct Link address).
5. On your Default agent, add a condition so it does not run on the Direct Link page (Page URL does not contain your Direct Link address).

With this configuration, no Bolt Agent responds on your Direct Link page, so visitors there chat directly with your staff. Your agents continue to respond everywhere else.

📝 **Note:** Both steps are required. When no team matches a page, Messenger falls back to the Default agent, which is why the Default agent needs the exclusion condition as well.

# Adding Messenger to Internal (Element451) Pages

Enabling the Messenger Messenger to display on your internal Element451 pages and sites is a straightforward process that doesn't require any coding. To show the messenger, you can activate the 'Activate Messenger' setting for each module. We have provided instructions below on how to access the settings in each module:

### Pages

1. Navigate to **Engagement > Pages**
2. Locate the page where you want to place the messenger, and then click on that page name.
3. Click on the **Setup** tab

## Triggering Agent Interactions

On any web page with the messenger installed, you can also add buttons or links to your web pages that trigger new agent conversations within the Messenger. For example, you can show your [Bolt Agents](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/7173429-getting-started-with-bolt-agents-for-students) on your website, and allow visitors to click a button to chat with each agent directly via text or [audio](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10093808-advanced-voice-mode-for-bolt-agents).

### Steps

1. Navigate to the **Engagement > Bolt Agents**.
2. Click the agent you want to create a trigger for.
3. Scroll down and copy the "Agent ID" at the end of the settings:

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1653534854/39067584867e7b263e7286583c4b/Screenshot%2B2025-08-04%2Bat%2B3_09_41-E2-80-AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=d984fb61f381cb263c79136e3667460f2d40756087322e93ffea45a5590d48d9&req=dSYiFcx9mYlaXfMW1HO4zSACZZzaOmpxGV2jBNSrrBvlXdoHDiHdsNY0fksp%0AVQCK%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1653534854/39067584867e7b263e7286583c4b/Screenshot%2B2025-08-04%2Bat%2B3_09_41-E2-80-AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=d984fb61f381cb263c79136e3667460f2d40756087322e93ffea45a5590d48d9&req=dSYiFcx9mYlaXfMW1HO4zSACZZzaOmpxGV2jBNSrrBvlXdoHDiHdsNY0fksp%0AVQCK%0A)
4. On your web page, whenever you want to trigger a new conversation with this agent, call the `Shell451.Messenger451.askAssistant` method for text chat, or the `Shell451.Messenger451.talkToAssistant` method for voice (see details below). Note that the agent you choose must be available within the messenger on the page where you call the code.

### Examples

*Text Chat*

```
  Shell451.Messenger451.askAssistant(  
    'When are housing applications due? And can i choose my roommate?',  
    'elementu.conversation_settings.292393'  
  );
```

The first parameter represents the message the user will send to the agent when this code is run. If you don’t want to send an initial message, pass `null`. The second parameter is the agent ID from step 3.

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1653535104/da8502b802e336f8f02780d95466/Screenshot+2025-08-04+at+3_09_56%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=5a25cae44f27bd8ca64cac791a66ebaf9bc24d7faea4dd2af41172aa32e7be33&req=dSYiFcx9mIBfXfMW1HO4zX32GbnJjiicXUdhlcxwbgvNUzJ6EbEIxYV9lGAg%0AF2gLNidLYHRYJh3qi%2B8%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1653535104/da8502b802e336f8f02780d95466/Screenshot+2025-08-04+at+3_09_56%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=5a25cae44f27bd8ca64cac791a66ebaf9bc24d7faea4dd2af41172aa32e7be33&req=dSYiFcx9mIBfXfMW1HO4zX32GbnJjiicXUdhlcxwbgvNUzJ6EbEIxYV9lGAg%0AF2gLNidLYHRYJh3qi%2B8%3D%0A)

*Voice Chat*

```
Shell451.Messenger451.talkToAssistant(  
  'elementu.conversation_settings.292393',  
  {  
    message: "I'm a current student who wants to switch majors-AI sounds cool, help me decide."  
  }  
);
```

Here the first parameter is the agent ID, while the second parameter is an object containing the initial message from the user. If you do not wish to pass an initial message, omit the parameter.

### Please Note

Starting conversations with a starter message may not work well with voice mode, especially when using our experimental Multi-Lingual voices. For consistent behavior, use our normal mono-lingual voices and do not include a starter message.

## Using the WordPress Integration Plugin

If your website is built on WordPress, you can simplify the process of adding Messenger by using the [Element451 Integration Plugin](https://intercom.help/element451/en/articles/10423713-element451-integration-plugin-for-wordpress) for WordPress.

* Once the plugin is installed and activated, Messenger will automatically be enabled on all pages of your WordPress site.
* To control Messenger’s visibility, use Messenger conditions (as described above in step 4).

For more information on setting up the plugin, [visit our help article](https://intercom.help/element451/en/articles/10423713-element451-integration-plugin-for-wordpress).

# Overview

[Bolt Staff Agents](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/7173423-getting-started-with-bolt-agents-for-staff) are designed to be your personal assistant, helping you get quick, accurate answers to questions about Element451’s features. One of its key skills is providing seamless access to Help Center content so you can easily find the guidance you need without disrupting your workflow.

## Key Features and Benefits

* **Gain Instant Access**: Ask questions about any Element451 feature, and Bolt Staff Agents will deliver responses drawn directly from the Help Center.
* **Stay Focused**: No need to switch tabs or navigate away from your current task to find the answers you need. Agents are right at your fingertips.
* **Receive Accurate Answers**: Answers are sourced from up-to-date help articles and documentation, so you know you’re getting the correct information quickly.
* **Explore Source URLs**: Want more context or details? The agent links relevant help articles, allowing you to explore further.

# How To: Ask for Help Using E451 Features

Using Bolt Staff Agents is simple. Here’s how you can make the most of this helpful feature:

## Step 1: Launch the Bolt Staff Agent Side Panel

* To access the Bolt Staff Agents, open the Bolt Staff Agent Side Panel. You can find it on the Start Page or the top navigation bar. [Learn more →](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/7173423-getting-started-with-bolt-agents-for-staff)

## Step 2: Ask Questions in Natural Language

* Once in the side panel, type your question about any Element451 feature or functionality. You can phrase your questions conversationally, such as:

  + “How do I create a new segment?”
  + “What settings can I customize in the tasks module?”
  + "What is a one-time campaign?"

## Step 3: Receive Quick, Contextual Answers

* The agent will deliver immediate answers to your questions, allowing you to get the information you need without leaving your current workflow.

## Step 4: Use Source Links to Dive Deeper

* If you want to explore further, each answer includes source links to the relevant help articles. Clicking on these links takes you to the full documentation, where you can find step-by-step guides, examples, and more in-depth information.

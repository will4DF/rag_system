Navigating the Export Overview Screen + Editing, Deleting, Searching, and Filtering Exports.

# Overview

The Export task list is found in the Import + Export screen along the left hand side, under Exports.

* **Displays All Exports**: The Exports Overview screen shows all export tasks you and your team have created. This includes tasks that have been running, are scheduled to run, and have yet to be run.
* **Advanced Filters**: Apply filters to focus on the export tasks you need to edit.

## Key Features

* **Manage Export Tasks**: Without leaving the screen, you can duplicate, delete, run, and jump into editing tasks.
* **Filters**: Apply filters and sort your Export Task list by export type, status, occurrence, created date, last run, and next run to find specific tasks quicker.
* **Organize Exports into Folders**: Teams create tons of exports, and your export tasks can get lost in the shuffle. Add exports to folders so you can quickly jump back into the exports you are working on.

## Accessing the Exports Page

To access the Exports Overview Screen, navigate to **Data** + **Automations** > **Import** **+** **Export** > **Exports.**

# Managing Exports

## Editing an Export

* Option 1: Click on the **Export Task Name.**
* Option 2: Click the ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/911527686/a564a35d2a992e31b2f11dd7/Screenshot+2023-12-16+at+9.30.16%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784430000&signature=fae0d35be743ffb2db1e1927092d0210e046c3ee74b4399385f406418e799f2b&req=fSEmE8t5m4lZFb4X1HO4gWopCNyJgLEc0F4H3Wcd4JRM%2F8gGu%2FintzmP8LPH%0A) more icon, then click **Edit.**

* Option 2: Click the ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/911527686/a564a35d2a992e31b2f11dd7/Screenshot+2023-12-16+at+9.30.16%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784430000&signature=fae0d35be743ffb2db1e1927092d0210e046c3ee74b4399385f406418e799f2b&req=fSEmE8t5m4lZFb4X1HO4gWopCNyJgLEc0F4H3Wcd4JRM%2F8gGu%2FintzmP8LPH%0A) more icon, then click **Run**.

# Filtering Exports

## Advanced Filters

The Exports feature offers several advanced filtering options to navigate and manage your Export Tasks efficiently. Use the filter icon to open the advanced filtering. Here’s how you can use these options:

* **Type**: Filter Export Tasks based on their **type**, **data**, or **documents**.
* **Status**: Filter Export Tasks by their current status, such as ‘**Draft**’, ‘**Scheduled**’, ‘**Running**’, and more.
* **Occurrence**: Filter Export Tasks to view tasks that have a repeating schedule.

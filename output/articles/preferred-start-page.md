Learn how to configure yours or someone else's Preferred Start Page.

# Overview

Your Preferred Start Page is the module you'll be automatically redirected to when signing into your Element451 instance. Customize it to match your workflow and prioritize the feature that matters most to you.

---

# Configure Your Preferred Start Page

1. Navigate to your avatar/picture in the top right corner of the orange navigation bar.
2. Click **Manage** **Account.**
3. Click **Edit.**
4. Use the **Preferred Starting Page** drop-down to select a page.
5. Click **Save** in the top right corner.

# Configure Another User's Preferred Start Page

📙Note: In order to configure another user's preferred start page, you must have *Administer Internal Users* permission.

1. Navigate to your avatar/picture in the top right corner of the orange navigation bar.
2. Hover over **Settings.**
3. Click **Manage** **Users.**
4. Locate the user's name of your choosing and **click their name**.
5. In the Profile tab (which opens by default), under Basic Information, click **Edit** next to Personal Information.

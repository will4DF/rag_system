# Overview

The Bolt Campaign Creator Agent is a revolutionary way to create multi-channel, multi-communication campaigns in Element451. The agent will talk through the needs of your campaign and then share outlines, write content, create communications, and build workflows to power your campaign.

Currently, the Bolt Campaign Creator Agent cannot generate push notification campaigns as part of their drip campaigns.

## Before Trying the Bolt Campaign Creator Agent

One of the most important aspects of successful campaigns is personalization. That's why the agent utilizes your information from Element451, including **Content Tokens** like colors and logos, and the **Knowledge Hub**, to tailor your campaigns.

It's crucial to keep these elements updated to ensure optimal results. By staying on top of these details, the agent can create campaigns that truly resonate with your audience and achieve your desired outcomes.

1. Review your **Content Tokens** to ensure they are up-to-date.

   * To access Content Tokens: **Data** **+ Automations** **> Packs > Content Tokens**
   * For more information on Content Tokens, [click here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6825249-guide-to-packs-tokens).  
     ​
2. Add sources to the Knowledge Hub and review existing sources for accuracy and relevance.

   * To access the Knowledge Hub: **Data** **+ Automations** **> Knowledge**
   * For more information on building your **Knowledge** **Hub**, [click here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10302715-getting-started-with-knowledge-hub).

# Generating Campaigns Using Bolt Campaign Creator Agent

Once you have accessed the Bolt Campaign Creator Agent using the process above, you can ask it to generate your Campaign. Here's how:

1. Tell the agent about the Campaign you want created.

   * Agents are conversational and designed to ask follow-up questions if it requires additional information. For instance, if you send the prompt "I need a campaign to increase enrollment for the fall semester," it will respond with follow-up questions. For an example of this, see the screenshot below:  
     ​

     [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/819266180/68ef946c4d58cca3f8ef0abe/Copilot+for+Campaigns+Follow-Up.png?expires=1784333700&signature=8861884bec73f4f4ae6ddce2c4fe51dd2c4f146af2f2ea044a5e12fb8ec766d1&req=fCEuFM94nIlfFb4f3HP0gAqmoZ7UTigImLDvOfpwFIosIoEfK3o5Y1kMVGAW%0A7DM%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/819266180/68ef946c4d58cca3f8ef0abe/Copilot+for+Campaigns+Follow-Up.png?expires=1784333700&signature=8861884bec73f4f4ae6ddce2c4fe51dd2c4f146af2f2ea044a5e12fb8ec766d1&req=fCEuFM94nIlfFb4f3HP0gAqmoZ7UTigImLDvOfpwFIosIoEfK3o5Y1kMVGAW%0A7DM%3D%0A)
2. Once the agent has all the information it needs, it will **draft an** **outline** of your campaign for you to review.
3. From the outline, click on each communication or the angle bracket to expand the details.
4. If you want to make changes, reply with your change requests. If you **approve**, tell the agent or click "**I like it. Continue.**"  
   ​

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/819279729/ea8197a82976dc69aef549c0/Copilot+for+Campaigns+Outline.png?expires=1784333700&signature=17bef0387db0269a01229d9641944721c89a26ecf5405e3691ba5e1d7fafdfb0&req=fCEuFM53moNWFb4f3HP0gOpRG1xcglCDkdy4vcQj9%2BLdlGZfc8BE0hAcV6vx%0Ah4k%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/819279729/ea8197a82976dc69aef549c0/Copilot+for+Campaigns+Outline.png?expires=1784333700&signature=17bef0387db0269a01229d9641944721c89a26ecf5405e3691ba5e1d7fafdfb0&req=fCEuFM53moNWFb4f3HP0gOpRG1xcglCDkdy4vcQj9%2BLdlGZfc8BE0hAcV6vx%0Ah4k%3D%0A)
5. Once you **approve** the outline, the agent will build your campaign.
6. Once the build is complete, the agent will list the **Communication** and **Workflow**:

   * **Communication**

     + Each piece of communication in the Campaign will be listed separately.
     + Clicking on the title *(e.g., Email 1 - Application Reminder)* will open the communication editor to review and make any necessary changes to that specific communication.  
       ​

       [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/819312403/d3802ef8d744bd6368e0cfe4/Final+Campaign+with+Links.png?expires=1784333700&signature=aa1b85428a1d685f6aaeefa82e08958d58c1b625a1b5b5e891aee19959b4a4be&req=fCEuFch8mYFcFb4f3HP0gM97ZMJNS%2FosdTEX4vyrv0rH1zvrJHbBYhyM6OjI%0AYuA%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/819312403/d3802ef8d744bd6368e0cfe4/Final+Campaign+with+Links.png?expires=1784333700&signature=aa1b85428a1d685f6aaeefa82e08958d58c1b625a1b5b5e891aee19959b4a4be&req=fCEuFch8mYFcFb4f3HP0gM97ZMJNS%2FosdTEX4vyrv0rH1zvrJHbBYhyM6OjI%0AYuA%3D%0A)

* **Workflow**

  + Listed last is the Workflow for the campaign.
  + Clicking the title *(e.g., Increase Fall Enrollment)* will open the Workflow. You will notice the agent has orchestrated the communication flow with appropriate delays.

# Using Bolt AI to Edit Campaigns

Suppose you don't want the Campaign agent to build an entire campaign. In that case, you can still use Bolt AI to help with other tasks.

## AI-Generated Subject Lines & Preview Text

Use Bolt AI to generate recommendations for a subject line and preview text using your preferred tone of voice: **professional**, **friendly**, **exciting**, or **urgent**.

*💡 Pro Tip: Use Bolt AI to generate the subject line and preview text after composing the email. It will use the email content for its recommendations.*

1. To begin, navigate to **Engagement** > **Campaigns** (one-time or ongoing).
2. Locate and click on the campaign for which you would like Bolt AI to generate a subject line or preview text.
3. Click **edit** to open the Campaign's editor side sheet.
4. You will see the Bolt AI buttons next to the **Subject Line** and **Preview Text** fields. See screenshots below.
5. Bolt AI will generate a few options from which you can choose, and you also have the ability to adjust the tone of voice.

## AI-Powered Bolt Writing Tools

Bolt AI provides a built-in copywriting agent available to help you review and edit the text within your campaign. Bolt Writing Tools provides the following features:

* Improve Writing
* Fix Spelling & Grammar
* Make Shorter
* Expand
* Change Tone
* Translate

To use Bolt Writing Tools to edit your Campaign content:

1. Highlight the text you wish to edit.
2. Select the **Bolt AI icon** (orange bolt and three stars).
3. Select the icon corresponding to the action you would like Bolt AI to perform.

# Overview

Staff Agents are more than just tools; they are your personal agents within Element451. At their core, **Staff Agents** harness the power of several advanced large language models (LLMs), including ChatGPT, to elevate efficiency and effectiveness, freeing you to concentrate on priorities that matter.

With its generative AI capabilities and specialized skills within specific modules, Staff Agents can interpret and execute complex queries, transforming how you interact with Element451.

Staff Agent capabilities are available throughout Element451 via a **conversational** **interface** and **specialized** **module** **skills**.

## Accessing Staff Agents

You can find Staff Agents in the Bolt Staff Agent Sidebar, accessed via the [Start Page](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/7173423-getting-started-with-bolt-agents-for-staff#) or your [top navigation bar](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/7173423-getting-started-with-bolt-agents-for-staff#). You can also find other [Staff Agent-powered tools](#h_006cb55016) throughout different modules of Element451.

# Staff Agent Types + Specializations

The Bolt Staff Agent Sidebar includes four Staff Agents, each with specialized skills: User Support, Campaign Creator, Appointments, and Copywriter.

Most agents can be accessed from the **[Bolt Staff Agent Sidebar](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/7173423-getting-started-with-bolt-agents-for-staff#)**, making it easy to switch between the ones you wish to interact with. Staff Agents also automatically hand off to one another when appropriate. Explore each Staff Agent below:

## Agents in the Side Panel

These agents are available from the Bolt AI Side Panel, so you can access them from anywhere in the platform. They’re action-oriented and ready to help, no matter where you are in the platform.

### User Support Agent

Your go-to for help using Element451. Ask questions in plain language—about your students, institution, overdue tasks, and more—and get answers sourced from your Element451 data, internal Knowledge Hub, or help center.

#### Context-Aware + Action-Oriented

This agent understands where you are in the platform and tailors its responses accordingly.

* **Query** **Answering**: Ask about your institution, students, or overdue tasks using everyday language. For example: “What’s Alma’s application status?” or “What tasks are due this week?”
* **Field** **Retrieval**: Pull data directly from contact records, including custom fields. You can ask, “What’s Taylor’s t-shirt size?” or “Show me Jordan’s assigned counselor."
* **Task** **Completion**: The agent can analyze task details, suggest next steps, draft SMS/email replies, and even mark tasks complete—all within the chat. [Explore more information here](#h_bf25f92f3d).

#### Element451 Help Center Integration

Ask about Element451’s features and get real-time answers sourced from our product documentation—no need to leave your current view.

#### Internal Knowledge Hub Access

The agent also connects to your private Knowledge Hub of internal documentation, making it easy to reference team-specific resources, such as policies or procedures.

* *Tip:* Want to prioritize your internal resources? Just include that in your prompt. For example: “Use the internal Knowledge Hub: how do I submit a travel request?”

### Campaign Creator Agent

This agent is your co-creator for drip campaigns. It helps you build complete campaign flows—from strategy to copy (and workflow)—so you can launch faster with confidence.

* **Complete Campaign Creation:** Generates multi-message drip campaigns tailored to your purpose, including logic and flow.

* **Subject Lines + Preview Text**: Suggests engaging subject lines and preview text to increase open rates.

* **Refine with Bolt Writing Tools**: Once your campaign is created, use Bolt Writing Tools to enhance the content:

  + Adjust tone and length
  + Improve grammar and clarity
  + Translate or rewrite content as needed

We have a separate help article that walks you through using the [Campaign Creator Agent](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8312170-bolt-campaign-creator-agent)—check it out for complete details.

[Explore More: Campaign Creator Agent](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8312170-bolt-campaign-creator-agent)

## Contact

* **Contact Summary**

  + Retrieves and summarizes demographics, traits, and contact information for a specific contact
* **Create Note**

  + Add a note to a particular contact record. The note type is automatically inferred based on the conversation.
* **Contact Activities**

  + Retrieves the latest contact-made activities (page views, link clicks in emails, text messages, or on pages, application activities, event registration, paid, deposited, etc.) for a specific contact
* **Contact Engagement Score**

  + Retrieves the engagement score for a specific contact
* **Contact Notes**

# Context-Aware + Collaboration

Staff Agents are context-aware and can automatically detect where you are in Element451 to help you execute tasks more efficiently and accurately. Staff Agents will also hand off to their counterparts if needed. For example, if you are speaking with the User Support Agent and wish to create a new Campaign, the User Support Agent will automatically hand your prompt over to the Campaign Creator Agent.

## Contacts

* **Using Shortcuts**: When you select a shortcut while viewing a student's record, the Staff Agent automatically fills in the student's name in the prompt. You can remove the contact association by clicking the name and X icon.   
  ​  
  ​*This intelligent design eliminates manual searching and selection, assuming the active record is your focal point. This saves time and reduces the likelihood of errors, ensuring that your communications and actions are always directed at the correct contact.*

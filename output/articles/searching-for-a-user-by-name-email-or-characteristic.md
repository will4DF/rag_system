# Overview

Searching for a user in your instance by their name, email, or profile characteristics is quick and easy.

---

# Search for a User by Characteristic or Behavior

To search by a user characteristic or behavior, you will add a filter to your search.

1. Navigate to **Contacts** > **People.**
2. At the top, click the **+ Add Filter** button to the right of **Segment**. A window will open to configure your filter(s).

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877645373/f89dc94f02638c7f8c1a93c0/People+-+Searching+User+-+Add+Filter.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f9ecc4703eeeb9e09a0052210feef28de9439275114a72e062dbc043a652b41a&req=fCcgEM17noZcFb4f3HP0gFxhJvX8KjQQHUuxnF64gP3qDXcjIQdCOc2gTAPS%0A9tk%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877645373/f89dc94f02638c7f8c1a93c0/People+-+Searching+User+-+Add+Filter.png?expires=1784333700&signature=f9ecc4703eeeb9e09a0052210feef28de9439275114a72e062dbc043a652b41a&req=fCcgEM17noZcFb4f3HP0gFxhJvX8KjQQHUuxnF64gP3qDXcjIQdCOc2gTAPS%0A9tk%3D%0A)
3. Select your filter **Type.** The options are All, Popular, Users, Activity, Relationships, Tasks, Decisions, Documents, Surveys, and Appointments. [You can learn more here about how these filters can be used](https://help.element451.com/people/user-profile/how-are-filters-used).

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877647414/0b92f251bde4b657100715c5/Searching+Users+-+Filter+Type.png?expires=1784333700&signature=364fa40387efb9f0b9cec5cf8d4da36052065524750609081e7c1c7a7e3f723d&req=fCcgEM15mYBbFb4f3HP0gNd09BCicWlRW0IACMrZwDtuIuLWCINY%2FQePabtq%0AEdw%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877647414/0b92f251bde4b657100715c5/Searching+Users+-+Filter+Type.png?expires=1784333700&signature=364fa40387efb9f0b9cec5cf8d4da36052065524750609081e7c1c7a7e3f723d&req=fCcgEM15mYBbFb4f3HP0gNd09BCicWlRW0IACMrZwDtuIuLWCINY%2FQePabtq%0AEdw%3D%0A)
4. After selecting the Type that matches how you want to search, you'll be prompted to choose additional options related to your specific search. You can also use the **Find Filter** search option to narrow the list quickly.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877648939/f50b15a9a3ba032c957e0e08/Searching+Users+-+Filter+Selections.png?expires=1784333700&signature=7f0d8ddbcf25a11fe194e4eee5f24d3aeeda2a8bff463eb990cc827b507dc8d0&req=fCcgEM12lIJWFb4f3HP0gHVyGQn6cugsbtukq3bhKcdhhhS0MWz2MENLrpy0%0ASWY%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877648939/f50b15a9a3ba032c957e0e08/Searching+Users+-+Filter+Selections.png?expires=1784333700&signature=7f0d8ddbcf25a11fe194e4eee5f24d3aeeda2a8bff463eb990cc827b507dc8d0&req=fCcgEM12lIJWFb4f3HP0gHVyGQn6cugsbtukq3bhKcdhhhS0MWz2MENLrpy0%0ASWY%3D%0A)
5. Click **Submit.**
6. You can then add additional filters, or if you're done, click **Apply.**

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877651508/a8ccac67cbcc8126090ef502/Searching+Users+-+Apply+Filter.png?expires=1784333700&signature=30f1852929f12ff8ad0bf9eb3272239a1c54361bf2e96d4452c6489fb88ffc65&req=fCcgEMx%2FmIFXFb4f3HP0gFB1cxWW9%2F6N4dpSFD%2BLAASU9hl2S%2FbF9Sf2VKZt%0AEOU%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877651508/a8ccac67cbcc8126090ef502/Searching+Users+-+Apply+Filter.png?expires=1784333700&signature=30f1852929f12ff8ad0bf9eb3272239a1c54361bf2e96d4452c6489fb88ffc65&req=fCcgEMx%2FmIFXFb4f3HP0gFB1cxWW9%2F6N4dpSFD%2BLAASU9hl2S%2FbF9Sf2VKZt%0AEOU%3D%0A)
7. People whose profiles fit your selected filters will now be displayed.
8. You can save your search as a new segment by clicking **Save As New Segment** or clicking **Clear** to clear your search to start over.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877652467/ae263f52ee83071043594e46/Searching+Users+-+Clear+Filters.png?expires=1784333700&signature=74306596965368870437d15d645971a18e1276227874071d7207a9ac02345172&req=fCcgEMx8mYdYFb4f3HP0gHjU%2F2MwpNpDgEOG4rxuworWxgT08kLsWFA9QiZk%0AN%2F0%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877652467/ae263f52ee83071043594e46/Searching+Users+-+Clear+Filters.png?expires=1784333700&signature=74306596965368870437d15d645971a18e1276227874071d7207a9ac02345172&req=fCcgEMx8mYdYFb4f3HP0gHjU%2F2MwpNpDgEOG4rxuworWxgT08kLsWFA9QiZk%0AN%2F0%3D%0A)

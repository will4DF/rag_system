Content is added in the form of blocks. Once you add a block, you can click an element inside the block to customize it further. For specifications on images, click [here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/2582895-creating-managing-pages#h_6fe0308747).

### Content Block Types

* Custom Components (blocks you've saved to reuse)
* Billboard with CTA

  + When you upload different images for desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints, our system uses the correct image for each screen size. However, there's an important design principle at work:

    - Images maintain their aspect ratio (width-to-height proportion) to prevent distortion
    - We use "cover" functionality so images fill their container completely
    - On smaller screens, this means the sides of wider images may be cropped to maintain focus on the center
  + Consider these aspect ratio guidelines for each breakpoint:

    - **Desktop:** Wider images (approximately 4:3 ratio)
    - **Tablet:** More balanced images (approximately 1:1 ratio)
    - **Mobile:** Taller images (approximately 3:4 ratio)
  + Pro Tip: Think about what needs to remain in focus on each device. For example, if you want a person's face centered on mobile but positioned to the left on desktop, choose different images that keep your key elements properly positioned for each screen size.
  + There are no exact pixel specifications because of the wide variety of devices available, but following these aspect ratio guidelines will help ensure your content remains visually effective across all breakpoints.

## Subpages

Within the Subpages tab, you can easily add and manage nested subpages. Subpages are designed to help you build more structured and navigable landing pages, perfect for detailed content organization.

For example, your primary page could be **academics.fireuniversity.edu**. You could organize related academic program content under subpages like **/biology** and **/accounting**. This results in a more organized landing page structure, enhancing user navigation and the overall user experience.

## Domains

### Primary Domain

Element451 automatically assigns your page a primary domain, such as `fire.pages451.sites.451.io`, which cannot be edited.

### External Domain

Although the primary domain is functional, it may not reflect your institution's brand identity as effectively as a .edu URL. You can set up an external domain that masks the primary domain with a URL incorporating your school's name or abbreviation. This often requires collaboration with your IT or website team.

# 🖼 Image Sizing/Ratio Suggestions

When adding images to your pages, here are some best practices for sizing/ratios:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Image Format** | **Dimensions (WxH)** | **Aspect Ratio** |
| Landscape | 1920px x 1080px | 16:9 |
| Portrait | 1080px x 1920px | 9:16 |
| Square | 1000px x 1000px | 1:1 |

How to guide a student through resetting their password and directions on manually updating their password.

# Overview

When students start an application, they are prompted to create an account. If, for some reason, they need to reset or change their password, they can do so through a self-service process, or you can manually change their password from the student's profile/record.

**Note**: This process is only for institutions utilizing Element451 authentication. If you are using SSO, the student's password must be reset by your institution.

---

# Student Self-Service Password Reset

When a student is attempting to access an application site or microsite, they will be prompted to sign in. If the student has forgotten their password, they can follow the process below to reset their password:

1. Click **forgot** **password.**

# Manually Change a User's Password

1. Navigate to **Contacts** > **People.**
2. Locate and open the person that you wish to deactivate.
3. Click on the **settings** icon (gray person silhouette with gear) in the upper right of the screen.
4. Click **Change User's Password.**
5. You will be prompted to enter a new password and then enter it again as confirmation.
6. Click **Save.**
7. The student should now be able to log in using the new password.

# Troubleshooting

**Issue: A student is trying to reset their password, and they have yet to receive the email.**

Solution: The student needs to check their junk or spam folder in case the email was mistakenly sent there. Also, ensure that the **User** **Password** **Reset** **Request** autoresponder message is activated in your application settings by going to **Applications** > **Applications** > **Application** **Settings**. The student will only receive the email if this message is enabled.

Learn how to navigate and leverage form submission data in Element451 to enhance contact profiles and engagement strategies.

# Overview

Forms play a crucial role in capturing vital information about students and other users, helping you to manage contacts more effectively and personalize your engagement strategies. This guide will walk you through what happens after a form is submitted, how this data enriches contact profiles, and how you can leverage these insights across the platform.

---

# What Happens After a Submission?

When a form is filled out and submitted, a couple of key processes occur:

* **Profile** **Creation** or **Update**: If the submitted form includes a known email address, the contact’s profile in your database is either created (for new contacts) or updated with the information provided. For instance, if a student already in your system selects "Accounting" as their intended major on a Request for Information (RFI) form, this detail is added to their profile, ensuring their information is current and accurate.
* **Prospect** **Milestone**: Utilizing a prospect form adds a prospect milestone to the student's record. This is particularly useful for tracking the student's journey and engagement levels from prospect to enrolled student.

# Accessing Submission Details

## Form Submissions Card (Form Snapshots)

You can use the **Form** **Submissions** card on a [contact's profile](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1475735-the-person-profile) to quickly glance at what a user submitted. This feature provides immediate access to the submitted data but requires that the card be enabled on your profile template.

## Contact’s Activity Feed

For more detailed insights on a submission, including UTM parameters and the form’s access variables, check the contact’s [Activity Feed](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/5159877-activity-feed-overview). This section offers a comprehensive view of the form submission details, including the URL of the page where the Form was submitted (plus any parameters appended to the URL such as UTMs), as well as browser, device and location data.

# Leveraging Form Submissions Across Element451

Form submission data isn't just for viewing; it's a powerful tool that can be used across various modules in Element451:

## People/Segments

Use the *[Submitted Form](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1476084-filters-overview)* [filter](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1476084-filters-overview) to segment users based on their form submissions. You can refine your segments with specific form properties, such as form name and UTM details, to tailor your outreach and engagement strategies.

## Workflows/Rules and Journeys

Automatically enroll people who submit specific forms into designated [workflows, rules](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1500290-triggers), or [journeys](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6825003-journeys). This allows for seamless automation of follow-up actions, ensuring timely and relevant engagement with your contacts.

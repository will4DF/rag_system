# Student Access

#### How do students access Microsites with magic links?

Students can access Microsites in two ways:

* **Log in manually** with the credentials they created when starting an application.
* **Using a Magic Link** sent via email or SMS, which allows them to bypass login.

**What is a Magic Link?** A Magic Link is a **unique, short-term login token** that identifies the student and links them to their existing record. This eliminates the need for a manual login, making access faster and easier.

**How do students use a Magic Link to access a Microsite?**

1. **An email or SMS campaign is sent** with the **Microsite login URL token** ([microsite:login\_url]).
2. **The student clicks the Magic Link** in the campaign.
3. **The Microsite automatically opens and authenticates them**, linking them to their existing record without requiring a login.

For more details, visit [Magic Links](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8106669-magic-links).

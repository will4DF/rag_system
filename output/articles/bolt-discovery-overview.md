# Overview

Bolt Discovery transcends traditional search, empowering users—students, parents, alumni, or other visitors—to understand topics quickly and accurately, enhancing overall satisfaction. Powered by Element451's Bolt technology, leveraging large language models (LLMs) and custom Natural Language Processing (NLP) models, Bolt Discovery revolutionizes search relevance, surfaces timely content, and predicts user intent while delivering human-like responses. Using your existing [Bolt Knowledge Hub](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10302715-getting-started-with-knowledge-hub), Bolt Discovery provides accurate and relevant answers, taking users beyond the conventional search experience into a new era of interactive discovery.

Here’s what the student/user can expect from Bolt Discovery:

## The Bolt Discovery Experience

* **Generative Q&A:**

  + **Intelligent Understanding**: Bolt Discovery doesn't just match keywords. It comprehends the intent behind queries, providing relevant results even for complex or ambiguous questions.
  + **Direct Answers**: Students get immediate, concise answers to their questions instead of a list of links. They don't have to navigate multiple pages to find answers or information.
* **Conversational Flow**: Bolt Discovery maintains context, suggesting relevant follow-up questions. It's like having a knowledgeable staff member available 24/7.
* **Multilingual Support**: Engage international students in their preferred language, breaking down barriers to information.
* **Follow-Up Questions**: At the end of each answer, Bolt Discovery automatically suggests intelligent follow-up questions based on the thread's context to create an **engaged exploration.** This allows the student to uncover aspects of your institution or their query they might not have initially considered, broadening their understanding and interest.
* Citations: When citation display is enabled for a source, Bolt Discovery can include URL, Website, and File Upload sources at the beginning of an answer with inline citation references. Citations give contacts more context and direct access to the source that informed the answer.
* **Search Animation**: An animation displays query-specific messages like *“Searching for the FAFSA deadline…”* to provide a more engaging wait experience. Some searches may include multiple text animations for added context.

## Interface Features

The Bolt Discovery interface/modal offers several additional functionalities to enhance the student experience:

* **Thread** **History**: Displays recent interactions sorted by today, yesterday, and then by date. Once Element451 confirms a user’s identity, all previous threads are associated with that user.
* **New Thread Creation:** Allows users to start new threads effortlessly.
* **Copy Response:** Users can copy responses directly from the modal.
* **Share Thread:** Share threads via a URL or social media. By default, threads are private and viewable only by the author. Users can use the sharing feature to share a thread with someone else, such as a parent. The recipient can continue the conversation without affecting the original thread.

## Lead Capture Forms

The dynamic lead capture feature enhances Bolt Discovery by providing a seamless way to identify and capture a prospective student's information within the Discovery interface.

📌 **Note:** You can disable the lead capture form prompt in [Discovery Settings](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9397400-bolt-discovery-settings).

### How it works:

1. **Prospect Detection**:

   * Discovery intelligently detects when a user is a potential prospect based on the query context.
2. **Dynamic Button**:

   * Once a prospect is detected, a follow-up question appears alongside search results and other intelligent follow-up questions. The button will have a note emoji (📝) and typically displays a message like "I'm interested in applying to `[Institution Name]`. Can you provide more information?"

     [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1231919880/66799ac8567f52a49fedf77831a9/Follow+Up+Prompts+-+RFI.png?expires=1784333700&signature=18b0e0247615ee85fc33b5b44b29ea1982d7e900043a2b74e3930d97ca59bd6e&req=dSIkF8B%2FlIlXWfMW1HO4zXAo2LaGR3uUoiepzyeK1FQW%2B2OLDhPj3riw5Qbj%0A430R%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1231919880/66799ac8567f52a49fedf77831a9/Follow+Up+Prompts+-+RFI.png?expires=1784333700&signature=18b0e0247615ee85fc33b5b44b29ea1982d7e900043a2b74e3930d97ca59bd6e&req=dSIkF8B%2FlIlXWfMW1HO4zXAo2LaGR3uUoiepzyeK1FQW%2B2OLDhPj3riw5Qbj%0A430R%0A)
3. **User Interaction**:

   * When the prospect clicks the button, the lead capture form opens.
4. **Lead Capture Form**:

   * Two types of forms are available:

     + **Custom Form**: Selected from your [existing prospect-type forms](https://help.element451.com/en/collections/3880841-forms) in the forms module.

       - Custom forms open as a separate module overlaying Bolt Discovery.
       - Multiple forms can be added with deployment controlled by page URL, path URL, or UTM parameter conditions.
       - To add custom forms, navigate to [Bolt Discovery Settings](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9397400-bolt-discovery-settings).
     + **Default Form**: If no custom form is specified, a default form asking for First Name, Last Name, and Email Address is used.

       - The default form opens inline within the Discovery module.

         [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1170194449/8186eeaed5d59ab8d96389c3/CleanShot+2024-09-05+at+12_22_36.png?expires=1784333700&signature=2f3e53efe4ee20a7f9d9f18a3dac2dc25064d9d071c53bb8d3a564491124e450&req=dSEgFsh3mYVbUPMW1HO4zY7GCjV6mopgvsi0IyIvBC8QBjdJ72P1TtEqo6N2%0AwR0b%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1170194449/8186eeaed5d59ab8d96389c3/CleanShot+2024-09-05+at+12_22_36.png?expires=1784333700&signature=2f3e53efe4ee20a7f9d9f18a3dac2dc25064d9d071c53bb8d3a564491124e450&req=dSEgFsh3mYVbUPMW1HO4zY7GCjV6mopgvsi0IyIvBC8QBjdJ72P1TtEqo6N2%0AwR0b%0A)
5. **Form Submission**:

   * The student fills out and submits the lead capture form.
6. **Automated Contact Creation**:

   * Upon form submission, the system checks if the user already exists in the database:

     + If the user exists, the record is updated with any new information.
     + If the user does not exist, a new contact record is created.
7. **Prospect Milestone Creation**:

   * The system assigns a prospect milestone to the contact (new or existing).
   * This milestone includes a hyperlink to the conversation thread, providing context for future interactions.
   * This milestone can be used to track the prospect's journey and trigger further marketing or admissions processes.

## Messenger Integration

Integrating Bolt Discovery and [Messenger](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6249763-messenger-live-chat) creates a powerful, user-centric support system that combines the efficiency of intelligent search with the personalization of live chat.

📌 **Note:** You can disable the Messenger handoff in [Discovery Settings](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9397400-bolt-discovery-settings).

### How it Works

1. **Feature Activation**:

   * To activate this feature, Messenger must be enabled on the same page (this can be on your website or an Element451 page) that Bolt Discovery is embedded and being accessed. For help enabling Messenger, [click here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6249763-messenger-live-chat). Note: Messenger is not compatible with your stand-alone Bolt Discovery URL.
2. **Bolt Discovery Search**:

   * Users begin their journey by utilizing the Bolt Discovery tool to find relevant information quickly and efficiently.
3. **Follow-up Question Button**:

   * When Messenger is enabled on the same page as Discovery, a follow-up question appears alongside search results and other intelligent follow-up questions. The button will have a chat bubble emoji (💬) and typically displays a message like "Can you connect me with someone who can provide more details about `[thread topic]`?" or "I have additional questions about `[thread topic]`. Can I speak to a representative?"
   * This button is the "handoff" between Discovery and Messenger.

     [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1231925097/457f4a0ba8789c967edcb96a2af5/Follow+Ups+-+Chat+Handoff.png?expires=1784333700&signature=6f8ba7950d50439ef15c66e7153e038ce9b5f6b3d9eb262587f7f4686c4bf312&req=dSIkF8B8mIFWXvMW1HO4ze7Vc1CyUdIvrfdBto3zDYwrcybtYdtAmF06FQf2%0A9uBz%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h9r2yo9x/1231925097/457f4a0ba8789c967edcb96a2af5/Follow+Ups+-+Chat+Handoff.png?expires=1784333700&signature=6f8ba7950d50439ef15c66e7153e038ce9b5f6b3d9eb262587f7f4686c4bf312&req=dSIkF8B8mIFWXvMW1HO4ze7Vc1CyUdIvrfdBto3zDYwrcybtYdtAmF06FQf2%0A9uBz%0A)
4. **Triggering Messenger**:

   * When a user clicks the follow-up question button, it automatically triggers the Messenger widget to open, Bolt Discovery is closed, and we automatically send that question on behalf of the user.

# Sharing a Thread: Step-by-Step

* By default, threads are private and viewable only by the user/author.
* If the user would like to share a thread with someone else—such as a parent—they can easily do so using the sharing feature.
* When a thread is shared, a new, private thread is created for the recipient, allowing them to explore independently without affecting the original.

## How To: Share a Thread

1. Click the **Share** button at the top of the thread modal.
2. Choose **Shareable**.
3. A shareable URL will automatically be copied to your clipboard, allowing you to paste wherever needed. Alternatively, you can select one of the other share methods listed.

# Pro Tips + Additional Resources

* Maintain your [Knowledge Hub](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10302715-getting-started-with-knowledge-hub) with up-to-date information to power Bolt Discovery with the most accurate information.

  + Note that Bolt Discovery, like Bolt Agents, prioritizes [Custom Answer sources](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10302715-getting-started-with-knowledge-hub). As a reminder, Custom Answers are always paraphrased, and combining multiple sources in the answer is possible.
* Utilize [starters](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9397400-bolt-discovery-settings#h_fda3d521c0) for user engagement with personalized recommendations to explore topics thoroughly.

  + Tailor the titles and messages of your starters to your users' common queries and interests to enhance engagement.
  + Use conditions to display relevant starters based on the user’s current interaction path or campaign source, optimizing the relevance of the prompts.
  + Regularly review and update your starters to align with current offerings and user needs, ensuring the prompts remain effective and relevant.

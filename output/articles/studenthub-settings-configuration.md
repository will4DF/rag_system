## Features Section

* **Element Messenger**

  + Use this toggle to enable or disable the [live chat messenger](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6249763-messenger-live-chat) and [Bolt Agents](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/7173429-bolt-assistants-for-students-overview) in StudentHub.
* **Discovery**

  + Toggle the [Bolt Discovery](https://help.element451.com/en/collections/9444230-bolt-discovery) feature on or off in the StudentHub.  
    ​
* **Event**

  + Toggle the [Events](https://help.element451.com/en/collections/124586-events) feature on or off in the StudentHub.
  + When enabled, students can review upcoming events, register for events, and manage their upcoming registrations.   
    ​
* **Applications**

  + Toggle the [Applications](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1963691-getting-started-with-applications) feature on or off in the StudentHub.
  + When enabled, students can view their applications, which include status, term, and major. For admitted students with released decisions, activated [package items](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9236362-decisions-packages) (such as acceptance letters and financial aid information) will also be visible here.   
    ​
* **Appointments**

  + Toggle the [Appointments](https://help.element451.com/en/collections/4066448-appointments) feature on or off in the StudentHub.
  + When enabled, students can review availability, schedule appointments, and manage their upcoming appointments.   
    ​
* **Tasks**

  + Toggle the [Tasks](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/3065709-getting-started-with-tasks) feature on or off in the StudentHub.
  + When enabled, students can view Contact Tasks assigned by internal users and mark them complete (if permitted in the task setting).   
    ​
* **Courses**

  + Toggle the [Courses](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10420398-getting-started-with-courses) feature on or off in the StudentHub.
  + When enabled, students can view their course data (course schedule, grades, sections, and more) synced from your LMS.  
    ​
* **Grades**

  + When Courses is enabled, the Grades setting appears. This setting allows you to control which grade fields are visible to students.

    - “Always Show” - shows all grades for enrollments
    - “Never Show” - does not show any grades for enrollments
    - “Only Show Current” - only shows the current grade for enrollments

    *Grades shown will match the grade displayed on the student’s course enrollment in Element451.*

The domains setting section in your StudentHub settings will only appear if you have uploaded a square logo. If you see a banner at the top of the page prompting you to add a square logo, complete that task first. This step ensures proper functionality of the Progressive Web App (PWA). Without a square logo in place before app installation, changes to the logo won’t automatically update in the app unless users delete and reinstall it.

* **Primary Domain**: Element451 automatically assigns Your StudentHub two primary domains, which can not be edited. They will look something like this: `elementuniversity.campushub.ai` and `elementuniversity.portal451.io`. You can use either one.
* **External Domain**: While the primary domain URLs are functional, they don't share the same institutional brand identity and trust as a URL ending in .edu. To that end, you can configure an external domain to mask or hide the primary domain and use a URL ending in your school name or abbreviation. You'll likely need to work with your IT or website team for this process. To explore configuring external domains further, [click here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9358702-configuring-external-domains).

# StudentHub Branding

StudentHub uses your existing branding settings from Element451. If you notice something that doesn't look right, ensure your logos, colors, and other brand elements are updated in [General Settings > Branding](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/8471334-general-settings#h_c6ecd1604e).

## Logo Placement

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Location** | **Logo Used** |
| App Icon | Square (1:1) |
| Welcome Page | Square (1:1) |
| Log-In Page | Full-Color Logo |
| Explore Page | All-White Logo |

## **Troubleshooting**

**“I updated my square logo, but the app doesn’t reflect the update.”**

If you’ve updated your square logo but the app isn’t showing the change, it’s likely due to the Progressive Web App (PWA) caching process. PWAs don’t automatically update the logo after app installation. To see the updated logo, users must delete and reinstall the app.

# Testing StudentHub

As you configure your settings—and periodically once your StudentHub is live—it’s crucial to test and experience it from a student’s perspective. This helps ensure everything works as expected and looks great.

## How to Test Your StudentHub

1. Create a test student user account through the app registration process.
2. Navigate to your Studenthub URL on your mobile phone, tablet, or computer.

   * While we recommend testing on a mobile phone for the most accurate experience, you can also use your desktop computer to view and test.
3. Log in using the test credentials to explore the student experience. You can register for an event, schedule an appointment, etc., to experience the powerful functionality that StudentHub offers.

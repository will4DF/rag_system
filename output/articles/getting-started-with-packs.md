# Overview

**Compilation Failed Error:** If you encounter the error **“Compilation Failed”** while trying to download a Pack, it may be due to incorrect campus address settings. You can find more information about this in our [troubleshooting section below.](#h_241eb64a05)

Packs are ready-to-use campaigns built by the Element team and informed by machine learning to help you move faster and do better work.  
​

The Packs library is filled with pre-built campaigns, automations, and tools to help you streamline repetitive tasks, take advantage of best practices, support the full student journey, and integrate with external tools.

Packs strategically combine campaigns, segments, workflows, and more, so with a few clicks, you have a targeted, personalized campaign with an automated workflow ready to launch. No other CRM has such out-of-the-box personalized campaigns that are as tightly integrated into the product as Packs.

# Packs Tokens

When a Pack is installed, assets like emails and landing pages are automatically generated using information about your institution and your brand—such as logos, fonts, colors, and photos. This ensures that the installed components are authentic and unique to your school.

*You can access your Packs Tokens at* **Data + Automations** > **Packs** > **Packs** **Content** **Tokens**.

To use the Packs library, you must first set up your Packs Tokens (usually done during implementation). **[Learn more about installing Packs Tokens](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6825249-guide-to-packs-tokens).**

# Browse Individual Packs

# Installing and Managing Packs

## Installing a Pack

1. To install a Pack, simply click the “**install**” button on the Pack description landing page.  
   ​

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/637907525/c47d9a8679d35c3832cf5e4a/image.png?expires=1784333700&signature=31197faccee928d387728b586eae385d76ac171b4d1aa0de838447015c9a02d0&req=ciMgH8l5mINaFb4f3HP0gJkSosAQB%2FG%2FQfZNAlTUoc8VQnOGVDOaoTq5fQOO%0AfdA%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/637907525/c47d9a8679d35c3832cf5e4a/image.png?expires=1784333700&signature=31197faccee928d387728b586eae385d76ac171b4d1aa0de838447015c9a02d0&req=ciMgH8l5mINaFb4f3HP0gJkSosAQB%2FG%2FQfZNAlTUoc8VQnOGVDOaoTq5fQOO%0AfdA%3D%0A)

Some Packs allow you to choose from several email designs and content styles.

# Troubleshooting

## “Compilation Failed” Error When Downloading a Pack

If you encounter the error **“Compilation Failed”** while trying to download a Pack, it may be due to incorrect campus address settings.

### Solution

1. Navigate to **Data Sources** > **Campuses.**
2. Ensure the campus address is selected using the **“Search Address”** feature instead of manually entering it.

Selecting the address through the “Search Address” feature captures the required geo-coordinates. This is essential for Packs that rely on the **distance segment filter,** which requires properly formatted geo-coordinates. After updating the address, try downloading the Pack again.

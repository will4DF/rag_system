# Overview

The All Decisions page in Element451 is your starting point for managing application submissions. It provides **a comprehensive list of all application decisions**, each with key information to assist in your review and decision-making processes.

* **[Decision List + Full Access](#h_3921c53f4a):** Click on a decision to access a comprehensive list of all decisions and a detailed view of each application. This unlocks comprehensive evaluation tools and complete information for in-depth analysis.
* **[Filter + Search](#h_33f232fde2):** View and manage all application decisions with options to filter or search based on criteria such as score, stage, status, and more.
* **[Quick Actions](#h_e8cb8ccd6a):** Add an assignee or edit tags from the list.

## Accessing All Decisions/Application Submissions

Navigate to **Applications** > **Decisions** > **All** **Decisions.**

# Searching and Filtering Decisions

The list of application decisions can be narrowed down using Cohorts, filters, and/or keywords.

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1026914569/5283f4ecd3f3da9033293157/Decisions+-+Filtering+Combo.png?expires=1784333700&signature=2d300b192dc451986b612141eef0df80ef60671e98264fe4dcf3d5a8eb8101bc&req=dSAlEMB%2FmYRZUPMW1HO4zSt7Psu%2Bl6cx2tOX9MTnNYV6jlvil3stiPXAHqML%0AiW1p1Du47LdkZ23EhSU%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1026914569/5283f4ecd3f3da9033293157/Decisions+-+Filtering+Combo.png?expires=1784333700&signature=2d300b192dc451986b612141eef0df80ef60671e98264fe4dcf3d5a8eb8101bc&req=dSAlEMB%2FmYRZUPMW1HO4zSt7Psu%2Bl6cx2tOX9MTnNYV6jlvil3stiPXAHqML%0AiW1p1Du47LdkZ23EhSU%3D%0A)

* **Cohorts**: On the left-hand side, the Cohorts you have access to are listed. Click on a Cohort to filter the list. [Explore more on Cohorts here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/9235440-decisions-cohorts).
* **Search** **Bar**: Use the search bar above the decision list tofind a decision by name or email. The results will appear as you type. You can also use quick filters and advanced filtering to narrow the list further.
* **Filter:** To apply a filter to your search:

  + Click on the **funnel icon** at the end of the search field.
  + **Select** the parameter(s) you wish to filter upon. If you use multiple filters, they behave as *AND* filters, meaning the decision must meet **all** the filter parameters to be returned in the search results.

    - You can filter by *Term*, *Status*, *Stage*, *Gender*, *Country*, *State*, *Major*, *Application*, *Time in Queue*, *Score*, *Assigned to*, *Checklist Completed*, *Citizenship Country*, *Fraud Detection*, and *Bolt App Reader Analysis*.

      * ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1109149312/bd5c5973a68213b8c24d8280/Note.png?expires=1784430000&signature=b3bbcd9ef2c3dfd037ed731d5eecdac10ca72a64f22aa73e0e660144460ed611&req=dSEnH8h6lIJeW%2FMW3Hu4gRyEnXek%2Bf8BWuIHV4giMlv3Jp3Q%2BjHwBRRBNh5K%0AoA%3D%3D%0A) When using the 'Score' filter, after applying the filter, you will need to click the red chip to define the score range.
      * ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1109149312/bd5c5973a68213b8c24d8280/Note.png?expires=1784430000&signature=b3bbcd9ef2c3dfd037ed731d5eecdac10ca72a64f22aa73e0e660144460ed611&req=dSEnH8h6lIJeW%2FMW3Hu4gRyEnXek%2Bf8BWuIHV4giMlv3Jp3Q%2BjHwBRRBNh5K%0AoA%3D%3D%0A) When using the 'Assigned To' filter, the user needs to be assigned to **at least one** Decisions permission group to be shown as an option. These permission groups are managed here: Applications > Decisions > Decision Settings > Groups & Permissions.
  + Click **Apply** **Filters**. You will be returned to the decision list, and your filter(s) will appear in the search bar, narrowing the list.
  + If you use the **Time in Queue** or **Score** filters, after applying the filter, you will see 'Click to define' on the filter chip within the search bar. Click the chip to define the value.
* **Quick Filters**: Quick Filter chips under the search bar allow you to quickly narrow down the list by when it was submitted (today, this week, or this month) and if it is assigned to you.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1026885064/a91f11a2130bb9c1b91dd73b/Pro+Tip+-+Orng.png?expires=1784430000&signature=01751941d7f43c59e2ed7136efda23c9c923bd4b6be3de8afc47184e7a0fe894&req=dSAlEMF2mIFZXfMW3Hu4gQ2Q6r%2BBLgbL1l98pwV0efoSLKIkCkxvnPgRKP4a%0AJA%3D%3D%0A) You can combine your filtering methods by selecting a cohort and applying a filter(s) for more advanced segmentation.

# Pro Tip: How To Access a Decision from a Student Profile

To open a student's application decision directly from their [profile](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1475735-the-person-profile#h_8456eb5949), make sure the application card is active on your [profile template](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6449965-bolt-profile-templates). Then, follow these steps:

1. Locate the **Applications** card.
2. Click on the application to expand its details.
3. Click on the Status/Decision chip to view the specific decision.

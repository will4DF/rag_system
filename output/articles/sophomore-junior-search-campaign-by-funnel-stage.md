The Sophomore / Junior Search Campaign Pack is a communications campaign targeted at high school sophomores and juniors. Message segmentation and CTAs will vary depending on whether the contact is a suspect or a prospect.

This Pack can be used by any type of institution looking to reach high school students at the beginning of their college search. Institutions may also be interested in our [Sophomore / Junior Search Campaign by Grade Level Pack](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/6916482-sophomore-junior-search-campaign-by-grade-level).

## Pack Install Options

* **Intended Terms:** Select the intended enrollment term(s) for the campaign audience
* **Prospect Action URL:** Enter a URL to be displayed in the CTA buttons for **prospects**, such as a link to sign up for a visit day.
* **Suspect Action URL:** Enter a URL to be displayed in the CTA buttons for **suspects**, such as a link to an RFI or pre-populated form to verify contact information.
* **Audience Segment:** Specifies who will receive these messages.
* **Configure Communications**

  + **Sender email address and name**: configures the reply-to email address and name of the sender in messages that are sent.
  + **SMS phone number and sender name**: similarly configures the phone number and name referenced in text messages that go out. *(e.g., "This is Chris from Element University...")*
  + **Contact info**: Populates the footer fields in the associated emails.
* **Communications frequency:** sets the delay between messages (weekly by default).

## Email Components

The emails included with this campaign include suggested pre-written content to address topics of interest to prospective students, such as financial aid opportunities, the return on investment of a college degree, and the campus community. The CTA in each message points to the URL that you specified in the Pack install options.

**Suspects** are encouraged to fill out a profile, while **prospects** are encouraged to sign up for a visit event.

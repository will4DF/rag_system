Learn how to review and manage enrollees: track progress, filter by steps, manually add or remove contacts, and view enrollment details.

# Overview

On a Journey’s page, you can review which contacts have been enrolled in the Journey via the established triggers and view details about their progress. Additionally, you can manually add individual contacts or segments of contacts to the Journey.

## Accessing Journey Enrollees

1. Navigate to **Data + Automations** > **Journeys** > **All Journeys**.
2. Locate the Journey you wish to view and click on its name.
3. From the Journey page, click on **Enrolled People** from the left-hand menu.

# Reviewing + Managing Enrolled People

In the **Enrolled People** section, you can view a table listing all contacts enrolled in the Journey. The table includes columns that provide the following details about each contact’s progress through the Journey:

* **Current Status**: Indicates whether the contact is active, completed, or exited.
* **Journey Started Date**: The date the contact was enrolled in the Journey.
* **Journey Completed Date**: The date the contact completed the Journey.
* **Journey Exited Date**: The date the contact exited the Journey.
* **Last Step Completed**: The most recent step the contact has finished.

## Filtering List

Next to the total count of people enrolled in the Journey, there is a drop-down field. Select this field to filter the list by step, allowing you to see which contacts are at each specific stage. By default, the filter is set to All Steps to provide a comprehensive overview.

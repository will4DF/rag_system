The process for manually unsubscribing a person from email and SMS communication.

# Overview

The steps below outline the process for **manually** unsubscribing a person from email and SMS subscriptions.

Here are a few things to note about unsubscribing:

* A person can self-unsubscribe from email by clicking the unsubscribe button in the footer of a message.
* A person can self-unsubscribe from SMS by replying to a message with the word STOP.
* When a person unsubscribes from email or SMS, the '**Unsubscribe**' Milestone is added to their profile. To learn more about Milestones, [click here](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/3419189-milestones-an-overview).

This article will walk you through manually unsubscribing a user from their profile.

## Option 3: Manually Add a Milestone

1. Navigate to **Contacts** > **People** or use the search bar in the top right corner to search the student's name.
2. Click on the student who is requesting unsubscribe to open their profile.

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/897656633/7fbc1357a7c27ec723561985/Unsubsubscribe+name.png?expires=1784333700&signature=83d1ab637928ea78e988185bf5471bbdc95f2d80f2d56a2237c8339b4dc191d0&req=fCkgEMx4m4JcFb4f3HP0gD1Sq%2B%2ByH66GQcGqlOlDT075WeAjb1AfBKhIZH4c%0Ab8U%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/897656633/7fbc1357a7c27ec723561985/Unsubsubscribe+name.png?expires=1784333700&signature=83d1ab637928ea78e988185bf5471bbdc95f2d80f2d56a2237c8339b4dc191d0&req=fCkgEMx4m4JcFb4f3HP0gD1Sq%2B%2ByH66GQcGqlOlDT075WeAjb1AfBKhIZH4c%0Ab8U%3D%0A)
3. In the **contact info** section of the profile, locate the phone number or email address that needs to be unsubscribed and **copy it**.
4. Next, locate the **Milestones card** on the profile. If you have trouble finding it, you can use the search profile feature to find it.
5. Click the **plus** **sign** on the Milestones card to add a new Milestone.
6. Complete the required fields and click **save** when you're finished.

   * To unsubscribe from email, select **Email Unsubscribe Date** as the type.
   * To unsubscribe from SMS, select **SMS Unsubscribe Date** as the type.

*📙 Note: It is best practice to add a label to the person's profile in addition to unsubscribing them from email/SMS.*

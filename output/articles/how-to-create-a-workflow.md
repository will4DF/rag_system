Learn about the different steps necessary to create a Workflow.

# Overview

Creating a workflow is a multi-step process that involves the initial creation, then adding steps and actions, and finally, fine-tuning it by adding specific conditions, delays, and triggers to make it work just the way you need.

In this article, we will break down the creation of a Workflow into nine steps.

1. Create a New Workflow
2. Add the First Step + Action
3. Add a Delay
4. Add Additional Actions
5. Set Condition(s)
6. Add Additional Steps
7. Finish Workflow
8. Activate Workflow
9. Add Trigger/Audience\*

\**It is important to note that adding an audience/trigger may appear as your first step after the initial creation of your Workflow; however, we recommend doing this step last.*

# Step 2: Add the First Step + Action

After completing step one, you are taken to **edit** your new Workflow. **Your first step will automatically be created using your first action.**

1. Click the **+ button** ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/937563380/c6f2bfab71e573baeaf9c5cc/Screenshot+2024-01-18+at+4.28.01%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784430000&signature=0dbc957e5877673b9e32691406111e7263fd422135cafd8b0e14e4dceb8af74b&req=fSMgE899nolfFb4X1HO4gazj%2FaWs2fjj1xKqg8tFSiKpfkdLWr5B3xUrYD%2Bf%0A) to open the **Action** side sheet, allowing you to select an action.
2. Click **Select Action** to pick an action from the list. For a complete list of actions and descriptions, visit our [help article on Actions](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1500292-actions).
3. After selecting the Action, you'll be prompted to select a corresponding value. For example, if you choose **Send Communication** as your action type, you must select which campaign you wish to send.
4. Once your action is configured, click **Save** in the top right corner. After clicking save, you will be redirected back to your Workflow, and you will see your first step with your chosen Action.
5. Replace "Step 1" at the top of the step card with a name that briefly describes the function of this step (this is helpful for identification purposes)

# Step 3: Add a Delay

By default, there is no delay when you add a new step to a workflow. This means that the step will happen immediately unless you specify a delay.

1. Click the **delay button** ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/941819592/546fe6e7681c6f95b0da1011/Screenshot+2024-01-23+at+1.36.58%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784430000&signature=68f01824712ba5eb5c16022b83bf6617c00cec03510e1b11d3a02d4c9d915f6b&req=fSQmHsh3mIhdFb4X1HO4gcHDOrID8Hb0kbFI0d1mF6Q5RjIc367YMm3ZpYpi%0A) on the left side of your step.
2. The **Add Delay** form will open, allowing you to configure a **standard** or **relative** delay. Select the type and enter the time details. You must select parameters using the relative type (e.g., five days **after** **application** **submission**). For more information on the types and parameters of delays, view our [Delays help article](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/10618338-delays).
3. Click **Save**. The delay button should now be yellow, indicating an active delay before this step.

# Step 7: Finish Workflow

If you add conditions to your Workflow, you likely already did this. However, it is best practice to add one final Action to your Workflow, **Finish** **Workflow**. This indicates that it is the final step of the Workflow.

2. Click **Select Action** to select **Finish Workflow** as the Action.
3. Click **Save**.

# Step 9: Add Trigger/Audience

Lastly, it's time to configure how Contacts will enter or enroll in your Workflow. There are two ways a Contact can enter a workflow:

## Search by Name or Load Segment

This option is ideal when your focus is on a specific and unchanging group of Contacts, where automatic enrollment in the Workflow is not required. By selecting this method, you can manually add Contacts by typing their names or loading an existing segment.

It's important to note that this is a one-time action; any Contact added to the segment after it has been enrolled in the Workflow will not be included.

* To search and add Contacts individually, click **Search by Name**.
* To load an existing Segment, click **Load** **Segment**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/941943073/d61140cda32f111667ab5ea4/Screenshot+2024-01-23+at+4.23.48%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784430000&signature=385dc649897e7be1619d1d01c4109c1d51e647a4a1cf73f1a0afd1b63cb831a0&req=fSQmH819nYZcFb4X1HO4gXdeEzWFqFfpCjiMXI%2FJC3DGc9J5nzcy8x0QCkNg%0A)  
​

## Audience: Add a Trigger

This option allows Contacts to be automatically enrolled into a workflow due to a specific action or activity, such as attending an appointment, submitting an application, or filling out a form. The workflow audience dynamically adapts by enrolling Contacts based on their real-time actions.

1. Click **Add** **Trigger**

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/941945152/b33ec77fee2d83a5d290b32b/Screenshot+2024-01-23+at+4.27.20%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=173d31c22a975019cacf5882e72b17abe9b50ecb5d89a9c721da71e78f77121c&req=fSQmH817nIRdFb4f3HP0gDuWMwl2Qhnb6K7JSP0kjXEXstJAwJbGSO4%2Fj0sf%0AFjI%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/941945152/b33ec77fee2d83a5d290b32b/Screenshot+2024-01-23+at+4.27.20%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=173d31c22a975019cacf5882e72b17abe9b50ecb5d89a9c721da71e78f77121c&req=fSQmH817nIRdFb4f3HP0gDuWMwl2Qhnb6K7JSP0kjXEXstJAwJbGSO4%2Fj0sf%0AFjI%3D%0A)
2. Click **Select Trigger** to pick a trigger from the list. For a complete list of triggers and descriptions, visit our [help article on Triggers](https://help.element451.com/en/articles/1500290-triggers).   
   ​

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/944066497/8f81970dc15f8a542e177242/Screenshot+2024-01-25+at+2.09.43%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=e66fc55e5248f8551af97f8a9bde70b834f0ef4061859134a8cb8bc18e39ba61&req=fSQjFs94mYhYFb4f3HP0gE%2F7Dy445vHB6Gh6siC4PJjagEHzAwsOtBE3GxK7%0Ad5c%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/944066497/8f81970dc15f8a542e177242/Screenshot+2024-01-25+at+2.09.43%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=e66fc55e5248f8551af97f8a9bde70b834f0ef4061859134a8cb8bc18e39ba61&req=fSQjFs94mYhYFb4f3HP0gE%2F7Dy445vHB6Gh6siC4PJjagEHzAwsOtBE3GxK7%0Ad5c%3D%0A)
3. After selecting the trigger, you'll be prompted to configure the properties/settings for that specific trigger. For example, if you choose **Form Submitted** as your trigger, you must select which form you want the trigger to use.  
   ​

   [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/944070362/d4de2fe4744ade6196bd769c/Screenshot+2024-01-25+at+2.13.56%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=67ba01c6b501ef7dd6cf6711afcaad26ddc0f2e66ac0ad3c400b650665986741&req=fSQjFs5%2BnoddFb4f3HP0gKVMx9UNES1IXuMdzaZrSp%2B7boZ6z%2BAWg2kmTjm1%0AGzM%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/944070362/d4de2fe4744ade6196bd769c/Screenshot+2024-01-25+at+2.13.56%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1784333700&signature=67ba01c6b501ef7dd6cf6711afcaad26ddc0f2e66ac0ad3c400b650665986741&req=fSQjFs5%2BnoddFb4f3HP0gKVMx9UNES1IXuMdzaZrSp%2B7boZ6z%2BAWg2kmTjm1%0AGzM%3D%0A)
4. Add additional triggers if needed. Note: You can have multiple triggers, and they operate **independently**. This means that a person only needs to meet the criteria of **one** trigger to be enrolled in the Workflow.
5. Click **Save** in the top right corner.

[Learn More: Triggers](https://help.element451.com/workflows/triggers)

# Monitor Your Workflow

Once you've completed step 9, you're finished! You can now edit or monitor your Workflow to see how Contacts move through it (where they are exiting, who has finished, etc.).
